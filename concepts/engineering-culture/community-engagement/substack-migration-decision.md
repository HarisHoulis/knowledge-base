# Hello World

Charity Majors explains her decision to move her blog from WordPress to Substack and then to stay, despite criticism that Substack platforming Nazis. She acknowledges the ethical concerns but argues that Substack is not analogous to far-right platforms like Gab or Parler, but rather a 'light dove grey' on a continuum of governance challenges. Her primary motivation is re-engaging with the technical community: she believes she has been 'going dark' on social media and neglecting a core part of her job, which is to actively participate in and listen to the software engineering discourse, particularly around AI and observability.

- Charity chose to remain on Substack after reconsidering, prioritizing active community engagement and low-friction publishing over platform boycotts.
- She argues that the tech mainstream still exists on X, but she prefers to be active on LinkedIn and Substack instead.
- She sees Substack as ethically acceptable ('light dove grey') and feels more compromised by her YouTube subscription than by Substack.
- To mitigate harm, she does not accept payments, encourages RSS/email use, and plans to auto-post to WordPress.
- She respects dissenters but states that platform protest is not her battle to fight.
