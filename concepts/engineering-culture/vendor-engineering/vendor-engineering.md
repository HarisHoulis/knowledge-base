# Got Opinions on Observability? I Could Use Your Help (Once More, With Feeling)

Charity Majors, while working on the second edition of her book, asks for community input on observability and software buying. She explains that vendor engineering is a high-leverage, underappreciated skill for staff+ engineers, allowing them to influence many engineers and companies through procurement and technical decisions. She describes her previous request for help was successful, and this follow-up targets specific areas where she has less direct experience, such as working with traditional 'three pillars' observability models (metrics, logs, traces) and managing costs at scale (Majors, 2025).

- Vendor engineering is one of the highest-leverage activities for senior engineers, with impact on every engineer and customer.
- Majors is collecting advice on observability migrations, cost control, telemetry pipelines, build vs. buy, OpenTelemetry, and tool consolidation.
- She explicitly requests stories from large enterprises, including how they structure tiers of service and who owns observability without a dedicated team.
- The post also touches on helping engineers reduce cognitive overhead through instrumentation and automatic tooling.
- Contributors' names may be printed, but employers will be kept anonymous.
