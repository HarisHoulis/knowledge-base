# Fragments: July 6

The article also discusses rising token costs, with leaked internal materials from companies like Citi and Amazon showing dramatic increases in AI spending, leading to throttling and even absurd measures like making AI speak like cavemen. Fowler also shares Charity Majors' ethical perspective on AI, advocating for engagement over renunciation, and notes a fraudulent DMCA takedown of Gergely Orosz's article, highlighting vulnerabilities in AI-affected content ecosystems.

- Agentic development has moved from aspiration to production; engineers are shipping real software.
- Architecture and design remain crucial for AI maintainability; good design benefits both humans and agents.
- Token costs are becoming a major concern, causing companies to throttle AI usage and seek cost controls.
- Ethical engagement with AI is preferable to disengagement, as renunciation does little to help those harmed.
- Fraudulent DMCA claims can be used to suppress critical articles, underscoring new risks in the AI era.
