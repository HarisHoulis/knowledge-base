# The Pulse: What can we learn from the Railway hack?

The Pulse newsletter covers events and trends across Big Tech and startups. In this edition, Gergely Orosz highlights cloud platform Railway's publication of an extremely detailed postmortem about a recent hack (Pragmatic Engineer, 2026).

According to the newsletter, the attacker likely used 'abliterated' AI models — models with their safeguards removed — during the intrusion. In response, Railway has hardened its systems. The postmortem is framed as a learning opportunity for other engineering organizations.

- Railway published a detailed postmortem of its recent hack
- The attacker likely used 'abliterated' AI models without safeguards
- Railway hardened its systems following the incident
