# On Friday Deploys: Sometimes that Puppy Needs Murdering

Charity Majors clarifies her stance on Friday deploy freezes, responding to misconceptions from her earlier "murdering puppies" post. She emphasizes that she does not advocate for eliminating deploy freezes universally; rather, they are a pragmatic workaround for teams that lack the observability and confidence to move quickly. The key issue is not the freeze itself but the self-righteous attitude some adopt, when it is simply a practical hack.

- Deploy freezes are sensible when you don't have the tooling to catch problems before customers do.
- If you must freeze deploys, also freeze merges to avoid accumulating unreleased changes.
- Don't actually stop the deployment process; keep running deploys to avoid systemic issues from inactivity.
- Avoid moral posturing about freezes—they are a workaround, not a virtue.
- The author is pragmatic, not extremist, despite the hyperbolic title.
