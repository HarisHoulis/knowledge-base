# Dario Amodei on AI Trust and Marketing

Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, argues that the public's negative view of AI is not primarily caused by AI leaders' warnings about risks, but rather by a deep crisis of trust in companies, governments, and the tech industry. He believes that ordinary people suspect that AI is just another way to exploit them, a suspicion rooted in decades of institutional betrayal, with AI being the latest iteration (source). Amodei rejects the suggestion that a glitzy marketing campaign with a positive spin would win back trust, noting that claims like 'AI will cure cancer' are now clichés that many view as deceptive. Instead, he contends that the only effective way to restore trust is to actually deliver on the big promises to benefit the world. He acknowledges that the most accurate criticism of AI companies, including Anthropic, is their failure to deliver on these promises, and he urges critics to focus on this substantive issue rather than messaging and marketing.

- Negative public opinion about AI stems from a fundamental crisis of trust, not from risk warnings by AI leaders.
- Marketing campaigns with positive spin are ineffective; they are seen as deceptive clichés.
- Restoring trust requires actually delivering on promises to benefit society, such as curing cancer.
- The most valid criticism of AI companies is that they have not yet delivered on their big promises.
- Critics should focus on companies' actual impact rather than their messaging or marketing.
