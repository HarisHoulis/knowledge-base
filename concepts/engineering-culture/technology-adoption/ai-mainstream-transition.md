# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a parallel between the mainstream adoption of AI in 2025 and the shift to cloud computing around 2010. She recounts how, at Linden Lab in 2006, using S3 for an experimental asset store was a marginal hack; by 2010, cloud had become the default foundation and datacenters were legacy. Now, just eight months after an SRECon keynote urging SREs to engage with AI, she observes that AI has similarly moved from experimental satellite tech to core, foundational infrastructure in developer tools. She acknowledges there is a speculative bubble—just as there was with cloud, the internet, and every major innovation—but insists that froth does not invalidate the real value being created. For her, 2025 marks the year AI crossed the threshold into mainstream engineering, making it an exciting time to be a builder.

- AI has transitioned from experimental, peripheral technology to mainstream, foundational infrastructure in developer tools, much like cloud computing did by 2010.
- The rapid shift became visible within only eight months, contrasting with earlier skepticism that treated AI as a hype cycle separate from core engineering.
- Acknowledging the existence of a bubble (hype/froth) does not negate the underlying transformative value of AI, just as with previous tech waves.
- SREs and technical pessimists need to engage with AI from a position of expertise and risk awareness rather than dismiss it outright, to keep their critiques relevant.
