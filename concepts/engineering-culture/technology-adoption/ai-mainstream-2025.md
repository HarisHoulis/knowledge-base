# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a parallel between the adoption of cloud computing around 2010 and the state of AI in 2025. Reflecting on her early days at Linden Lab, where S3 was initially used as an experimental asset store before cloud became the mainstream foundation, she argues that AI has similarly crossed the threshold from satellite/experimental technology to a core part of developer tools. She notes that just eight months earlier she and Fred Hebert were delivering an SRECon keynote urging SREs to engage seriously with AI, but the landscape has shifted dramatically since then. While acknowledging the inevitable hype bubble, she contends that froth does not disprove the underlying value, and expresses excitement about the current era of building.

- AI in 2025 is analogous to cloud in 2010: moving from experimental to foundational.
- The author's own position evolved from cautious engagement to recognizing AI as mainstream.
- Hype bubbles are common with major technological shifts, but they don't negate real value.
- Skeptics should ground their critiques in expertise to remain relevant and credible.
