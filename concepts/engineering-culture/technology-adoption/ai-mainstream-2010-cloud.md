# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a parallel between the adoption of cloud computing around 2010 and the current state of AI in 2025. She recounts her early experience at Linden Lab using S3 as an experimental asset store, which quickly shifted from experimental to mainstream within a few years. Similarly, she argues that AI has moved from being a satellite or experimental technology to becoming foundational, particularly in developer tools, even though it is still accompanied by a hype bubble.

- AI in 2025 is comparable to cloud in 2010: transitioning from experimental to mainstream foundational technology.
- Hype bubbles exist alongside real value; the presence of froth doesn't negate the underlying benefits.
- SREs and pessimists are encouraged to engage with AI critically and constructively, grounding their critique in expertise.
- The shift is especially visible in developer tools, where AI is becoming ubiquitous.
