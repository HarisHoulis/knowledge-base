# Dario Amodei on AI's Crisis of Trust and the Need to Deliver

Dario Amodei argues that the public's negative view of AI is fundamentally a crisis of trust, not primarily caused by AI leaders warning about risks. He believes that ordinary people distrust companies, governments, and the tech industry due to decades of built-up suspicion, and AI is the latest iteration of this pattern. According to the quote, a glitzy marketing campaign with a positive spin would not win back trust; saying AI will cure cancer is now a cliché and widely perceived as deceptive.

- The public's negative view of AI is a crisis of trust, not a messaging problem.
- Distrust in tech companies predates AI and is deeply rooted.
- Marketing spin is ineffective; the way to build trust is by actually delivering beneficial outcomes.
- The most accurate criticism of AI companies is that they have not yet fulfilled their big promises.
- Amodei suggests focusing on substantive delivery rather than messaging and marketing.
