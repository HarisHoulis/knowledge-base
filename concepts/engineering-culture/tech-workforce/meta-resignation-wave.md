# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta’s layoffs and forced reassignments are causing a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly affected. The uncertainty and perceived unfairness are pushing these employees to seek opportunities elsewhere, despite Meta’s attempts to retain them with large equity packages. This predictable outcome highlights the broader cultural damage from poorly managed workforce reductions (Orosz, 2026).

The article also discusses Grok Bot, a generic AI harness built by the Cursor team, which the author describes as the 'OpenClaw moment' for managed AI agents. The author tested it and found it effective for automating daily knowledge-work workflows, predicting that other AI vendors will soon copy the concept (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments are driving unaffected engineers to resign, despite equity retainers.
- The resignation wave illustrates how poorly handled workforce cuts can damage company culture and retention.
- Grok Bot, from the Cursor team, is a generic AI harness for knowledge work, comparable to Codex but for broader tasks.
- The author personally found Grok Bot useful for automating daily workflows and expects widespread imitation.
