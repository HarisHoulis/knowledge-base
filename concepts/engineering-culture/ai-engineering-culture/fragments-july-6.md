# Fragments: July  6

Martin Fowler reflects on the second Future of Software Development Retreat, where agentic development shifted from speculation to shipping. Attendees reported confidence that the value of AI-assisted software engineering is real, with production usage replacing earlier hesitancy. Fowler notes emerging patterns and practical concerns such as harnessing engineering and the rising cost of tokens (Fowler, 2026).

- At the second retreat, agentic development moved from aspirational to production, with attendees shipping real software.
- New concerns include harness engineering and the skyrocketing cost of AI tokens, with companies throttling model usage.
- Architecture and design remain important; good modularity and naming help both humans and AI agents.
- Ethical engagement with AI is preferable to purity; renouncing use does little to help those harmed.
- Fraudulent copyright claims can be used to remove articles from search engines, a growing platform risk.
