# Practitioner Voice: The Writing Category Nobody has Named Yet

Jim Highsmith recognized that effective writing from a practitioner is a distinct style from academic writing or thought-leadership content. This style, which Martin Fowler advocates and his contributors mostly follow, is now named "practitioner voice." Highsmith decided it was important to give it a name and identify what makes it distinctive, offering a clearer way to categorize and understand this form of professional writing.

- Practitioner voice is a writing style distinct from academic writing and thought-leadership content.
- Jim Highsmith named this category to recognize its unique nature.
- Martin Fowler advocates for practitioner voice and his contributors generally follow it.
- The article highlights the importance of identifying what makes practitioner voice distinctive.
