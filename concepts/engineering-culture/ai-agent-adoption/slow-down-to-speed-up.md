# Slow down to speed up: so much has changed in 6 months’ time

In this article based on his Craft Conference keynote, Gergely Orosz argues that the software engineering landscape has shifted dramatically in the six months since late 2025, when more capable AI agents such as Opus 4.5 and GPT-5.4 were released. He uses Meta's most embarrassing outage as a cautionary tale: an account takeover vulnerability via Meta AI was shipped because code was AI-generated and AI-reviewed, while security and integrity teams had been gutted or reassigned. This illustrates the risks of aggressively prioritizing AI at the expense of engineering quality and reliability (Orosz, 2026).

- AI agents have become genuinely useful since November 2025, transforming how software is written and reviewed.
- Meta's account-takeover outage was caused by AI-generated, AI-reviewed code and weakened security teams, a warning about neglecting engineering health.
- Developers are shipping far more code, but fewer changes are reviewed by humans, leading to higher risk and lower quality.
- Companies like Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Uber, startups, and traditional firms are all adopting AI agents, though with different strategies and tools.
- Engineers should focus on 'taste,' selective code review, and understanding AI limitations to future-proof their careers.
