# LIVE: Uncle Bob on Software Fundamentals in the Age of AI

In this interview, Robert C. Martin ("Uncle Bob") talks with Matt Pocock about his long career in software and his evolving perspective on AI. Martin began programming in 1964 at age 12, and he has authored influential books such as Clean Code. He recounts how he was initially unimpressed by early AI models, but later tried using an early Grock agent on an active project. He found it fast but sloppy, requiring constant cleanup and leaving messes behind. The conversation also touches on the origin of his "morning bathrobe rant" and underscores that fundamental software craftsmanship remains relevant even as AI tools change how code is produced.

- Uncle Bob has been programming for over half a century, starting in 1964.
- He is the author of Clean Code, one of the most influential software engineering books.
- He tested an early AI agent (Grock) and found it generated code quickly but often made a mess requiring cleanup.
- He believes AI tools should be used with an understanding of software fundamentals.
- His "morning bathrobe rant" began as an impromptu critique of SQL security issues.
