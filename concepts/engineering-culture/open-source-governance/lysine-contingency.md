# The Lysine Contingency

Jake Wharton announces that Retrofit, OkHttp, Okio, and SQL Delight are joining the Commonhaus Foundation and moving to a new GitHub organization, with a new home at lysine.dev. The move is meant to reinforce the projects' longevity and honor the fact that they have long since outgrown the stewardship of a single company (source: Jake Wharton, "The Lysine Contingency").

- Retrofit, OkHttp, Okio, and SQL Delight are moving to the Commonhaus Foundation and a new GitHub organization.
- The move aims to ensure the projects' longevity and independence from any single company.
- The name 'lysine contingency' is a Jurassic Park reference for a plan to limit damage from the former company after stewardship failed.
- The transfer of assets and infrastructure is currently underway, with the new home at lysine.dev.
- The author thanks contributors and looks forward to continued collaboration at the new organization.
