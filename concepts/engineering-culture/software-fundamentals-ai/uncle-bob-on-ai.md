# LIVE: Uncle Bob on Software Fundamentals in the Age of AI

The interview introduces Uncle Bob (Robert C. Martin), a programmer since 1964 who started at age 12 with a three-bit finite state machine. He is best known for his influential book 'Clean Code,' which remains a frequent reference in software engineering discussions. Martin's early career involved reading Fortran, Cobol, and PL1 books, writing programs on paper and executing them mentally before getting a job at age 18. (Pocock, 2026)

- Uncle Bob began programming at age 12 with a simple 3-bit machine, and has been a professional programmer since age 18.
- He authored 'Clean Code,' a highly influential book in software engineering.
- He was initially unimpressed with AI chatbots, but later experimented with an early Grok agent.
- When using the agent on a real project, it produced messy code that needed constant cleanup.
- The 'bathrobe rant' started as a spontaneous video at 6am about SQL's flaws.
