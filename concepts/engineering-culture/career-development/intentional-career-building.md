# Intentional Career Building

Kent C. Dodds shares practical strategies for intentionally advancing your software career and building a professional reputation. He emphasizes that communicating your accomplishments is critical: even significant work goes unrecognized if others don't understand its scope or know who did it. He advises giving credit where it's due while making sure the value you create is visible. Dodds also describes a "double dip" approach, where you answer co-workers' questions in public formats like GitHub gists or Twitter, solving an internal problem while simultaneously building external content and reach.

- Communicate your accomplishments and give credit where it's due so others understand the value you create.
- Look for 'double dip' opportunities: answer work questions publicly to help both your colleague and a broader audience.
- Own your content and platform by using domains you control, enabling easy redirection when you change services.
- Be consistent in name, avatar, and messaging across platforms, and time your sharing for maximum visibility.
- Solve real problems you actually experience and be patient; building a following takes time and sustained effort.
