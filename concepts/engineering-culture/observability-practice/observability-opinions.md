# Got Opinions on Observability? I Could Use Your Help (Once More, With Feeling)

Charity Majors follows up on an earlier call for advice about buying software, which she says received an overwhelming response. She now asks readers to share opinions on observability, an area where she worries her writing may be weak or biased toward the o11y 1.0/three-pillars model. She frames vendor engineering as one of the most powerful and subtle applications of deep technical expertise, enabling staff+ engineers to develop executive-level skills while remaining individual contributors. The post lists a wide range of unanswered questions covering observability migrations, cost control, telemetry pipelines, team structures, build vs. buy, OpenTelemetry adoption, instrumentation, tool consolidation, and open-source strategies. She requests email responses within a week and says she may credit names but not employers. The post also includes a brief grammar discussion about "opinions on" vs. "opinions about," ending with a lighthearted Buffy reference.

- Vendor engineering is a high-leverage, underappreciated skill for senior engineers.
- Charity is collecting practitioner opinions on observability to strengthen her book's second edition.
- Key topics include migrations, cost control, team ownership, OpenTelemetry, instrumentation, and consolidation.
- Responses are due within one week; names may be credited but employers will not be mentioned.
