# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her migration from WordPress to Substack after a decade of blogging. She cites WordPress's friction and the vibrant longform tech writing ecosystem on Substack as primary motivators. The move involves exporting email subscribers (pending manual review), porting the post corpus, and preserving existing links by temporarily keeping the old site active. She also mentions working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share insights from that process on the new platform.

- Author is moving from WordPress to Substack after 10 years of blogging.
- WordPress is criticized for friction and being undesirable to use.
- Substack is seen as the current hub for longform tech writing.
- Email subscriber export is stuck in manual review; comments won't transfer.
- Old site will stay up to preserve bookmarks and article links.
