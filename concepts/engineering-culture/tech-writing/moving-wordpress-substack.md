# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her decision to move her blog from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade of writing on WordPress. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction and the vibrant longform tech writing ecosystem on Substack as primary motivations. She also mentions working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share insights via Substack once drafts are complete. She notes practical migration issues: email subscriber export is stuck in manual review, comments cannot be transferred, and she will keep the original site up to preserve bookmarks and links.

- Charity Majors is moving her blog from WordPress to Substack after 10 years.
- She despises WordPress and finds the friction discourages posting.
- Substack is seen as the vibrant ecosystem for longform tech writing.
- She is writing the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and will share thoughts on Substack.
- Migration limitations: email subscribers pending review, comments not portable, old site remains for link preservation.
