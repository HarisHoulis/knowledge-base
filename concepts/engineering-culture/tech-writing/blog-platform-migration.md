# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors (2025) announces her decision to move her long-running blog from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade on the platform. She cites WordPress's friction as a major obstacle to posting, and notes that Substack has become the hub for long-form tech writing and community engagement (https://charity.wtf/p/moving-from-wordpress-to-substack).

- After 10 years, Charity Majors is leaving WordPress due to friction and embracing Substack's vibrant tech writing ecosystem.
- She is working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share insights on Substack.
- Email subscriber import is pending manual review; existing comments will not transfer.
- charity.wtf will remain active to avoid breaking bookmarks and article links.
