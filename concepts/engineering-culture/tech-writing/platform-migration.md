# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her move from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade of blogging. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction and the vibrant longform tech writing ecosystem on Substack as primary motivations. Majors reflects on starting her blog in December 2015 after leaving Facebook and before incorporating Honeycomb, and notes that she misses tech Twitter. She has been working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to stream her thoughts via Substack once drafts are turned in. She also handles housekeeping: email subscriber export is held for manual review, comments cannot be transferred, and she will keep charity.wtf live to avoid breaking bookmarks or article links.
