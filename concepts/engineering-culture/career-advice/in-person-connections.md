# AI is taking your job

The article argues that while AI is increasingly used in job applications and hiring, it cannot replace the value of in-person human connections. The author shares an anecdote about an interviewer who caught a candidate using ChatGPT and later found the same person applying under a different name with a different AI-generated resume, illustrating the absurdity of AI-driven applications. This creates a competitive environment where personal relationships stand out even more.

- AI-generated resumes and automated filtering make it harder for real candidates to be noticed, but personal connections cut through the noise.
- The author credits every job he has had since graduating to personal relationships, not online applications.
- In-person interactions strengthen relationships even with people you know online, and often lead to future opportunities.
- Conferences and camps are intentionally structured to maximize hallway conversations, extended breaks, and communal activities, as these are where real value emerges.
