# Are you an experienced software buyer? I could use some help.

Charity Majors, co-author of the second edition of "Observability Engineering," shares that she and her team have been working on the book since April or May 2025, adding a substantial new section called "Observability Governance." This section covers topics such as staffing observability teams, their placement in the org chart, buying and integrating tools, managing costs, and making business cases to executives. Majors notes that observability engineering teams are now ubiquitous, a significant change since the first edition was written (Majors, 2025).

- Observability engineering teams are now commonplace, leading to a new 'Observability Governance' section in the book's second edition.
- Majors openly admits she has never bought software at a large enterprise and lacks firsthand experience in vendor selection, stakeholder management, and driving consensus for large purchases.
- She solicits advice from experienced engineers and VPs on practical questions like how to shop for options, run a good proof of concept, identify when to switch vendors, and manage multi-million-dollar decisions.
- Any advice shared will not be quoted without permission, and no vendor names will be mentioned in the book.
- This is an O'Reilly writing project, not a Honeycomb promotional effort.
