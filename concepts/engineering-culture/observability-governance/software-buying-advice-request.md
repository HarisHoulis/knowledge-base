# Are You an Experienced Software Buyer? Charity Majors Seeks Advice for Book

Charity Majors, along with Liz, Austin, and George, is working on the second edition of "Observability Engineering," which will include a new, substantial section on "Observability Governance." This section will cover staffing observability teams, org placement, buying and integrating tools, cost management, making business cases to executives, and managing schemas and semantic conventions at scale (Charity Majors, 2025).

- The second edition of "Observability Engineering" will include a new section on Observability Governance.
- Majors openly admits she has never bought software at a large enterprise scale and is seeking advice from experienced software buyers.
- She solicits email advice with the subject line "Buying Software," asking for context about company size and the reader's role in purchasing.
- The request is for an O'Reilly writing project, not a Honeycomb initiative, and vendor names will not be mentioned.
- Contributors' quotes will only be used with permission and a signed release.
