# Do you even need a presentation?

Martin Fowler shares that Sumeet Gayathri Moghe is also frustrated by poor presentations and bad slide decks, and has begun writing a series of posts on avoiding these issues. The first post in the series questions whether a presentation is needed at all, suggesting that presenters should reconsider the necessity of a slide deck before creating one. Fowler highlights this as a starting point for improving technical communication by focusing on the fundamental choice of medium.

- Poor presentations with bad slide decks are a common problem.
- Before creating slides, consider whether a presentation is even necessary.
- Alternatives to a formal presentation may be more effective for certain content.
