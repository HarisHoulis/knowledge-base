# A Few Predicted Talks From QConAI 2030

In this QConAI presentation summary, Meryem Arik outlines her predictions for software engineering in 2030. She argues that the rise of AI agents will make token spend management and parallel agent infrastructure central concerns for engineering organizations, while non-technical builders will increasingly create software directly, reshaping the traditional development pipeline. Arik also anticipates agent-driven vendor decisions, meaning AI systems will play a larger role in selecting and evaluating technology vendors (Arik, 2026).

Arik highlights upcoming regulatory hurdles that will affect how agents are deployed and governed. She suggests that software engineers must pivot from pure coding skills toward product leadership and multi-agent coordination, as routine coding becomes automated. The article frames engineering in 2030 as a discipline focused on orchestrating AI agents, managing costs, and navigating compliance, rather than writing code line by line (Arik, 2026, InfoQ).

- Token spend management will become critical as agentic workflows scale.
- Parallel agent infrastructure will be key to supporting concurrent AI operations.
- Non-technical builders will contribute more directly to software creation.
- Software engineers will shift toward product leadership and multi-agent coordination.
- Regulatory hurdles will shape how AI agents are adopted and governed.
