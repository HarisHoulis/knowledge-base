# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors, co-founder of Honeycomb, announces her move from her self-hosted WordPress blog to Substack after a decade of writing. She cites WordPress's friction and the vibrant ecosystem of longform tech writers on Substack as key motivations. Although she tried to export email subscribers, the process is pending manual review, and historical comments cannot be migrated. She plans to keep the original site up to avoid breaking bookmarks and links while porting her post corpus over. The move is also timed with the completion of the second edition of "Observability Engineering," as she looks forward to publishing her newly gained insights on Substack.

- Charity Majors is migrating from WordPress to Substack after 10 years of blogging.
- Reasons include frustration with WordPress's friction and a stronger tech-writing community on Substack.
- Email subscriber import is stuck in manual review; comments from the old site will be lost.
- The original site will remain accessible to preserve links, while posts are ported.
- She plans to share lessons from writing the second edition of "Observability Engineering" on Substack.
