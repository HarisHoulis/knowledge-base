# Simon Willison on Technical Blogging

Simon Willison shares advice on technical blogging, emphasizing that the number one tip is to lower your standards. He recommends aiming to hit publish while still actively unhappy with what you've written, as the alternative is a folder full of drafts and never publishing anything. Willison argues that the flaws you perceive in your writing are invisible to everyone else, and that no one will know how perfect your intended post would have been. The key is to prioritize publishing over perfection.

- Lower your standards to consistently publish.
- Publish while still unhappy with the piece to avoid endless drafts.
- The flaws you see in your writing are invisible to readers.
- Perfectionism leads to a folder of drafts and no published output.
