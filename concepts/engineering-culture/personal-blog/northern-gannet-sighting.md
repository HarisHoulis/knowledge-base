# Northern Gannet

Simon Willison shares a photo and brief note about Morris, a Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus) that has become a local celebrity in Pillar Point Harbor, California. Morris is the only known Northern Gannet in the entire Pacific Ocean, having originally arrived in the Farallon Islands off San Francisco 14 years ago (as linked in the post to a Bay Nature article). Since then, Morris has made Pillar Point harbor his home, where he is easily spotted due to being the only white bird with a yellow head, typically hanging out with smaller black Brandt's cormorants near the harbor sign visible from the end of the commercial pier. The post highlights Morris's uniqueness and the joy of local wildlife observation.

- Morris is the only known Northern Gannet in the Pacific Ocean.
- Morris has been in California for 14 years, first appearing in the Farallon Islands.
- Morris is now resident at Pillar Point Harbor, often seen with Brandt's cormorants.
- He is easily identified as the only white bird with a yellow head in the harbor.
