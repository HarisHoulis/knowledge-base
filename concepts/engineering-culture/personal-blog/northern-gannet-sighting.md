# Northern Gannet

The article reports a sighting of Morris, a Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus), in Pillar Point Harbor, California. Morris is notable as the only known Northern Gannet in the entire Pacific Ocean, making it a local celebrity. The sighting is documented with a photograph and details on how to identify Morris among other seabirds.

Morris first appeared in the Farallon Islands off the coast of San Francisco 14 years ago, as linked in a Bay Nature article. Since then, Morris has made Pillar Point Harbor its home, where it is easily spotted from the end of the commercial pier. The bird is described as the only white bird with a yellow head, often seen hanging out with smaller black Brandt's cormorants near the harbor sign. The Bay Nature article suggests that Morris's arrival may be linked to melting Arctic ice, although this is not confirmed in the current sighting post.

- Morris is the only known Northern Gannet in the Pacific Ocean.
- Morris has been in California for 14 years, first appearing in the Farallon Islands.
- Morris can be easily identified by its yellow head and white body, often near Brandt's cormorants in Pillar Point Harbor.
- The arrival of Morris may be connected to melting Arctic ice, according to a referenced article.
