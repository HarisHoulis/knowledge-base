# Why is Meta destroying its engineering organization?

Gergely Orosz examines Meta's dramatic shift away from its historically strong engineering culture. For two decades, Meta empowered engineers with autonomy, a 'move fast' mentality, and a focus on individual impact, making it one of Silicon Valley's most prestigious workplaces. This culture, detailed in Facebook's 'little red book' and later characterized by 'move fast with stable infra,' was a key driver of the company's success (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's engineering culture, once a profit center, is being treated as a cost center under AI-driven reorganization.
- Engineers face forced reassignment: 30-50% of core team members, especially in infra and security, are moved to ADO for data labeling and RLHF.
- Keystroke and mouse tracking for AI training data was implemented with no opt-out, raising privacy concerns and employee pushback.
- The Scale AI partnership and Alexandr Wang's leadership prioritize training data workflows, but the execution reduces skilled engineers to repetitive tasks.
- The changes risk long-term damage to Meta's engineering capability, even as AI investment accelerates.
