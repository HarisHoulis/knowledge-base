# Why is Meta destroying its engineering organization?

Meta historically had one of the most engineering-centric cultures in Big Tech, evolving from a 'move fast and break things' ethos to 'move fast with stable infra.' Engineers were empowered, given autonomy through bootcamp team selection, and their work was treated as a profit center. However, in 2026, leadership shifted drastically, prioritizing AI investments and forcing engineers into data labeling and RLHF roles (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's engineering culture was once highly valued, with engineer autonomy, minimal process, and a focus on individual impact.
- Leadership forced 30-50% of engineers on core and infrastructure teams into the Agent Data Optimisation (ADO) org for data labeling and AI training tasks.
- Meta implemented keystroke and mouse-click tracking to generate AI training data, initially without opt-out, later adding limited pause and exemption controls after employee pushback.
- The changes signal a shift from engineering as a profit center to a cost center, causing widespread demoralization and internal disruption.
