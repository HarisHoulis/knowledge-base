# Got opinions on observability? I could use your help (once more, with feeling)

Charity Majors asks the engineering community for input on observability practices to inform the second edition of her book. She emphasizes that buying software and managing vendor relationships is a high-leverage skill for staff+ engineers, allowing them to develop executive-level communication and strategy while remaining individual contributors (Majors, 2025).

- Vendor engineering is a subtle, powerful application of deep technical expertise.
- Majors requests community answers on observability migrations, cost control, OpenTelemetry, instrumentation, and team structures.
- She offers attribution by name only, not employer, and has a one-week deadline.
- The article also includes a lighthearted note on the grammar of 'opinions about' vs 'opinions on'.
