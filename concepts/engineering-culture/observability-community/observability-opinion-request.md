# Got Opinions on Observability? I Could Use Your Help (Once More, with Feeling)

Charity Majors, in a follow-up post, asks the observability community for input on a range of practical topics to inform the second edition of her book. She emphasizes that vendor engineering—the art of buying and managing software—is a high-leverage skill for senior engineers, enabling them to affect many engineers and customers. She shares that the previous request for advice on buying software yielded a strong response, validating the value of community expertise.

- Vendor engineering is a subtle, powerful application of deep technical expertise and one of the only ways staff+ engineers can develop executive-level skills while remaining individual contributors.
- Majors is soliciting community opinions on observability migration, cost control, telemetry pipelines, build vs. buy, OpenTelemetry adoption, instrumentation, and tool consolidation.
- She notes her own selection bias: she knows few people who use the 'three pillars' model happily, so she explicitly asks for perspectives from that world.
- Responses will be used for her book, with contributors credited by name but not by employer; deadline is one week from the post date.
