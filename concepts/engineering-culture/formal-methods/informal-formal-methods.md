# Formal Methods Pay Off

Kent Beck reflects on his long relationship with formal methods, recalling that he TA'd an undergraduate formal methods course for two quarters in graduate school. Although he has rarely applied full-blown formal methods professionally, he notes that he uses them informally all the time.

He describes being reintroduced to formal methods by Fortune Buchholtz about a year before writing, but trying them again led him to get lost in the proofs and eventually give up. The post is a short personal reflection rather than a detailed technical argument, and the excerpt ends mid-sentence.
