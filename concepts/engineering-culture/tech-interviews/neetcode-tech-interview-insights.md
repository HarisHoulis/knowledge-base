# Tech interviews with NeetCode

In this episode of The Pragmatic Engineer, Gergely Orosz interviews Navdeep Singh (NeetCode), creator of NeetCode.io and a former Amazon and Google engineer. They discuss why leetcode-style interviews persist—not because they predict job performance, but because they scale across large organizations that must train many interviewers (source: https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/tech-interviews-with-neetcode). Singh also critiques the CAP theorem's two-out-of-three framing, citing Martin Kleppmann's critique as validation for questioning widely taught theories.

Singh shares lessons from his career, including how Amazon's intense culture made him reluctant to ask questions, a habit his Google manager interpreted as independence, leading to a rapid promotion from L3 to L4. He also reveals that Google has restarted onsite coding interviews because AI-powered cheating tools make data structure and algorithm interviews easy to pass remotely. On AI more broadly, Singh finds it most useful as a tech debt and refactoring assistant, and he believes 'effort' is becoming the key differentiator as AI makes other aspects of engineering cheaper. He does not foresee mass layoffs of engineers, and argues humans remain better at weighing tradeoffs than LLMs.

When hiring for NeetCode, Singh prioritizes personality traits and motivation over coding skill, citing a high-agency undergrad with little experience as his best recent hire. The conversation also touches on systems thinking, domain expertise, and the value of going deep on difficult problems as lasting skills in the age of AI (source: same article).

- LeetCode-style interviews persist because they scale at big tech companies, not because they predict job performance.
- Google has restarted onsite coding interviews to combat AI-powered cheating tools in remote DSA interviews.
- Navi's rapid L3-to-L4 promotion at Google was partly due to his independent working style, shaped by Amazon's intense culture.
- AI is most valuable to NeetCode as a tech debt and refactoring assistant, not as a replacement for engineers.
- For hiring, NeetCode values high agency, personality, and motivation more than raw coding experience.
