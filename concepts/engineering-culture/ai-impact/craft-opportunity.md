# The craft "dying" is your OPPORTUNITY

Kent C. Dodds argues that while concerns about AI making developers lazy and producing slop are valid, they miss the larger historical pattern. He notes that AI is not deterministic like a compiler, but neither are humans, and the tendency to abdicate judgment in favor of others (e.g., adopting libraries from big companies) predates AI. Thus, AI hasn't fundamentally changed human nature, but it is shifting the role of the engineer.

- AI is non-deterministic, but so are humans, so blaming AI for bugs ignores long-standing abdication of judgment.
- As agents become more autonomous, engineers must focus on building confidence in software systems to enable that autonomy.
- The opportunity lies in spending less time on trivial details and more on critical system design and building 'software factory' primitives.
- Ownership, accountability, and responsibility are the unique human contributions in an AI-augmented workflow.
- AI enables solo developers to manage large codebases (e.g., a half-million-line project), shifting focus to product-level decisions.
