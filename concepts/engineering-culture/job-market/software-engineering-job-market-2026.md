# State of the software engineering job market in 2026, part 2

This article, part 2 of a series on the 2026 tech job market, analyzes data from partners like Interviewing.io, Workforce.ai, SignalFire, and TrueUp. A key finding is that top AI labs, especially Anthropic and OpenAI, have become more attractive to job candidates than Big Tech, with Anthropic receiving the majority of interview coaching requests. The article also reports that new graduate hiring has become significantly harder: intern intakes have fallen even as overall software engineering recruitment recovers, and candidates from elite universities account for a growing share of new grad hires. The role landscape is shifting, with demand for frontend and native mobile engineers declining while AI and Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) roles surge. AI engineering compensation now exceeds traditional software engineering compensation, and management layers continue to flatten, with fewer engineering managers per engineer. Big Tech employees are staying longer in their roles, and the article concludes with insights on where engineers go after leaving Big Tech, noting differences among Amazon, Google, Apple, Meta, and Microsoft.

- Anthropic and OpenAI are the most sought-after employers, attracting over half of interview coaching requests, surpassing Google and other Big Tech companies.
- New grad hiring has dropped sharply: only 1 in 10 engineering hires at large US tech companies were recent grads in 2025, down from nearly 3 in 10 in 2023.
- Intern intakes have not bounced back with recovery, and elite university graduates are increasingly favored, while mobile and frontend engineer roles are in decline.
- AI engineers are paid more than software engineers, and at the 80th percentile in the US, $300K+ base salaries are becoming common for senior roles.
- Engineering management is flattening, with fewer managers per engineer and fewer VP/Director roles, while Big Tech employee tenure continues to rise.
- Interview preparation signups spike at companies doing mass layoffs, and Amazon engineers leave to a wide variety of destinations, while Google/Apple/Meta engineers mostly go to AI labs.
