# Social Media Engagement: summer 2026

Martin Fowler analyzes the engagement and referral traffic generated by his social media announcements over a roughly three-month period in 2026 (June 16 to September 6, 19 posts total). Using strip charts and box plots, he compares LinkedIn, X, Fediverse (Mastodon), and Bluesky across retweets, likes, and replies. He finds a clear ranking: LinkedIn leads in all engagement metrics, X is second, and Bluesky and Mastodon are far behind, with Mastodon still visibly above Bluesky (Martin Fowler, 2026).

Looking at website analytics for the 90 days prior to September 7, 2026, LinkedIn again proves to be the larger traffic source compared to X and the open platforms, but even LinkedIn is a minor contributor relative to Google, which accounts for roughly 300,000 of the 1.7 million total visits. Compared to his early 2025 analysis, LinkedIn has grown slightly, X has dropped notably, and Bluesky has dropped dramatically.

Fowler explains his continued posting to low-engagement open platforms because he prefers open platforms over walled gardens. He also describes his social media habits: he mostly posts announcement links, has shifted short thoughts to his fragments posts, reads replies, follows fixed lists in reverse-chronological order, avoids algorithmic feeds, and prefers RSS. He notes LinkedIn is the biggest engagement draw but also the platform he dislikes most because it forces algorithmic feeds, while X can be tamed with lists.

- LinkedIn receives the highest engagement (retweets, likes, replies) for Fowler's announcement posts, followed by X, then Mastodon, then Bluesky.
- LinkedIn also drives more referral traffic to martinfowler.com than X, but all social media are dwarfed by Google in overall traffic.
- Compared to early 2025, LinkedIn engagement grew slightly, while X and Bluesky dropped notably.
- Fowler continues posting to Mastodon and Bluesky despite weak engagement because he prefers open platforms over walled gardens.
- Fowler actively avoids algorithmic feeds, uses lists on X, and prefers RSS and fragments for sharing thoughts.
