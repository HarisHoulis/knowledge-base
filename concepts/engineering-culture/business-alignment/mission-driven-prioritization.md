# Business and Engineering Alignment

The article argues that engineers should view themselves as employees hired to further the company's mission, not just to write code. Therefore, convincing business stakeholders to support engineering investments (e.g., tech debt, migrations) requires framing those investments as mission-critical with an appropriate benefit-to-effort ratio over a relevant time horizon.

- Engineers should align their proposals with the company's mission, not just technical preferences.
- Before requesting investment, analyze its benefit-to-effort ratio compared to feature work, including long-term impact.
- Communicate the problem first, then the solution, ensuring stakeholders understand the cost of inaction.
- Trust product managers to prioritize, but engage in good-faith discussions if priorities seem misaligned.
- If company goals are dysfunctional or misaligned with personal career goals, consider leaving.
