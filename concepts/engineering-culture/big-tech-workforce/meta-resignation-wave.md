# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta's recent layoffs and forced reassignments have inadvertently triggered a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly affected by these measures. Despite Meta offering large equity retainers to keep these employees, the strategy does not appear to be effective, as many engineers are choosing to leave anyway. This outcome was predictable given the uncertainty and morale damage caused by the restructuring.

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments pushed unaffected engineers to seek new jobs.
- Large equity retainers offered by Meta are failing to stem the resignation wave.
