# Meta wanted to reduce teams by 60% because of AI

The article reports on a Reuters investigation revealing that Meta's leadership decided to reduce team sizes by 60% as part of plans hatched in January, driven by AI advancements. This move would constitute the social media giant's largest-ever layoffs. The report underscores the growing influence of AI on organizational restructuring within Big Tech.

- Meta leadership planned to slash team sizes by 60%.
- The plans were formulated in January.
- The layoffs would be the largest in Meta's history.
- AI was cited as a primary reason for the reduction.
