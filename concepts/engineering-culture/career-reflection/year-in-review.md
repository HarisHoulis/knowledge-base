# 2025 in Review

Kent C. Dodds reflects on 2025, a year of major professional pivot from EpicReact/EpicWeb toward EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). He describes the shift as moving from "add a chatbot to your app" to "add your app to the chatbot," driven by the vision of a Jarvis-like assistant that coordinates across services. This pivot was inspired by conversations with Ryan Florence and Evan Bacon's React Conf demo, leading to the launch of EpicAI.pro in March and the creation of MCP workshops and self-paced content.

- Pivoted focus from EpicReact/EpicWeb to EpicAI and MCP, with the goal of enabling a 'Jarvis' interface that can take actions across apps.
- Launched EpicAI.pro in March 2025, including workshops and the 'Epic MCP: From Scratch to Production' self-paced course.
- Epic Stack saw meaningful updates: React Router v7, passkeys, Tailwind v4, Tigris image storage, and react-router-auto-routes.
- Epic Web Conf 2025 ran but lost money; Epic Web Camp 2025 was cancelled due to focus and financial concerns.
- Personal highlight: welcoming a sixth child, which will shape commitments in early 2026.
