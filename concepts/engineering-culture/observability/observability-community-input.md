# Got opinions on observability? I could use your help (once more, with feeling)

Charity Majors follows up on an earlier request for advice about buying software, thanking respondents and sharing that their input will shape the second edition of her book. She argues that vendor engineering is one of the subtlest and most powerful applications of engineering expertise, giving staff+ engineers a chance to develop executive-level communication, strategy, and execution skills while remaining individual contributors (Majors, 2025).

- Vendor engineering is high-leverage work that can affect every engineer and customer, and is a rare IC path to executive skills.
- Majors requests community opinions on observability migrations, cost control, telemetry pipelines, team structures, build vs. buy, OpenTelemetry, instrumentation, and tool consolidation.
- She asks for email submissions within one week, with optional name attribution but not employer, to use in the second edition of her book.
- The post highlights open source observability tools as one piece of a potential comprehensive tooling strategy, and asks how practitioners guide engineers on where to send data.
