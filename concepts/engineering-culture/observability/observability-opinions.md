# Got opinions on observability? I could use your help (once more, with feeling)

Charity Majors, CTO of Honeycomb, requests community input on observability practices, building on a previous plea for software-buying advice. She argues that vendor engineering is a subtle but high-leverage application of deep technical expertise, enabling staff+ engineers to develop executive-level skills while remaining individual contributors (Majors, 2025). She emphasizes that choosing and managing observability tools affects every engineer and customer, making it one of the most impactful decisions an engineer can make.

- Vendor engineering is a powerful practical application of expertise, allowing engineers to leverage the work of many engineers for low cost.
- Majors poses detailed questions about migrating observability vendors, cost control, telemetry pipelines, build vs buy, OpenTelemetry adoption, instrumentation, and tool consolidation.
- She asks how observability teams are structured, noting that many orgs lack dedicated teams and may rely on platform or SRE groups.
- Majors requests email responses and will print names but not employers, encouraging candid input before a one-week deadline.
- The article includes a humorous grammar note about 'opinions about' vs 'opinions on,' highlighting the difference between broad and specific subject matter.
