# Got opinions on observability? I could use your help (once more, with feeling)

Charity Majors asks for community input on observability-related topics to inform the second edition of her book. She emphasizes that buying and managing software vendors is a high-leverage engineering skill, requiring technical depth, breadth, and systems thinking, and can help staff+ engineers develop executive-level skills (Majors, 2025). She lists specific questions about migrating between observability vendors, controlling costs, using OpenTelemetry, and structuring observability teams, acknowledging her own selection bias toward the "three pillars" model.

- Vendor engineering is a subtle, powerful form of engineering that can leverage hundreds or thousands of engineers' labor for fractions of pennies.
- The author is seeking real-world experiences on observability vendor migration, cost management, and team structures.
- She asks about build vs. buy, OpenTelemetry adoption decisions, and how to reduce cognitive overhead for engineers through instrumentation.
- She also inquires about tool consolidation vs. proliferation and open-source observability strategies.
