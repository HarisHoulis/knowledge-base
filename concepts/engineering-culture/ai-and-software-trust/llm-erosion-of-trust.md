# LLMs Erode Trust in Documentation and Software

Kyle Kingsbury, quoted by Simon Willison, argues that LLM use broadly erodes trust in documentation, in software, and in the organizations that produce them. Because the signals people relied on as markers of a mature system are now easier to counterfeit, engineers may start treating any new software project with suspicion.

Kingsbury frames the resulting challenge as two questions for maintainers: what can they do to ensure correct software and accurate documentation in the age of LLMs, and, having done that work, how can they demonstrate its quality to users?

- LLM use erodes trust in documentation, software, and the organizations behind them
- Signals previously used to identify mature systems are now easier to counterfeit
- Engineers may grow suspicious of any new software project
- Maintainers face a dual challenge: ensuring correctness and proving quality to users
