# Hello World

Charity Majors announces her decision to remain on Substack after receiving backlash for initially planning to leave. She explains that she paused her migration to Substack after reading the LeaveSubstack site and consulting with experts like Sean and Liz Fong-Jones, ultimately deciding that leaving would hinder her primary goal of re-engaging with the tech community. She argues that Substack is not an extremist platform, describing it as "a light dove grey" on the platform ethics spectrum, and that she feels more ethically compromised by her YouTube subscription than by using Substack (Charity Majors, "Hello World", https://charity.wtf/p/hello-world-7b0).

- Charity Majors chose to stay on Substack after reconsidering her initial migration away, citing her core responsibility to actively participate in technical discussions.
- She views Substack as having lower friction for posting compared to Wordpress, which helps her write more frequently and engage with the community.
- She differentiates Substack from far-right platforms like Gab and Parler, seeing it as a more moderate platform, though still imperfect.
- To mitigate support for Substack, she does not accept paid subscriptions and encourages readers to use RSS or email rather than the app.
