# Northern Gannet

The article reports a sighting of a Northern Gannet (*Morus bassanus*) named Morris in Pillar Point Harbor, California. Morris is described as the only known Northern Gannet in the entire Pacific Ocean, making it a local celebrity. The bird initially appeared at the Farallon Islands off San Francisco 14 years ago and has since established itself near the harbor sign visible from the commercial pier (https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/15/sighting-391300422/).

Morris is easily identifiable by its appearance: a white bird with a yellow head. It is often seen associating with smaller black Brandt's cormorants, which helps birdwatchers locate it.
