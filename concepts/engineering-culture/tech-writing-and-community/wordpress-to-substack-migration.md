# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors, co-founder of Honeycomb and author of "Observability Engineering," announces her decision to move her long-running blog from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade. She reflects on her first post in this space on December 27, 2015, shortly after leaving Facebook and before incorporating Honeycomb. The move is driven by her dislike of WordPress's friction and the recognition that Substack has become the home for vibrant longform tech writing, which she also connects to her nostalgia for "tech twitter" (Majors, 2025).

She has been working on the second edition of "Observability Engineering" and plans to share insights from that process on Substack, saying her thoughts are "burning a hole in my brain." Her housekeeping notes include that email subscriber export is stuck in manual review, comments from the old blog cannot be transferred, and she will keep charity.wtf active to preserve existing bookmarks and links. She invites readers to subscribe at charitydotwtf.substack.com, expressing gratitude for the community she's found over the past ten years despite her frustrations with WordPress.

- After a decade on WordPress, Charity Majors is migrating to Substack because WordPress is frustrating to use and Substack hosts a vibrant tech-writing community.
- She will leave charity.wtf up to avoid breaking links, even while porting her post corpus.
- Email subscriber import is held up for manual review, and old comments cannot be exported to Substack.
- She is writing the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share new insights on Substack.
