# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a parallel between the rise of cloud computing and the current state of AI, arguing that 2025 marked AI's transition from experimental, satellite technology to mainstream foundational infrastructure, particularly in developer tools. She recounts her experience at Linden Lab when EC2 and S3 emerged, noting how cloud started as an experimental project to solve specific problems and quickly became the center of gravity, relegating datacenters to legacy. She suggests the same shift is happening now with AI, despite the hype and bubble concerns. Majors emphasizes that the existence of a bubble doesn't disprove the underlying value, referencing her and Fred Hebert's SRECon keynote where they urged SREs to engage with AI constructively rather than dismiss it. She notes that even eight months prior, AI felt separable from mainstream tech, but now it's becoming foundational.

- AI in 2025 is transitioning from experimental to foundational technology, akin to cloud in 2010.
- The hype bubble around AI does not negate its real and lasting value.
- SREs and technical pessimists should engage with AI to provide grounded, accurate critique.
- For developer tools, AI is now mainstream rather than peripheral.
