# The Pulse: Meta wanted to reduce teams by 60% because of AI

The article reports that Reuters revealed Meta's leadership decided to reduce team sizes by 60%, hatching plans in January to execute the social media giant's largest-ever layoffs. AI is cited as a driving factor behind this drastic organizational shakeup. [1] The report is part of The Pulse series, which covers events and trends within Big Tech and startups, highlighting how AI is beginning to reshape engineering team structures and workforce planning. The specific details of the plan remain as reported by Reuters, indicating a major shift in Meta's approach to team composition and efficiency. [1]
