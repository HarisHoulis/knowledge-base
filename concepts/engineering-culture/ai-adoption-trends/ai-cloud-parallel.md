# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a direct parallel between the state of AI in 2025 and cloud computing in 2010. She recalls how EC2 and S3 started as experimental solutions at Linden Lab in 2006, but by 2010 the cloud had become mainstream infrastructure while datacenters were relegated to legacy status. Majors argues that AI has similarly crossed a threshold in 2025, becoming a foundational technology rather than an experimental side project, at least within developer tools (Majors, 2025).

Just eight months ago, Majors and Fred Hebert delivered an SREcon keynote titled 'AIOps: Prove It!' urging SREs to engage with AI seriously, despite their natural skepticism. She notes that her own position has shifted dramatically since then. AI no longer feels like a satellite of the tech mainstream; it has become the center of gravity. Even acknowledging the presence of a bubble, Majors contends that hype waves accompany every major innovation—cloud and the internet included—and the existence of froth does not invalidate the underlying value. She concludes that this is an exciting time to be a builder, as AI moves into the core of engineering practice (Majors, 2025).

- AI in 2025 mirrors cloud in 2010: shifting from experimental to foundational technology.
- Majors' own position evolved from cautious engagement to seeing AI as mainstream, especially in developer tools.
- Bubbles are inevitable with major innovations, but they don't negate real value.
- The shift is particularly evident in developer tools, where AI has become integral.
- It's an exciting time for builders as AI embeds into mainstream engineering.
