# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

The article reports on two major stories. First, Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments have triggered a resignation wave among engineers not directly impacted, as they foresee instability and seek opportunities elsewhere. Meta is offering large equity retainers to retain these employees, but the approach appears ineffective, according to Orosz.

Second, the Cursor team has built and released Grok Bot, a generic AI harness that extends the Codex experience to general knowledge work. Orosz describes trying it out and automating many daily workflows, expressing enthusiasm and predicting that more AI vendors will copy this harness.

The Pulse positions these as notable trends in Big Tech and startups, highlighting challenges in talent retention and the rapid evolution of AI agents for knowledge work.

- Meta's layoffs and reassignments are pushing unaffected engineers to quit, despite equity retainers.
- The retention offers don't seem to be working, signaling deeper trust issues.
- Grok Bot is a generic AI harness for knowledge work, similar to Codex but broader.
- Orosz is a fan and expects the harness to be widely copied by AI vendors.
