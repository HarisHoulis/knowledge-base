# Thoughts on Motivation and My 40-Year Career

Charity Majors reflects on her unconventional path from a fundamentalist homeschooled childhood in Idaho to a career in tech, emphasizing how work became a source of liberation and meaning. She describes leaving home at 15, fabricating her transcript, and working multiple jobs to put herself through school, eventually finding computers and the internet as a practical escape from poverty (Majors, 2025). Her early career was driven by a desire for autonomy and success, but it wasn't until she resolved her internal conflict with her religious upbringing that her career took off (Majors, 2025).

- Work can be a vehicle for personal growth and liberation, not just a paycheck.
- Creation is harder than critique; building a company forces you to reconcile ideals with reality.
- Motivation often comes from a desire to do work that feels worth doing, not just happiness.
- Meaningful work involves learning from people you disagree with and finding common purpose.
