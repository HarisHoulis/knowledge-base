# Thoughts on Motivation and My 40-Year Career

A key theme is that 'creation is harder than critique,' a quote she attributes to Jeff Gray. Majors describes how starting Honeycomb forced her to confront the ethical dimensions of business decisions and the conflict between maintaining ideals and winning in the marketplace. She argues that reality resists easy answers, and that the process of trying to win while honoring your values is what makes work feel worth doing, even if it took her several years to find happiness in her role as founder and CEO (Majors, 2025).

- Majors' career was shaped by a drive to escape poverty and a fundamentalist upbringing, leading her to reinvent herself through tech.
- She describes her motivation as distinct from 'advancing the industry'; it is about integrity, meaningful life, and building enduring value.
- She values work as a source of liberation and growth, despite acknowledging that many people have valid reasons to view work as exploitation.
- Starting a company taught her that creation is harder than critique, and that balancing ideals with business success is the real challenge.
- She found joy in solving systems problems, including organizational ones, and eventually found happiness in her role as founder after several years.
