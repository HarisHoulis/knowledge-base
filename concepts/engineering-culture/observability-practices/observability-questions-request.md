# Got opinions on observability? I could use your help (once more, with feeling)

Charity Majors requests community input on observability practices, vendor relationships, and tooling trade-offs. She emphasizes that buying and managing software vendors is a high-leverage engineering skill, requiring technical breadth and strategic thinking. She asks readers to share experiences on migrating observability vendors, cost control, telemetry pipelines, build vs. buy decisions, OpenTelemetry adoption, and tool consolidation. The post highlights her work on a second book edition and her desire to broaden perspectives beyond the "three pillars" model.

- Vendor engineering is a subtle and powerful application of engineering expertise, with high leverage and impact on every engineer and customer.
- The author seeks practical answers on observability migrations, cost control, team structures, and telemetry pipeline usage.
- She explicitly invites feedback on topics she may be uninformed about, including positive experiences with the three pillars model.
- The post includes a call for email responses with attribution options and a tight one-week deadline.
