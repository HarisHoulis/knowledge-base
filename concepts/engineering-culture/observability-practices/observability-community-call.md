# Got Opinions on Observability? Charity Majors Seeks Community Input

Charity Majors, writing on her blog, issues a follow-up call for expert opinions and advice on observability, particularly from practitioners who use the 'three pillars' model (metrics, logs, traces) and are happy with it. She notes that her previous request for software-buying advice yielded rich responses, and she is incorporating these insights into the second edition of her book. She emphasizes that vendor engineering is a subtle, high-leverage form of engineering work that allows staff+ engineers to develop executive-level skills while remaining individual contributors.

- Vendor engineering is a powerful application of deep technical expertise and one of the highest-leverage activities an engineer can undertake.
- Majors seeks help on specific observability topics: vendor migrations, cost control, telemetry pipelines, build vs buy, OpenTelemetry adoption, and instrumentation.
- She acknowledges selection bias in her network and explicitly asks for perspectives from organizations happily using traditional three-pillars architectures.
- Feedback and contributions will be credited by name but not by employer, and must be submitted within a week of the post.
- The post also includes a humorous grammar digression about 'opinions on' vs 'opinions about'.
