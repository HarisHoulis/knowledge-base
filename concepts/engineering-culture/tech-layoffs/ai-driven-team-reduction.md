# The Pulse: Meta wanted to reduce teams by 60% because of AI

The Pulse covers a Reuters report on Meta's plans to reduce team sizes by 60%, allegedly due to AI. According to the report, Meta's leadership hatched plans in January to execute the social media giant's largest-ever layoffs by slashing team sizes dramatically. The article raises the question of whether this decision is truly AI-driven, suggesting deeper context behind the move.

- Meta leadership reportedly planned to reduce team sizes by 60%.
- Plans were made in January, aiming for the largest-ever layoffs at the company.
- The article questions the narrative that AI alone drove this decision, hinting at additional factors.
