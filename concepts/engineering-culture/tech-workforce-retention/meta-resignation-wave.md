# The Pulse: Meta's self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments have triggered a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly impacted, as they proactively seek new opportunities. In response, Meta is offering large equity retainers to retain this talent, but these measures appear ineffective (Orosz, 2026). The situation highlights a predictable consequence of broad workforce restructuring: even those who keep their jobs lose trust and motivation, leading to attrition that retention packages cannot easily reverse.

The article also covers the release of Grok Bot, an AI harness built by the Cursor team that delivers a 'Codex experience, but for knowledge work.' The author reports automating daily workflows with it and expresses strong enthusiasm, predicting that more AI vendors will copy the approach. This segment points to an emerging trend of managed AI agents for general knowledge tasks, beyond coding (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments are causing unaffected engineers to resign voluntarily, despite equity retainers.
- Retention packages alone are unlikely to stem the resignation wave, as trust and morale have already been damaged.
- Grok Bot, created by the Cursor team, is a generic AI harness for knowledge work, akin to Codex for coding.
- The author predicts widespread imitation of Grok Bot's approach among AI vendors.
