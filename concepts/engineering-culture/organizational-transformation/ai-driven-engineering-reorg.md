# Why is Meta destroying its engineering organization?

Meta's engineering culture, once a legendary driver of innovation and individual impact, has undergone a rapid and disruptive transformation in recent weeks. Historically, Meta empowered engineers through autonomy, a 'move fast' ethos, and a focus on impact, making it a profit center for the company. However, a strategic pivot to AI leadership has led leadership to reallocate engineering talent toward data labeling and AI training, fundamentally breaking the established culture and demoralizing engineers (Orosz, 2026).

The catalyst for this shift is Meta's massive investment in AI, including the acquisition of a 49% stake in Scale AI and the appointment of Alexandr Wang to lead AI strategy. To build competitive LLMs, Meta has instituted mandatory tracking of keystrokes and mouse clicks, and forcefully reassigned 30-50% of engineers on core teams to the ADO (Agent Data Optimisation) org for repetitive AI training tasks. This has been particularly jarring for infrastructure and security teams, who lost top engineers and now feel their work is menial and detrimental to career growth (Orosz, 2026).

The internal response has been overwhelmingly negative, with comparisons to 'The Hunger Games' and widespread privacy concerns over surveillance. While some engineers find the training work engaging, the majority see it as a downgrade from building products used by hundreds of millions of users. This radical overhaul reflects Zuckerberg's determination to dominate AI, but it risks destroying the high-performance engineering culture that made Meta successful in the first place (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's historically empowering engineering culture, characterized by autonomy and impact, is being dismantled by forced reassignments to AI data labeling roles.
- The company is investing heavily in AI, including a $14.8B stake in Scale AI, and is using engineering labor for RLHF and training data under Alexandr Wang's leadership.
- Mandatory keystroke and mouse-click tracking for AI training data, with no opt-out, has raised privacy concerns and employee anger.
- 30-50% of engineers on core teams have been forcefully reassigned to the ADO org, with infra and security teams hit hardest, leading to morale collapse and a metaphor of 'The Hunger Games'.
- The leadership's actions reflect a fear of missing the AI platform opportunity, but at the cost of the engineering-centric culture that once made Meta a top workplace.
