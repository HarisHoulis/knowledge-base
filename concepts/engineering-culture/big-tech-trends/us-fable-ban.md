# The Pulse: Big implications of US banning Anthropic’s new model, Fable

The US government has banned Anthropic's new model, Fable, restricting usage to US citizens only. This policy could have significant global implications: as other countries and non-US companies seek capable AI models, they may turn to open models, most of which come from China, potentially increasing China's influence in AI (Orosz, 2026). The article also provides a follow-up on Meta's engineering org, noting that non-engineering teams lost more than 10% of staff and Integrity teams were already stretched before cuts. Additionally, major corporate moves include SpaceX's IPO making Elon Musk a trillionaire, SpaceX acquiring Cursor, and Cursor acquiring Continue, positioning SpaceX to compete with Anthropic and OpenAI. Other industry updates include Gemini's lead quitting for OpenAI and Microsoft considering dropping OpenAI for DeepSeek.

- US-only restriction on Fable may boost China's open-source models.
- Meta's non-engineering staff cut by >10%, Integrity teams stretched.
- SpaceX acquired Cursor, which acquired Continue, entering AI competition.
- Gemini lead departs to OpenAI; Microsoft mulls DeepSeek alternative.
