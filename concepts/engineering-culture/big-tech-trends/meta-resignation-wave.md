# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

According to Orosz (2026), Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments have inadvertently triggered a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly impacted. The company's attempts to retain these employees with large equity offers have reportedly been ineffective, highlighting the cultural fallout of restructuring. In the same newsletter, Orosz discusses Grok Bot, a generic AI harness released by the Cursor team, which he describes as offering a 'Codex experience, but for knowledge work.' He notes that he automated many daily workflows using the tool and expects more AI vendors to copy the approach. Together, these stories reflect a tech sector navigating workforce instability and the rapid evolution of AI-powered productivity tools.

- Meta's restructuring has caused unaffected engineers to resign, and equity retainers are not stopping the exodus.
- Grok Bot, an AI harness from the Cursor team, provides a Codex-like experience for knowledge work.
- The author personally found Grok Bot useful for automating daily workflows.
- The author predicts that other AI vendors will copy Grok Bot's approach, potentially making it an industry standard.
