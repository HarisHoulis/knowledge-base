# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

In this edition of The Pulse, Gergely Orosz reports on two significant developments in the tech industry. First, Meta is experiencing a self-inflicted resignation wave: layoffs and forced reassignments have prompted even unaffected engineers to seek new opportunities (Orosz, 2026). Despite Meta offering large equity retainers to retain these employees, the measures appear ineffective, suggesting deeper cultural issues. Second, the Cursor team has released Grok Bot, a generic AI harness that extends the Codex experience to knowledge work. Orosz, who tested it, is enthusiastic about its ability to automate daily workflows and anticipates that more AI vendors will emulate this approach (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments are pushing non-affected engineers to resign, despite equity retainers.
- Equity retainers have not effectively curbed the resignation wave at Meta.
- Grok Bot by Cursor team is a generic AI harness for knowledge work, praised by the author.
- The author expects more AI vendors to copy Grok Bot's approach.
