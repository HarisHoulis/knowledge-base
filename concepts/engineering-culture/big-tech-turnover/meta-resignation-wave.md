# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta’s recent layoffs and forced reassignments have triggered a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly affected. According to the article, these employees are proactively seeking new jobs, and Meta’s attempt to retain them with large equity retainers is proving ineffective. This highlights the unintended cultural fallout of restructuring decisions in big tech. (Orosz, 2026)

- Meta’s layoffs and forced reassignments prompted unaffected engineers to leave voluntarily.
- Equity retainers offered by Meta have not successfully curbed the resignation wave.
