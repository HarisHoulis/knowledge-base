# On Friday Deploys: Sometimes that Puppy Needs Murdering

The post also appreciates the slower holiday period for creative work and praises an "icing" and "defrosting" period suggested in LinkedIn comments. Overall, Majors advocates for a stable, low-change period during holidays, but argues that can be achieved without high-stakes lock-ins by deliberately freezing merges rather than deploys, and by maintaining deploy rituals even when not shipping new code.

- Deploy freezes are acceptable when teams lack the observability and confidence to move swiftly; they are a workaround, not a moral victory.
- If you freeze deploys, freeze merges too, to avoid a pileup of untested changes that will explode later.
- If you normally deploy frequently, a sudden long freeze can cause instability; continue running deploys without shipping new code to keep systems healthy.
- Avoid moral posturing around freezes; admit they are a hack born of constraints.
- The goal is a stable holiday period with lower change rate, not high-stakes lock-ins and January recoveries.
