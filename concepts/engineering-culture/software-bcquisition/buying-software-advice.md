# Are you an experienced software buyer? I could use some help.

Charity Majors, co-author of the second edition of 'Observability Engineering,' is seeking advice from experienced software buyers to inform a new section on Observability Governance. The world has changed since the first edition (2019-2022), with observability engineering teams now common. The new section will cover staffing, org placement, tool procurement, integration, cost management, and business cases. Majors admits she lacks experience buying software at large enterprises and wants insights from principal engineers, staff engineers, VPs, and directors who have done it multiple times. She specifically asks about evaluating options, vendor changes, proof-of-concept best practices, stakeholder concerns, consensus-building with millions at stake, and primary vs. secondary considerations. She requests emails with subject 'Buying Software' and pledges not to quote without permission or name vendors, emphasizing this is for an O'Reilly book, not Honeycomb marketing.

- The second edition of Observability Engineering will include a major new section on Observability Governance.
- Charity Majors is soliciting firsthand advice on software buying processes, as she has never bought software at scale.
- Key questions include evaluating vendors, running POCs, managing stakeholders, and driving consensus for major purchases.
- All advice will be anonymized and vendors not mentioned; this is an O'Reilly writing project, not a Honeycomb promotion.
- Contributors can email with context on company size and their role in purchasing.
