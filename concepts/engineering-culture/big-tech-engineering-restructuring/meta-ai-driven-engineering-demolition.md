# Why is Meta destroying its engineering organization?

Meta's engineering culture, historically celebrated for its "move fast and break things" ethos and later "move fast with stable infra," has been abruptly dismantled. Engineers previously enjoyed high autonomy, choosing teams via bootcamp and focusing on impactful work. However, starting in late April, leadership forcefully reassigned 30-50% of engineers from core teams to the ADO (Agent Data Optimisation) org for data labeling and RLHF tasks, with infrastructure and security teams hit especially hard (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's engineering culture was historically engineering-centric, with high autonomy, team choice via bootcamp, and minimal process.
- Leadership forced 30-50% of core engineers into data labeling and RLHF work, impacting morale and infrastructure stability.
- Keystroke and mouse tracking for AI training data was introduced without opt-out, later partially rolled back after employee backlash.
- These changes are driven by Zuckerberg's determination to build competitive LLMs, with Scale AI's CEO Alexandr Wang given broad reign over AI strategy.
- The ADO org now has ~6,500 people, more than OpenAI and Anthropic's entire workforces, yet work is often repetitive and demotivating.
