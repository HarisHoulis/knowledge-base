# Interviewing at Staff+ level

Manuel Vivo shares his preparation strategy for Staff+ Individual Contributor interviews, covering the four most common interview types: Data Structures and Algorithms (DS/A), area of expertise (e.g., Android), system design, and behavioral interviews. He emphasizes the need for flexibility, structured preparation, and practice, as Staff+ roles are competitive and require a broad skill set spanning technical depth, leadership, and business impact. He recommends resources like "The Staff Engineer's Path" by Tanya Reilly and "Staff Engineer" by Will Larson to understand the role, and suggests using LeetCode, Pramp, and the Grind 75 list for DS/A practice (Vivo, 2024).

For system design, Vivo highlights the importance of asking questions to gather functional and non-functional requirements, and suggests using Alex Lementuev's "Mobile System Design" GitHub project and mock interview videos, along with books like "System Design Interview" by Alex Xu and "Designing Data-Intensive Applications" by Martin Kleppmann. He advises practicing by designing features of favorite apps. For behavioral interviews, he recommends the structured approach from "Cracking the Coding Interview," preparing stories around challenges, leadership, failures, and business impact, while stressing honesty and authenticity. He also advises preparing for Android-specific interviews by practicing building simple apps, reviewing architecture patterns like MVx and UDF, and understanding the company's tech stack (Vivo, 2024).

Vivo concludes that successful interview preparation involves learning about each interview type, choosing a personal strategy, doing mock interviews, actively listening to interviewers, and staying authentic. He encourages candidates to have fun, leverage their unique experiences, and consider reading "The First 90 Days" by Michael Watkins to prepare for the onboarding phase after landing the job. The overall message is that Staff+ interviews require a balanced focus on technical excellence, leadership, and impact, and that dedicated preparation is key to standing out (Vivo, 2024).

- Staff+ interviews typically include DS/A, area-specific, system design, and behavioral rounds, requiring a versatile and structured preparation approach.
- For DS/A, practice with platforms like LeetCode, use cheat sheets, and follow a problem-solving structure: listen, define edge cases, brute force, optimize, walk through, implement, and test.
- In system design interviews, gather requirements first, discuss trade-offs, and leverage resources like the Mobile System Design GitHub repository and mock interview videos.
- Behavioral interviews should be prepared with concrete examples that demonstrate leadership, impact, and authenticity, using a structured project reflection approach.
- Emphasize your unique strengths and experiences, and treat interviews as opportunities to learn and engage, not just tests to pass.
