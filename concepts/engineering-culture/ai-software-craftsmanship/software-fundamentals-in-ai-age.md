# LIVE: Uncle Bob on Software Fundamentals in the Age of AI

In this interview, Uncle Bob Martin discusses his long software engineering career, which began in 1964 with a three-bit finite state machine toy computer. He describes learning programming from books and executing programs on paper before getting his first job at age 18. The interviewer highlights the outsized influence of Martin's book Clean Code, noting it is the software engineering book most often quoted back to him.

- Uncle Bob's programming career spans over half a century, beginning in 1964 at age 12.
- His 'morning bathrobe rant' about SQL's textual nature and security flaws has become part of his public character.
- Early AI agents like Grock produced poor-quality code that required constant cleanup.
- AI is recognized as fast but prone to making messes, reinforcing the need for software fundamentals.
