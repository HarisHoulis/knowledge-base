# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her decision to move her blog from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade of writing. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction and the vibrant longform tech writer ecosystem on Substack as key reasons for the switch. The move is prompted by her upcoming second edition of "Observability Engineering" and a desire to share her learnings more easily.

- Charity Majors is moving her blog from WordPress to Substack after 10 years.
- She cites WordPress friction and Substack's tech writer community as reasons.
- Email subscriber exports are pending manual review; comments will not be migrated.
- Existing charity.wtf links will remain active for the foreseeable future.
- She will publish new content on charitydotwtf.substack.com.
