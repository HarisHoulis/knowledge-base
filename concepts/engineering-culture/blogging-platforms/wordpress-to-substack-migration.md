# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her move from WordPress to Substack after a decade of blogging. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction and the thriving long-form tech writer community on Substack as key reasons. She mentions working on the second edition of "Observability Engineering" and plans to share her thoughts via Substack. She notes that email subscriber export is pending manual review, comments cannot be transferred, and she will keep charity.wtf live to preserve existing links. She expresses gratitude for the community over the years.

- After 10 years, Charity Majors is moving her blog from WordPress to Substack.
- Reasons include WordPress's high friction and Substack's vibrant long-form tech writer ecosystem.
- She is working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and will publish insights on Substack.
- charity.wtf will remain online to avoid breaking bookmarks, but posts are being ported.
- Email subscriber migration is pending; comments from the old site won't carry over.
