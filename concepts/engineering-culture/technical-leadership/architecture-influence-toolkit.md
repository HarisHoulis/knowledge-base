# The Influence Toolkit: Driving Technical Strategy as a Senior Engineering Leader

Henry Wilson draws on the BBC Sounds re-architecture to present a practical toolkit for senior engineering leaders who need to turn technical strategy into organizational change. The central argument is that effective software architecture is fundamentally about communication, relationship-building, and organizational influence rather than purely technical decisions.

The toolkit covers building buy-in across stakeholders, navigating active resistors, and using written artifacts such as RFCs and ADRs to make decisions transparent and durable. Wilson also emphasizes balancing tradeoffs openly, so that architectural choices are understood and supported rather than imposed.

Taken together, the talk frames architecture leadership as an influence discipline: leaders succeed by persuading, documenting, and negotiating with the organization, not just by designing systems.

- Effective software architecture is fundamentally about communication, relationship-building, and organizational influence
- Build buy-in deliberately and plan for navigating active resistors to technical strategy
- Use RFCs and ADRs as tools to make architectural decisions transparent and durable
- Balance and communicate tradeoffs openly rather than imposing choices
