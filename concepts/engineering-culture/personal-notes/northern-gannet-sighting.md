# Northern Gannet

Simon Willison documents a sighting of Morris, a Northern Gannet, in Pillar Point Harbor, California. Morris is notable as the only known Northern Gannet in the Pacific Ocean, having arrived at the Farallon Islands about 14 years prior and later settling in Pillar Point Harbor. The post provides identification details—a white bird with a yellow head—and notes that Morris associates with Brandt's cormorants near the harbor sign, making it easy to spot from the commercial pier. (Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/15/sighting-391300422/)

- Morris is a Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus) and the only known one in the Pacific Ocean.
- First appeared in the Farallon Islands around 14 years before this 2026 sighting.
- Currently resides at Pillar Point Harbor, readily identifiable by its white body and yellow head.
- Often seen near Brandt's cormorants by the harbor sign at the end of the commercial pier.
