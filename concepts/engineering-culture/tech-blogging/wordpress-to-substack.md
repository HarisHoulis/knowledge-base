# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announced she is moving her blog from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade of writing at charity.wtf. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction and the fact that 'the vibrant ecosystem of tech writers in longform' now lives on Substack as primary motivators (Majors, 2025). Majors also hints at an upcoming wave of writing: she has been working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and says the rough drafts are 'burning a hole in my brain' (Majors, 2025).

The migration involves practical complications. Majors attempted to export her email subscribers but the process is 'held up for manual review' (Majors, 2025). Existing comments from the WordPress years will not be transferred, which she apologizes for. To avoid breaking bookmarks and article links, she plans to leave charity.wtf online indefinitely, and she invites readers to subscribe at her new Substack, charitydotwtf.substack.com (Majors, 2025).

The move reflects a broader trend in tech writing, where longform content and community have shifted from self-hosted platforms to newsletter platforms like Substack. Majors' decision is both practical and emotional: she misses 'tech twitter' and hopes to reconnect with readers on the other side (Majors, 2025).
