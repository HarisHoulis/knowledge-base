# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her decision to move her blog from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade of posting on charity.wtf. She cites the friction of WordPress and the vibrant longform tech writing community on Substack as key reasons for the change. The post reflects on her history, starting the blog in 2015 after leaving Facebook, and touches on her current work on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering', which she plans to discuss in future Substack posts.

Majors outlines practical migration details: email subscriber export is pending manual review, existing comments cannot be transferred, and the old site will remain up to preserve bookmarks and article links. She invites readers to subscribe to her new Substack, charitydotwtf.substack.com, and expresses gratitude to the community that made her decade on WordPress meaningful, despite her frustrations with the platform.

- Charity Majors is migrating from WordPress to Substack after ten years of blogging.
- WordPress friction and the strong tech writing ecosystem on Substack are primary motivations.
- She is working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share insights on Substack.
- Old content and links at charity.wtf will remain accessible for the foreseeable future.
- Email subscriber migration is pending; comments from the old site will not carry over.
