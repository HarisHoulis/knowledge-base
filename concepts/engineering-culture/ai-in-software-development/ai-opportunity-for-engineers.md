# The craft 'dying' is your OPPORTUNITY

Kent C. Dodds argues that while AI is making developers lazy and generating slop, this is not a new phenomenon—humans have always been prone to abdicating judgment. He notes that AI is non-deterministic, but so are humans, and bugs have always existed due to laziness or deference to authority. The real change is that agents are becoming more autonomous, which shifts the developer's role from implementing tickets to designing systems and maintaining product vision. He calls this an opportunity: developers who retain critical thinking and intentionally design systems with AI agents in mind will become differentiators, while others will hand off all responsibility. He shares his own experience with a 500,000-line codebase built solo since March, which was previously unimaginable, and encourages developers to focus on building the 'software factory' and primitives that make agents more effective. The human value now is ownership, accountability, and responsibility, which always mattered but is more crucial as AI handles more routine work.

- AI is not the root cause of laziness; humans have always deferred judgment to libraries, companies, or other authorities.
- Agents are becoming more autonomous, so developers must focus on system design and product vision to ensure a good user experience.
- Critical thinking and intentional design are the new differentiators in a world where AI can generate code.
- Developers should invest time in building software factory primitives to make their AI agents more effective.
- Ownership, accountability, and responsibility are the core human contributions, amplified by AI assistance.
