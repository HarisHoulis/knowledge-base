# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a parallel between the rise of cloud computing in 2010 and AI's mainstream adoption in 2025, using her experience at Linden Lab where S3 was an experimental solution to a data problem. She notes that by 2010, cloud had shifted from experimental to the default, and similarly, in 2025 AI has become foundational technology, at least in developer tools. She acknowledges the existence of a bubble but argues that froth doesn't disprove value, emphasizing that every major innovation wave comes with hype. Majors also addresses SREs, urging them to engage with AI rather than dismiss it, because their critique must be grounded in expertise to be relevant and heard. Her own position evolved significantly in just eight months, from viewing AI as a satellite topic to recognizing it as mainstream and exciting for builders.

- Cloud's transition from experimental to mainstream in 2010 mirrors AI's similar shift in 2025.
- Even with a bubble, AI has real value and is now foundational in developer tools.
- SREs should engage with AI to keep their criticism informed and relevant, not dismiss it from the sidelines.
- The author's perspective shifted within eight months, highlighting how quickly AI's role solidified.
