# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a parallel between the state of AI in 2025 and the state of cloud computing in 2010, arguing that AI has shifted from experimental, satellite technology to mainstream, foundational technology, at least in developer tools. She recalls her early days at Linden Lab when cloud was experimental, used for overflow and side projects, but by 2010 it became the default, with datacenters relegated to legacy. She suggests AI is undergoing the same transition now. She acknowledges the hype bubble around AI, but asserts that froth does not disprove value, just as with past transformative technologies. Majors also references a keynote with Fred Hebert at SRECon where they urged SREs to engage with AI rather than dismiss it, emphasizing that realistic critique must be grounded in expertise.

- AI in 2025 is analogous to cloud in 2010: moving from experimental to mainstream foundational technology.
- Even if there is a bubble, the underlying value of AI remains real, just as with cloud and the internet.
- SREs and engineers should engage with AI constructively rather than dismiss it, to keep critiques grounded.
- The shift is especially evident in developer tools, where AI has become central.
- The transition is a pattern seen with prior major tech shifts: hype waves accompany real innovation.
