# When AI Tooling Doesn't Help: Documenting Limits as Engineering Judgment

In an update from Amazon engineering leadership, Terk Terkowitz argues that engineers whose work does not benefit from current AI tooling should treat that as a finding rather than a personal failing. The recommended response is to document why the tooling falls short, share that evidence with peers, raise it with managers, and work toward solutions.

Terkowitz frames this explicitly as a demonstration of judgment: an engineer who can articulate where AI does not yet help their work is showing exactly the kind of discernment the organization values. The quote, as relayed by Simon Willison, positions honest assessment of AI's limits as a legitimate and valued engineering contribution rather than resistance to adoption.

- Lack of benefit from AI tooling is a finding to document, not a personal failing
- Share evidence of where AI falls short with peers and managers
- Explaining AI's limits in your work demonstrates engineering judgment
