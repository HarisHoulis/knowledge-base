# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws an analogy between the state of AI in 2025 and the state of cloud computing in 2010, arguing that AI has shifted from experimental, satellite technology to a foundational part of mainstream engineering, particularly in developer tools. She recalls how cloud adoption began as 'experimental' projects at Linden Lab and by 2010 became the default, with datacenters becoming legacy. She sees a similar tipping point for AI, despite acknowledging the existence of a hype bubble. Majors emphasizes that bubbles and froth do not disprove underlying value, and she urges SREs and engineers to engage with AI critically but constructively rather than dismissing it entirely. She references her SRECon keynote with Fred Hebert, 'AIOps: Prove It!', where they called on SREs to ground their skepticism in expertise and active participation.

- AI in 2025 has become mainstream foundational technology, analogous to cloud in 2010.
- The AI hype bubble exists but does not negate the real value AI provides.
- Engineers, especially SREs, should engage with AI rather than take potshots from outside.
- AI has been integrated into developer tools, marking a shift from experimental to standard.
- The cloud analogy shows that disruptive technology often starts as 'experimental' projects.
