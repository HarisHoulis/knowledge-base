# Stop being skeptical about AI for development with Charity Majors

Charity Majors argues that AI skepticism was rational until 2025, but that by 2026 AI is clearly transforming software engineering. Her turning point came in November 2025 with Opus 4.5 and, more importantly, the coding harness Claude Code, which turned AI from a scripting aid into serious infrastructure. She compares AI's industry impact to that of cloud computing in 2010: a shift that is now inevitable, not optional (Orosz, 2026).

- Skeptics had valid reasons before 2025 due to overhyped past technologies like COBOL and no-code tools, but the evidence for AI's generational impact is now strong.
- One critical question for engineers: what would it take to confidently ship code you have not read? Majors believes this will become standard practice.
- Code review is overrated; humans add more value in deciding what to build than in reading code for correctness.
- DevOps has failed at its goal of connecting software engineers to production, and AI-driven non-deterministic systems require more validation discipline, not less.
- Career advice: run toward AI, get it on your resume, and use small acts of control (like no-AI Wednesdays) to combat AI fatigue.
