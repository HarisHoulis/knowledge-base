# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors recounts early cloud adoption at Linden Lab, where using S3 as an experimental asset store led to a proliferation of other experimental cloud projects. By 2010, the cloud had shifted from experimental to mainstream, while datacenters became legacy. Majors draws a direct parallel to AI in 2025, noting that just eight months earlier she and Fred Hebert delivered an SRECon keynote titled "AIOps: Prove It!" urging SREs to engage with AI because it is not all hype. Since then, her position has changed: AI is no longer satellite or experimental but has become the mainstream, foundational technology, at least in developer tools. She acknowledges that a bubble exists—just as it did for cloud and the internet—but argues that froth does not disprove the existence of value. She concludes that it is an exciting time to be a builder.

- AI in 2025 resembles cloud in 2010: shifting from experimental to foundational in engineering.
- Despite hype cycles, AI has real value beyond the bubble, as seen with previous transformative technologies.
- The SRE community is called to engage with AI critically and from expertise rather than dismiss it from outside.
- The author's perspective evolved from cautious to excited within eight months.
- The existence of a bubble does not negate the long-term value of the technology.
