# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a direct parallel between the state of AI in 2025 and the state of cloud computing in 2010. She recounts her experience at Linden Lab in 2006 when EC2 and S3 were introduced, and how cloud initially served as an experimental solution to datacenter constraints before gradually becoming the mainstream foundation. By 2010, the center of gravity had shifted from datacenters to the cloud, and Majors argues the same shift is now happening with AI, at least in developer tools (Majors, 2025).

Majors acknowledges the widespread skepticism among SREs and the industry's history of hype cycles, but insists that AI is not merely a mirage. She points to her own recent evolution in perspective—just eight months prior, she and Fred Hebert were delivering a keynote urging SREs to engage with AI rather than dismiss it. The existence of a bubble, she argues, does not disprove the existence of value. This year, AI stopped being satellite technology and became foundational, similar to cloud's tipping point in 2010 (Majors, 2025).

- Cloud transitioned from experimental to mainstream around 2010, and AI is undergoing the same transition now.
- AI's current hype bubble is real, but like past bubbles, it does not negate underlying value.
- SREs and technical pessimists should engage with AI rather than dismiss it, to keep critique grounded in expertise.
- By 2025, AI has become foundational technology in developer tools, no longer a separate or satellite topic.
