# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors draws a direct parallel between the adoption of cloud computing and the current state of AI. She recalls that when S3 and EC2 were introduced in 2006, they were experimental; by 2010, the cloud had become the mainstream foundation for engineering (Majors, 2025). Similarly, she argues, 2025 marks the year AI stopped being satellite or experimental technology and became foundational, at least in developer tools.

- Cloud adoption followed a trajectory from experimental to mainstream, and AI is now on the same path.
- SREs must engage with AI rather than dismiss it, bringing their risk-aware and critical expertise to the table.
- The existence of a hype bubble does not negate the underlying value of a transformative technology.
- The shift of AI into mainstream foundational technology is especially visible in developer tools.
