# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

The author reflects on a keynote with Fred Hebert at SREcon where they urged SREs to engage with AI rather than dismiss it as hype. She emphasizes that professional skeptics need to ground their critique in expertise to remain relevant. While acknowledging the AI bubble, she insists that froth doesn't negate underlying value, and that AI is now central to building—not a satellite technology (Majors, 2025).

- AI in developer tools shifted from experimental to mainstream in 2025, mirroring cloud's shift around 2010.
- Experimental AI projects are becoming the standard, just as early cloud experiments at Linden Lab eventually replaced datacenters.
- Skeptics should engage with AI constructively rather than dismiss it, grounding critique in technical expertise.
- The AI hype bubble is real, but bubbles can coexist with genuine transformative value.
