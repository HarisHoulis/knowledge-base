# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors argues that 2025 marks a turning point for AI: it has moved from experimental, satellite technology to the mainstream foundation of developer tools, analogous to how the cloud became the default by 2010. She recalls her early experiences at Linden Lab using S3 when cloud was an experimental bet, only for it to become the new norm within a few years. She and Fred Hebert gave a keynote at SRECon urging SREs to engage with AI rather than dismiss it, emphasizing that their expertise is needed to ground the hype in reality. While acknowledging that a bubble exists, she insists that froth does not negate the underlying value of AI, just as prior bubbles accompanied transformative technologies.

- AI in 2025 is like cloud in 2010: shifting from experimental to foundational in engineering practice.
- Skeptics and SREs should engage with AI to provide grounded, expert critique rather than dismissing it outright.
- Hype bubbles accompany every major technological shift, but real value remains underneath.
- Author's personal history with early cloud adoption at Linden Lab parallels today's AI adoption curve.
