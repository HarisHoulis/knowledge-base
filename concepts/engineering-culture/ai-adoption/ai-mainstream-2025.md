# 2025 was for AI what 2010 was for cloud

Charity Majors argues that 2025 marked a turning point for AI, analogous to how cloud computing became mainstream around 2010. She recalls her early experience at Linden Lab, where cloud services like S3 were initially experimental but quickly became the foundation for engineering work. Similarly, AI has shifted from a satellite technology to a core part of developer tools and infrastructure (Majors, 2025).

Majors highlights her SRECon keynote with Fred Hebert, where they urged fellow SREs to engage with AI rather than dismiss it as hype. While acknowledging the existence of a speculative bubble, she insists that froth does not disprove underlying value. The article emphasizes that engineers' realistic risk assessment is crucial, but it must be grounded in expertise to be relevant (Majors, 2025).

Despite initial skepticism, Majors expresses excitement about the transition, viewing it as a time of opportunity for builders. She draws a direct parallel to past technology shifts, noting that every major innovation comes with hype, but the value persists (Majors, 2025).

- AI in 2025 for developer tools is comparable to cloud in 2010: moving from experimental to mainstream foundation.
- The shift happened rapidly, within roughly eight months, changing how engineers view AI's role.
- A hype bubble exists, but it does not negate the real value AI brings.
- SREs and engineers should engage with AI critically and constructively, not just dismiss it.
- The author feels it is an exciting time to be a builder as AI becomes integral.
