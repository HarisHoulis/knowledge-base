# Open Source Is Dead. Long Live Open Source.

Saoud Rizwan, founder of Cline, argues that AI has fundamentally changed open source, killing its communal spirit. He notes that platforms like GitHub have become archives of AI-generated pull requests and issues, eroding trust and turning community collaboration into skepticism. Projects like Zig have banned AI use entirely to preserve contributor growth, while curl's CEO has considered shutting down the bug bounty program due to AI-generated bug reports. tldraw now auto-closes all pull requests, and GitHub has added a feature to disable third-party PRs altogether, signaling the end of the open contribution model that defined open source.

- AI-generated contributions have made open source communities untenable, with projects like Zig banning AI and tldraw auto-closing all PRs.
- Maintainers face a flood of low-quality AI bug reports and PRs, leading some to disable third-party contributions entirely.
- Supply chain attacks are an increasing risk, as seen in the lightllm compromise where attackers stole publishing tokens and installed a credential harvester.
- Despite the death of community-driven open source, the principles of free use and building on top remain vital, especially for open-weight models.
- The economic value of open source is shifting from community contribution to open models and permissive licensing, but security requires new safeguards.
