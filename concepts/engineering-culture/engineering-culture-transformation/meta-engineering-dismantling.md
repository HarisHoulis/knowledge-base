# Why is Meta destroying its engineering organization?

Meta's engineering culture, once celebrated for empowering engineers and prioritizing impact, has undergone a dramatic shift in recent weeks under CEO Mark Zuckerberg's aggressive AI push. Historically, Meta (Facebook) fostered a high-performance, engineering-centric culture with principles like "move fast and break things," later evolving into "move fast with stable infra." Engineers had autonomy, easy team transfers, and were treated as a profit center. However, since April 2026, leadership has implemented policies that many engineers see as dismantling this successful culture.

- Meta has forcefully reassigned 30-50% of engineers from core product and infrastructure teams to the new Agent Data Optimisation (ADO) org for data labeling and RLHF work, causing significant morale issues.
- Meta installed a system that tracks all employee keystrokes and mouse clicks to generate AI training data, with no opt-out initially, later adding limited controls after employee pushback.
- The article suggests Zuckerberg is determined not to miss the AI platform shift, investing heavily in AI (including acquiring a 49% stake in Scale AI) and treating engineering as a cost center rather than a profit center.
- Engineers report feeling like 'tributes in the Hunger Games,' randomly selected and removed from impactful work to do repetitive AI training tasks, which they fear will hurt their careers.
- The ADO org now has around 6,500 people, more than the entire headcount of OpenAI or Anthropic, reflecting Meta's massive bet on AI training over traditional software engineering.
