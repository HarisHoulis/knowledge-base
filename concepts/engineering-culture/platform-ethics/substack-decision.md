# Hello World

Charity Majors announces her decision to stay on Substack despite community backlash over the platform's handling of Nazi content. She explains that her primary motivation is to re-engage with the technical community, which largely still exists on X, but she prefers not to return there. Substack offers a lower-friction publishing experience compared to WordPress, which she hopes will enable her to post more frequently and participate in ongoing technical discussions. She argues that Substack is not comparable to platforms like Gab or Parler, and while governance is difficult, she considers Substack a 'light dove grey' rather than an unacceptable choice. She acknowledges the ethical concerns and provides methods for readers to interact with her content without financially supporting Substack, such as using RSS or email, and she commits to not accepting paid subscriptions. Ultimately, she respects that others may make different decisions and frames this as choosing her battles: her job is to contribute to the technical mainstream, and she believes this platform enables that mission despite its flaws.
