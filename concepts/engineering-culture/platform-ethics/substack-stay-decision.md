# Hello World

Charity Majors explains her decision to remain on Substack after initially planning to migrate from WordPress, despite criticism that the platform supports Nazis. She argues that platforms exist on a continuum and that Substack is not akin to Gab or Parler; she feels more ethically compromised by her $15.99 monthly YouTube subscription than by her $0 relationship with Substack. Her primary goal is to re-engage with the tech mainstream, which still largely exists on X, but she prefers to be active on LinkedIn and Substack. She also finds Substack reduces friction for posting compared to WordPress's heavyweight feel. To minimize value to Substack, she does not accept paid subscriptions, provides an RSS feed, and plans to auto-post to WordPress. She respects those who choose to leave but states this battle is not hers (Charity Majors, "Hello World", 2025).

- Decided to stay on Substack after pausing migration and consulting with the LeaveSubstack author, experts, and stakeholders.
- Primary goal is to get back into the mainstream of technical discussion; secondary goal is to remove posting friction.
- Views Substack as 'light dove grey' on a continuum of platforms, not a Nazi-supporting site like Gab or Parler.
- Practical mitigation: no paid subscriptions, use RSS/email instead of the app, and set up auto-posting to WordPress.
- Respects conscientious objectors who cannot engage with Substack at all, but she will not fight that battle.
