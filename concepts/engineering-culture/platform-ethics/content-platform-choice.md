# Why I'm Staying on Substack

Charity Majors explains her decision to remain on Substack after initially planning to migrate from WordPress. She acknowledges the ethical concerns raised by critics regarding Substack's moderation of right-wing extremism, but argues that Substack is far from platforms like Gab or Parler. She frames her primary goal as returning to active technical discussion, noting that the tech mainstream still largely exists on X, which she prefers to avoid. Substack offers lower friction for posting than WordPress, which she sees as heavyweight and a barrier to sharing ideas frequently.

- Charity decided to stay on Substack after considering the LeaveSubstack argument and consulting peers.
- Her primary goal is to re-engage with the technical community; Substack and LinkedIn are her chosen venues.
- She minimizes Substack's revenue by not accepting paid subscriptions and encourages email/RSS use over the app.
- She compares Substack to 'light dove grey' rather than an outright extremist platform.
- She respects others' decisions to boycott Substack but does not consider this her battle.
