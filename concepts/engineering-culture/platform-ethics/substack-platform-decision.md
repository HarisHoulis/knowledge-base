# Hello World

Charity Majors explains her decision to remain on Substack despite criticism that the platform supports Nazis. She acknowledges the concerns, researched LeaveSubstack, and consulted peers, but ultimately concluded that her primary responsibility is to re-engage with the technical community. She notes that the tech mainstream still largely exists on X, but she prefers to avoid returning there and instead will be more active on LinkedIn and Substack (Majors, 2025).

She argues that Substack is not like Gab or Parler, which exist solely to platform violent extremists, and that all content platforms exist on a continuum of gray. To minimize financial benefit to Substack, she does not accept paid subscriptions, encourages use of RSS or email over the app, and plans to auto-post to Wordpress. She respects that others may disagree and uses a vivid metaphor about picking shit off one's plate to acknowledge the moral stance of conscientious objectors (Majors, 2025).

- Charity Majors decided to stay on Substack because her job requires active participation in mainstream technical discussions, which currently still happens on platforms like X and Substack.
- She believes Substack is far from platforms like Gab or Parler, describing it as 'a light dove grey' rather than a morally irredeemable platform.
- To reduce support for Substack, she does not accept paid subscriptions and encourages readers to use RSS or email instead of the app.
- She plans to auto-post from Substack to Wordpress to lower friction and maintain a presence on other platforms.
- She respects the decision of those who cannot in good conscience engage with Substack, and understands the 'shit on the plate' metaphor.
