# Hello World

Addressing the accusation that using Substack supports Nazis, Majors argues that Substack is far from platforms like Gab or Parler and exists on a spectrum of grey governance. She personally feels more ethically compromised by her YouTube subscription than by Substack. To minimize value to Substack, she avoids paid subscriptions, encourages RSS/email usage, and plans to auto-post to WordPress. She respects others who choose to leave, using a vegan friend's metaphor to acknowledge that some may see Substack as irredeemable, but asserts this battle is not hers to fight (source: https://charity.wtf/p/hello-world-7b0).

- Charity Majors decided to stay on Substack after originally planning to move from WordPress.
- Her primary goal is to rejoin the technical mainstream and engage more actively with the community, which currently exists on X and similar platforms.
- Substack reduces friction for posting, making it easier to write and publish quickly.
- She does not accept paid subscriptions and encourages RSS/email use to minimize Substack's financial benefit.
- She respects that others may view Substack as unethical, but she personally sees it as acceptable and not a platform for Nazis.
