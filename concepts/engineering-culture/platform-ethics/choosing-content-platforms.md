# Hello World

Charity Majors announces her decision to move her blog from WordPress to Substack, reversing her initial pause after community backlash over Substack's moderation of extremist content. She explains that her primary motivation is to re-engage with the technical community, which requires being active where the conversations happen, and that Substack lowers the friction for posting compared to WordPress. She addresses ethical concerns by clarifying that she does not accept payments, provides an RSS feed for conscientious objectors, and views Substack as a light grey platform rather than one that solely exists to platform Nazis. Ultimately, she respects others' decisions to boycott Substack but asserts this is not a battle she will fight, as her core duty is to contribute to tech discourse.

- The author decided to stay on Substack after pausing migration to consider ethical objections.
- Her primary goal is to rejoin mainstream technical discussion, which currently requires being on platforms like LinkedIn and Substack.
- Substack lowers posting friction compared to WordPress, helping her write more regularly.
- She minimizes Substack's benefit by not accepting money and promoting RSS/email consumption.
- She distinguishes Substack from platforms like Gab or Parler and sees it as an acceptable grey compromise.
