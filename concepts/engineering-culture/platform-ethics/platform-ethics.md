# Hello World

Charity Majors reflects on her decision to move her blog from WordPress to Substack in December 2025, after receiving criticism that Substack supports Nazi content. She paused the migration, read LeaveSubstack, consulted experts and stakeholders, and ultimately decided to stay. Her primary reason is professional: she feels she has been 'going dark' on social media and needs to re-engage with the technical community, which largely still exists on X. Substack lowers friction for posting and feels more like writing an email, which she hopes will help her contribute more regularly to the mainstream technical discussion. She argues that while Substack is not perfect, it is far from platforms that solely exist to support Nazis, and she compares it to a 'light dove grey' on a continuum of governance challenges. To mitigate ethical concerns, she does not accept paid subscriptions, promotes RSS and email for reading, and plans to auto-post to WordPress. She acknowledges that some may see any contact with Substack as unacceptable but states that this is not her battle, emphasizing that she must decide where to spend her energy and focus on doing her job.

- Charity Majors decided to stay on Substack after reconsidering due to LeaveSubstack criticisms, but she will not accept money for subscriptions.
- Her main motivation is to re-enter the technical mainstream and post more frequently, which she found difficult on WordPress.
- She sees Substack as ethically acceptable compared to extremist platforms, though not perfect, and plans to minimize Substack's benefit by using RSS and auto-posting to WordPress.
- She acknowledges that other creators may choose differently, but she has decided this is not her battle.
