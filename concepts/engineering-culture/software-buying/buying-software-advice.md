# Are you an experienced software buyer? I could use some help.

Charity Majors, co-author of the second edition of "Observability Engineering," is adding a substantial new section on Observability Governance. The section will cover topics such as staffing observability teams, tool selection and purchasing, cost management, and making business cases to executives. However, Majors admits she has never worked at a large software-buying enterprise and lacks firsthand experience in purchasing tools, so she is publicly asking for advice from engineers and leaders who have navigated vendor selection, proof-of-concept evaluation, and multi-million-dollar procurement decisions. She invites readers to email her with their insights, requesting context about company size and role, and explicitly states that she will not quote anyone without permission or name specific vendors, as this is an O'Reilly book project, not a Honeycomb initiative.

- The second edition of Observability Engineering includes a new 'Observability Governance' section covering team staffing, tool buying, cost control, and executive communication.
- Majors admits she lacks direct experience buying software at large enterprises and asks for practical advice from experienced software purchasers.
- She requests email submissions with context about company size and the sender's role in purchasing decisions.
- All submissions are treated confidentially: no quotes without signed permission and no vendor names will be mentioned, good or bad.
- The project is for O'Reilly, not Honeycomb, despite Majors' affiliation with Honeycomb.
