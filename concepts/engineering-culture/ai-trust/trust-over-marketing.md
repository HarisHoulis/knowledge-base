# Dario Amodei on AI Trust and Delivering on Promises

In a quote shared by Simon Willison, Dario Amodei argues that negative public perception of AI is not primarily caused by AI leaders' warnings about risks, but by a fundamental crisis of trust. He suggests that people distrust companies, governments, and the tech industry overall, and AI is just the latest focus of this long-standing skepticism. According to Amodei, a glitzy marketing campaign with a positive spin would not restore trust; phrases like 'AI will cure cancer' have become clichés and are often seen as deceptive. Instead, he insists that the only effective way to regain public trust is to actually deliver on big promises to benefit the world. Amodei acknowledges that the most accurate criticism of AI companies, including Anthropic, is their failure to date to deliver on those promises, and he directs critics to focus on that rather than messaging and marketing.

- Public distrust of AI stems from a broader crisis of trust in institutions, not just AI risk warnings.
- Marketing campaigns with positive spin will not fix the trust problem; they may even backfire.
- Building trust requires actually delivering on promised benefits, e.g., curing cancer, not just saying it.
- The most valid criticism of AI companies is that they haven't yet delivered on their big promises.
