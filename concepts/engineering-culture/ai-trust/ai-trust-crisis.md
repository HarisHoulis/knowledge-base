# Dario Amodei on AI Trust and Delivering on Promises

Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, argues that the public's negative view of AI stems from a fundamental crisis of trust, not from AI leaders' warnings about risks. He asserts that ordinary people distrust companies, governments, and the tech industry, a skepticism that predates AI and is not easily remedied by marketing campaigns. (source: [Quoting Dario Amodei](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/16/dario-amodei/))

Amodei criticizes the suggestion that Anthropic should run a positive-spin campaign, stating that phrases like 'AI will cure cancer' have become clichés and are perceived as deceptive. He believes the most accurate criticism of AI companies is that they have not yet delivered on their big promises to benefit the world. The way to rebuild trust, he insists, is through actual tangible outcomes, such as curing cancer, rather than messaging or narrative control. (source: [Quoting Dario Amodei](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/16/dario-amodei/))

- Public distrust in AI is part of a broader crisis of trust in institutions, not caused primarily by AI safety warnings.
- Marketing spin and inspirational messaging are ineffective and can be seen as deceptive.
- AI companies, including Anthropic, have failed to deliver on their most significant promises to benefit the world.
- Rebuilding trust requires concrete achievements, such as actually curing cancer, rather than better communication.
