# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta’s recent layoffs and forced reassignments have triggered a resignation wave, with even engineers not directly affected choosing to seek new jobs. Despite Meta offering large equity retainers to keep these employees, the strategy does not appear to be working, as the departures continue (Orosz, 2026). This outcome was described as predictable, suggesting management was aware of the risk but proceeded anyway.

The article also highlights Grok Bot, a generic AI harness built by the Cursor team that delivers a ‘Codex experience, but for knowledge work.’ The author tested it, automated daily workflows, and became a fan, predicting that more AI vendors will copy the harness. This signals a shift toward managed AI agents that can handle broad knowledge-work tasks beyond coding (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments caused a resignation wave, including among non-affected engineers.
- Meta offered large equity retainers to counter the wave, but the approach appears ineffective.
- Grok Bot is a generic AI harness for knowledge work, similar to Codex but broader.
- The author of The Pulse predicts more AI vendors will copy Grok Bot.
