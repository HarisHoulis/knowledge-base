# Uncle Bob on Software Fundamentals in the Age of AI

In this interview, Uncle Bob (Robert C. Martin) discusses his long career in software development, beginning in 1964, and shares his initial reactions to AI coding tools. He recounts how he was skeptical of early AI models, but was surprised by a Grok agent that could write code quickly—though it made a mess that he had to constantly clean up. The conversation highlights that despite AI's speed, core software engineering fundamentals remain essential for managing quality and maintainability (Pocock, 2026).

- Uncle Bob has been programming for over half a century, starting at age 12 with a toy computer.
- He is the author of Clean Code, one of the most influential books in software engineering.
- Early AI agents produced code rapidly but required significant cleanup, underscoring the need for human oversight.
- Software fundamentals like clean code practices remain critical even as AI tools become more capable.
