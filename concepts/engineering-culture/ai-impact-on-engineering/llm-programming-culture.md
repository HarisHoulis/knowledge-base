# Fragments: June 16 - AI, Domain-Driven Design, and Decentralization

Martin Fowler's June 16 fragments open with Dave Thomas's celebration of programming with LLMs. Thomas, co-author of 'The Pragmatic Programmer', finds that AI removes drudgery, speeds up feedback loops, revives abandoned projects, and enables exploration of new technologies, making programming more fun than ever. Fowler connects this to broader industry observations about AI's positive impact on developer experience.

- Dave Thomas: LLMs make programming more fun by removing drudgery and accelerating feedback
- Chelsea Troy: Be conscious of different conversation registers with LLMs; changing registers warrants a fresh context window
- Charity Majors: There's a dangerous divide between AI enthusiasts and skeptics; we need shared feedback and engineering discipline
- Simon Willison: AI coding agents have achieved product-market fit, driving price increases at Anthropic and OpenAI
- Mike Masnick: Decentralization and user control over data are essential to fight enshittification
