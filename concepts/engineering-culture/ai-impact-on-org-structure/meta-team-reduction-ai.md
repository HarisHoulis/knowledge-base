# The Pulse: Meta wanted to reduce teams by 60% because of AI

The article reports on a Reuters investigation revealing that Meta's leadership decided to reduce team sizes by 60%, with plans hatched in January to execute the company's largest-ever layoffs. The decision is directly attributed to the perceived productivity gains from AI, reflecting a broader trend of AI-driven organizational downsizing in Big Tech (Orosz, 2026). The piece situates this within The Pulse's coverage of Big Tech and startups, highlighting the significant operational impact of AI on engineering team structures. While the full details of the Reuters report are not fully reproduced, the core claim is that Meta is aggressively reshaping its workforce around AI capabilities.

- Meta's leadership planned to reduce team sizes by 60% due to AI.
- Decisions were made in January, according to Reuters.
- The plan would result in Meta's largest-ever layoffs.
- This reflects a broader trend of AI-driven organizational restructuring in tech.
- The report is covered by The Pulse, a newsletter from Gergely Orosz.
