# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announced she is moving her blog from WordPress to Substack after a decade of writing at charity.wtf. She notes that she wrote her first post on December 27, 2015, and now feels it's time to change platforms because WordPress is frustrating and creates friction that discourages posting (Charity Majors, "Moving from WordPress to Substack", 2025).

The move is also driven by the fact that Substack has become the home of longform tech writing, and she misses tech Twitter. She is currently working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share her thoughts on Substack as drafts are turned in (Charity Majors, 2025).

She provides housekeeping notes: email subscriber export is under manual review, comments from the old site won't transfer, and she will keep charity.wtf up to avoid breaking bookmarks or links. She invites readers to subscribe at charitydotwtf.substack.com (Charity Majors, 2025).

- After ten years on WordPress, Charity Majors is moving her blog to Substack.
- Primary reasons: WordPress friction and Substack's vibrant tech longform community.
- She's working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and will share insights.
- Subscriber import is pending, comments won't transfer, old site stays up for link preservation.
