# Hi, thanks for reaching out to me 👋

Kent C. Dodds explains that he receives 30-100 direct support requests per day across Twitter, GitHub, Discord, email, and other channels. While he genuinely enjoys helping others and credits answering questions for much of his experience, the volume has become unsustainable and is interfering with his actual commitments and family life. He acknowledges that his responsiveness helped create this demand, but he now needs to reduce his involvement in open source and direct messaging to avoid burnout.

- Answering questions was key to Kent's growth, but the number of direct requests has grown to an unsustainable level.
- Kent is reducing open source involvement and responding less on social media and email to protect time for family and commitments.
- He will continue consolidated office hours but stops being a 'search engine' for his content.
- Technical support for his courses goes through dedicated email channels, while community questions can be addressed in the Epic Web Discord.
- He encourages others to help answer questions in the community as a way to gain experience and reduce the burden on maintainers.
