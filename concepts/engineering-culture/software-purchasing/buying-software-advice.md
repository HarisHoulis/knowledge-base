# Are you an experienced software buyer? I could use some help.

Charity Majors announces she is working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and is adding a new section on 'Observability Governance.' This section addresses how to staff observability teams, where they should sit in the org chart, how to buy good tools, integrate them, manage costs, and make business cases to executives. Majors admits she has never bought software at a large enterprise scale and is seeking advice from experienced engineers and VPs on how to approach software purchasing, including vendor evaluation, proof of concept, stakeholder management, and consensus-building.

- The second edition of 'Observability Engineering' will include a new 'Observability Governance' section.
- The author is seeking practical advice on buying software, as she lacks direct experience in large-scale enterprise purchasing.
- Questions include how to shop for options, signs for changing vendors, conducting POCs, and driving consensus among stakeholders.
- She explicitly states this is an O'Reilly writing project, not a Honeycomb marketing effort, and will not mention vendors by name.
