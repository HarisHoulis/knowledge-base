# The Pragmatic Engineer AMA

In this AMA episode of the Pragmatic Engineer podcast, Gergely Orosz shares personal stories from his career transition and editorial decisions. He reveals that the COVID-19 pandemic and Uber layoffs prompted him to leave his engineering manager role and eventually focus on writing, which led to the creation of The Pragmatic Engineer newsletter. The AMA also covers his decision not to publish a critical article about Bunq after receiving a message from an engineer who credited the company for giving them a chance, which shaped his editorial policy of writing about what works rather than what seems broken (Orosz, "The Pragmatic Engineer AMA," https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pragmatic-engineer-ama).

- Orosz's career shift was triggered by COVID-19 layoffs at Uber, leading him to finish books and eventually build The Pragmatic Engineer.
- A single message from a grateful Bunq engineer changed his editorial approach, focusing on positive company practices instead of exposés.
- Orosz argues LeetCode-style interviews persist because they select for tolerance of corporate nonsense, a trait valued in Big Tech.
- MCP became a standard partly because Anthropic was not yet a dominant lab, avoiding lock-in fears; a similar protocol from Google was not adopted.
- He warns that AI can cause skill atrophy and intentionally avoids AI in writing to preserve his craft.
