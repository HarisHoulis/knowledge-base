# Do Software Fundamentals Still Matter?

Matt Pocock argues that software fundamentals—code quality, software entropy, and good design patterns—are more critical than ever in the age of AI. He emphasizes that AI agents work best in well-structured codebases, and neglecting fundamentals increases entropy and degrades the codebase over time. Pocock suggests that investing in solid engineering practices makes codebases more understandable and maintainable for both humans and AI tools. The video directs viewers to resources for building AI-friendly codebases, reinforcing the idea that foundational skills amplify AI's effectiveness.

- Software fundamentals are still essential despite AI advancements.
- Code quality and low software entropy help AI agents navigate codebases effectively.
- Good design patterns make codebases easier for AI to understand and modify.
- Building codebases that AI 'loves' is a competitive advantage in development workflows.
