# Panel: Who Builds the Frontend Now? (And What Breaks When They Do)

This panel discussion brings together Luca Mezzalira, James Hall, Danielle An, and Ivan Zarea to examine how AI tools and vibe coding are reshaping frontend and mobile engineering practice. Rather than focusing on tooling mechanics, the panel centers on the human and organizational consequences of AI-generated code entering the development workflow.

The panelists discuss how the role of junior developers is evolving as AI takes over more routine code production, and how teams must rethink what skills and mentorship juniors need. They also address code review fatigue caused by the volume of AI-generated contributions, and the need for architectural guardrails to keep quality and consistency under control.

A further theme is designing systems that can accommodate autonomous agents as participants in the development process, alongside the economic shifts affecting open-source software sustainability as AI changes how code is produced and consumed.

- AI tools and vibe coding are changing how frontend and mobile engineering work is organized and staffed
- Junior developer roles are evolving, requiring new approaches to skills and mentorship
- Teams face code review fatigue from high volumes of AI-generated code and need architectural guardrails
- Systems must be designed to accommodate autonomous agents in the development workflow
- Economic shifts in open-source sustainability are a concern as AI alters code production
