# Do software fundamentals still matter?

In this short video, Matt Pocock argues that software fundamentals remain critical even as AI tools become more prevalent in development. He emphasizes that code quality, management of software entropy, and good design patterns are not outdated but are actually more important than ever when working with AI agents. The reasoning is that AI models perform better and produce more reliable results in well-structured, maintainable codebases, where clear patterns and low entropy reduce ambiguity and error-prone behavior.

Pocock suggests that the goal is to build codebases that AI agents 'love to work in'—environments with consistent conventions, minimal technical debt, and strong modularity. By prioritizing fundamentals, developers enable AI tools to be more effective and safer to use, turning them into powerful collaborators rather than sources of unpredictable changes. The video implicitly challenges the idea that AI makes deep software knowledge obsolete, instead positioning fundamentals as a prerequisite for successful AI-assisted development.

- Software fundamentals like code quality and design patterns remain essential in the age of AI.
- Managing software entropy is crucial to keep codebases maintainable and AI-friendly.
- Good design patterns help AI agents understand and modify code with fewer mistakes.
- The ultimate goal is to build codebases that AI agents can effectively and safely work within.
