# The Pulse: Big implications of US banning Anthropic’s new model, Fable

The article summarizes recent tech industry events, focusing on the US government's decision to restrict Anthropic's new model, Fable, to US citizens only. This policy could inadvertently boost China's influence, as other countries and non-US companies may turn to capable open-source models, many of which originate from China. The piece also provides follow-up on Meta's engineering org cuts, noting that non-engineering teams lost more than 10% of staff while Integrity teams were already stretched thin. Additionally, it reports SpaceX's IPO making Elon Musk a trillionaire, SpaceX acquiring Cursor, and Cursor acquiring Continue, indicating SpaceX's ambition to compete with Anthropic and OpenAI. Finally, it mentions other industry moves such as Gemini's lead leaving for OpenAI and Microsoft considering dropping OpenAI for DeepSeek.

- US ban on Anthropic's Fable may increase China's influence in AI via open-source models.
- Meta's layoffs disproportionately affected non-engineering teams, with Integrity teams already stretched.
- SpaceX acquired Cursor, which acquired Continue, signaling entry into the AI race against Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Industry shifts include Gemini lead departing to OpenAI and Microsoft weighing DeepSeek as an OpenAI alternative.
