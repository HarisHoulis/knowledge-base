# Linus Torvalds on AI Debugging: Persistent Grunt Work, But Gives Up

In a commit message for the Linux kernel, Linus Torvalds describes a grueling debugging session where AI played a significant supportive role. He credits the AI for doing much of the grunt work, such as adding debug code and analyzing results faithfully when pushed. However, he notes the AI repeatedly claimed the problem was impossible and unsolvable, suggesting it was trained to give up more easily than a stubborn human engineer. Torvalds still acknowledges the AI's contributions by letting it write the commit message.

- AI can substantially accelerate debugging by performing repetitive analysis and code instrumentation.
- Current AI models may prematurely conclude a problem is unsolvable, lacking the persistence of a determined human.
- The effective use of AI in debugging requires the human to push through the AI's limits and direct continued effort.
- Torvalds demonstrates a pragmatic attitude: give credit where due, but maintain human oversight and stubbornness.
