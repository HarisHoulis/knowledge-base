# Hello World

Charity Majors explains her decision to stay on Substack after initially announcing a move from WordPress. She paused her migration to listen to critics and consult stakeholders, including Liz Fong-Jones and the author of LeaveSubstack. Her primary motivation is to return to the mainstream of technical discussion, which she believes still exists on X, but she prefers to be active on LinkedIn and Substack to avoid X. She found WordPress too heavyweight for quick posts, while Substack feels like writing an email, lowering friction for posting more frequently. To address ethical concerns, she argues Substack is a 'light dove grey' compared to platforms like Gab or Parler, and she never accepts money from Substack subscriptions, while encouraging RSS readership and planning an auto-poster to WordPress. She respects that others may draw different ethical lines and choose to unsubscribe.

- Charity decided to remain on Substack after pausing migration and consulting with peers, customers, and critics.
- Her core goal is to re-engage with the tech community, especially via LinkedIn and Substack, while avoiding X.
- Substack reduces posting friction compared to WordPress, which she perceives as too heavyweight.
- She mitigates Substack's monetization by not accepting paid subscriptions, promoting RSS/email, and planning WordPress cross-posting.
- She views Substack as ethically acceptable ('light dove grey') and respects those who disagree.
