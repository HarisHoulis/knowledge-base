# The Pulse: Anthropic kills the coding interview

This edition of The Pulse reports that a team at Anthropic has removed coding (DSA) questions from its software engineering recruitment process, because the team found it got zero signal from them. The author notes the rest of the company and other AI-native startups could follow suit.

The newsletter also rounds up other industry news: incident.io cut its AI spend from $4,500 per developer per month in August to $1,600 now; Amazon formally incorporated AI usage into SDE and SDM expectations after finding a correlation between AI usage and productivity, and requires engineers and managers to stay close to customers; and Microsoft offered 60,000 employees the chance to quit by year-end, though the exit package is not especially tempting. Security incidents also continued to be reported over the week.

- Anthropic's team removed coding/DSA interview questions because they produced zero signal for hiring
- Other AI-native startups and the rest of Anthropic could follow the same approach
- incident.io reduced AI spend per developer per month from $4,500 to $1,600
