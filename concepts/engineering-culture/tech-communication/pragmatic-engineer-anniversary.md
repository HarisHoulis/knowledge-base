# The Pragmatic Engineer: Five Years

Gergely Orosz reflects on five years of The Pragmatic Engineer newsletter, which launched in 2021 and has grown to over 1.1 million readers, tens of thousands of paid subscribers, a podcast, and more than 500,000 YouTube subscribers. The growth was organic, driven largely by word-of-mouth and social media without paid advertising [1]. The publication began with deep-dive articles on topics like Uber's platform split and Big Tech's approach to project management, and later added a weekly 'Pulse' news section [1].

A turning point was the deep-dive on Uber's move to the cloud, initially reported via informal 'back door' sources. After that, companies began engaging through official channels, enabling more accurate and detailed stories on OpenAI, Meta's Threads, Stripe, and Figma [1]. In 2024, Orosz launched The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast to share longer conversations with engineers; in 2025, the first Pragmatic Summit was held in San Francisco with 500 attendees [1]. The article also announces a special anniversary offer of $100/year for new annual subscribers, valid until 8 September [1].

- The Pragmatic Engineer reached 1.1M+ readers and 500K+ YouTube subscribers in five years, growing without paid ads.
- Deep-dive reporting shifted from 'back door' informal sourcing to 'front door' official company access after Uber's cloud migration story.
- The publication expanded from weekly deep-dives to include 'The Pulse' news, a podcast (2024), and the Pragmatic Summit (2025).
- The anniversary special resets annual subscription pricing to $100/year, ending September 8.
- Orosz's path included a blog since 2007, an email digest in 2019, and going full-time after leaving Uber in 2020.
