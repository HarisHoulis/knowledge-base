# Developers Reporting Double-Digit Weekly Time Savings from AI Fell from 68% to 26%

Atlassian's State of Developer Experience 2026 reports a sharp drop in perceived AI productivity gains: the share of developers saving ten or more hours per week with AI fell from 68% to 26% year over year, according to InfoQ's coverage of the report. Only 28% of developers say they experience noticeably less friction than before AI was introduced.

The report also highlights a gap between leaders and practitioners. Engineering leaders report spending more time managing and correcting AI output than developers themselves do, suggesting oversight and review overhead is landing disproportionately on management.

Taken together, the findings indicate that early enthusiasm about AI-driven time savings has not held up as adoption matured, with friction and correction work offsetting expected gains.

- Developers saving 10+ hours weekly with AI dropped from 68% to 26% year over year
- Only 28% report noticeably less friction than before AI
- Engineering leaders spend more time managing and correcting AI output than developers do
