# Move Fast, Don't Break Trust: Shipping Constantly with Humans and Beyond

Suhail Patel describes how Monzo organizes its engineering organization and technical stack so that it can ship safely hundreds of times per day. The core enabler is a strong internal platform that gives engineers opinionated microservice abstractions, so teams can iterate rapidly without rebuilding common infrastructure or sacrificing reliability.

Safety at that velocity comes from layered guardrails rather than manual gates: tenancy-based staging environments, static analysis checks, and continuous profiling. These mechanisms catch problems early while keeping the path to production fast.

Patel frames the payoff as trust: a platform that makes the safe path the easy path lets engineers—and increasingly LLMs—iterate quickly without eroding confidence in the system.

- Monzo ships safely hundreds of times a day, enabled by a strong internal platform.
- Opinionated microservice abstractions let engineers iterate without rebuilding common infrastructure.
- Tenancy-based staging, static analysis checks, and continuous profiling provide safety without slowing delivery.
- The platform's guardrails extend to LLM-assisted iteration, preserving reliability as automation increases.
