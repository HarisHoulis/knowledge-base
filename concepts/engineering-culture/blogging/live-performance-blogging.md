# Quoting John Gruber

John Gruber responds to Simon Willison's technical blogging tips by comparing his blogging approach to playing live music rather than recording a studio album. He explains that while he occasionally aims for a polished, album-like piece, most posts are part of a continuous, professional performance. Gruber emphasizes concentration and careful execution, aiming to hit every note in time while moving fluidly from one topic to the next. This highlights a pragmatic, sustainable mindset for regular content creation, valuing consistency and professionalism over attempting to make every post a masterpiece. Simon Willison quotes this response on his blog, framing it as a valuable perspective on balancing quality and output.

- Blogging is like a live performance, not a studio album—most posts are part of an ongoing, imperfect but professional flow.
- Occasional pieces can be treated as polished 'albums,' but expecting every post to be a hall-of-famer leads to writer's block.
- Concentration and care matter, but so does the ability to move from topic to topic without over-polishing.
- The live-performance mindset helps maintain consistent output while preserving professionalism.
