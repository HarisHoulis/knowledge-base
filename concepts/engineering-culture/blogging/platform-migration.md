# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her migration from WordPress to Substack after a decade of blogging on charity.wtf. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction, which has discouraged her from publishing, and notes that Substack now hosts a vibrant ecosystem of longform tech writers. The move also aligns with her upcoming work on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering,' which has produced insights she plans to share.

- Charity is leaving WordPress due to friction and distaste for the platform.
- Substack is seen as the current home for longform tech writing.
- She is porting her post corpus but will keep charity.wtf active to avoid breaking links.
- Email subscribers are being transferred but are pending manual review.
- Historical comments cannot be exported to Substack.
