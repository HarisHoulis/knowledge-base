# 2025 in Review

In his 2025 year in review, Kent C. Dodds describes a major pivot in focus from EpicReact/EpicWeb toward EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). He recounts how a conversation with Ryan Florence after Epic Web Conf 2025 led him to the realization that the next era is 'add your app to the chatbot' rather than adding a chatbot to your app, and that MCP provides the standard protocol to make this vision possible (Kent C. Dodds, 2025-12-31). He launched EpicAI.pro, ran workshops, and released a self-paced MCP workshop, while continuing to improve the Epic Stack and the Epic Workshop App (epicshop).

- Kent C. Dodds made a major professional pivot from EpicReact/EpicWeb to EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- The core vision shifted from adding chatbots to apps to integrating apps into a unified assistant (Jarvis) via MCP.
- Epic Stack saw significant updates: React Router v7, passkeys, Tailwind v4, image optimization with Tigris/openimg, and a migration to react-router-auto-routes.
- Epic Web Conf 2025 and Epic Web Camp were cancelled due to financial losses and the need to avoid splitting focus.
- Plans for 2026 include developing a curriculum for an 'Intro to TypeScript Programming' college course.
