# 2025 in Review

Kent candidly shares that Epic Web Conf 2025 and Epic Web Camp 2025 lost money, forcing him to cancel future events. On a personal note, he welcomed his sixth child and shares travel statistics (58,698 miles, 16 trips). Looking forward, he plans to develop an "Intro to TypeScript Programming" curriculum for a local college.

- Pivoted from web development education to AI and Model Context Protocol (MCP), launching EpicAI.pro.
- Highlights the shift from 'add a chatbot to your app' to 'add your app to the chatbot,' envisioning a Jarvis-like assistant.
- Improved the Epic Workshop App with a new interactive CLI (`npx epicshop`).
- Epic Stack received significant updates including React Router v7, passkeys, and Tailwind v4.
- Epic Web Conf 2025 and Epic Web Camp 2025 were canceled due to financial losses, freeing focus for MCP initiatives.
