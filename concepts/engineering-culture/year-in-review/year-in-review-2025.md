# 2025 in Review

Kent C. Dodds reflects on a year of major professional pivots and personal milestones. He shifted focus from EpicReact/EpicWeb to EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), driven by a vision of a 'Jarvis'–like assistant that coordinates across services rather than a chatbot bolted onto an app. This pivot was catalyzed by conversations with Ryan Florence and Evan Bacon's React Conf demo, leading to the launch of EpicAI.pro and workshops like 'Epic MCP: From Scratch to Production' (Kent C. Dodds, 2025-12-31, https://kentcdodds.com/blog/2025-in-review).

- Pivoted from web development education to EpicAI and MCP, envisioning a future where users add their apps to an AI assistant rather than using separate chatbots.
- Launched EpicAI.pro and ran MCP workshops, including a self-paced production-focused workshop, confirming the importance of MCP in the AI ecosystem.
- Epic Web Conf 2025 and Epic Web Camp were cancelled due to financial losses and the need to focus, marking a painful but deliberate reduction in event commitments.
- The Epic Workshop App CLI (epicshop) was significantly improved, and the Epic Stack received major updates including React Router v7, passkeys, Tailwind v4, and new tools like react-router-devtools.
- Personally welcomed a sixth child and spent 62,518 minutes listening to podcasts and audiobooks, while planning to teach an 'Intro to TypeScript Programming' class at a local college in 2026.
