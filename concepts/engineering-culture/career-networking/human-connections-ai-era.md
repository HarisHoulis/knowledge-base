# AI is taking your job (but not how you think)

The article argues that while AI is flooding job applications with generated resumes and automated filtering, it cannot replicate the power of genuine human relationships. The author shares an anecdote about interviewing a candidate who used ChatGPT and later reappeared under a different name with another AI-generated resume, illustrating the impersonal nature of AI-driven job seeking. In contrast, the author attributes every job he has had since graduating to personal connections, such as meeting someone at a conference who helped him land a remote position at PayPal.

- AI-generated resumes and automated filtering make job hunting more competitive, but real personal connections cut through the noise.
- In-person interactions strengthen online relationships and can lead to career opportunities that AI cannot replicate.
- Attending and organizing conferences and meetups creates valuable networking moments.
- Events like Epic Web Conf and Camp are intentionally structured to maximize human connection.
