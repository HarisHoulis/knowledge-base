# AI is taking your job

In this article, Kent C. Dodds argues that while AI is reshaping the job market—leading to AI-generated resumes and automated hiring filters—human connections remain a powerful differentiator. He shares an anecdote about a hiring manager encountering the same candidate under different names with AI-generated resumes, illustrating the absurdity of AI-driven applications. However, he emphasizes that personal relationships, especially formed in person, cut through the noise and have been the key to every job he's landed since graduating. He advocates for attending and organizing conferences and meetups, where structured breaks and activities maximize meaningful interactions. Dodds highlights his own events, Epic Web Conf and Epic Web Camp, as examples of fostering these connections, concluding that AI cannot replicate human relationships, making them more valuable than ever in a digital world.

- AI-generated applications are flooding job postings, making it harder for real candidates to stand out and for hiring managers to find them.
- In-person connections are more critical than ever; every job Kent has gotten came through personal recommendations or knowing the right person.
- Conferences and meetups are powerful for building relationships, not just for talks; the real value is in hallway conversations and shared activities.
- AI can automate many things, but it cannot replicate the depth of human relationships, which strengthen all future online and offline interactions.
- Events like Epic Web Conf and Camp are deliberately designed to maximize connection opportunities through single tracks, extended breaks, and group activities.
