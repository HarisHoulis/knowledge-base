# Practitioner Voice: The Writing Category Nobody has Named Yet

The article highlights a distinct style of writing from practitioners, which Jim Highsmith identifies as separate from academic writing and thought-leadership content. This style, which Martin Fowler advocates and encourages his contributors to follow, emphasizes practical experience and actionable insights. Highsmith names this category to recognize its importance and distinguish its unique characteristics.

- Practitioner voice is a distinct writing category separate from academic writing and thought-leadership.
- It focuses on practical experience and actionable insights from real-world practice.
- Jim Highsmith named this category to give it recognition and clarity.
- Martin Fowler supports and encourages this style among his contributors.
