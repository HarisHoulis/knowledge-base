# The Pulse: Big implications of US banning Anthropic’s new model, Fable

The newsletter discusses the US government's decision to restrict Anthropic's new model, Fable, to US citizens only, and the potential global consequences. It suggests that this move could increase China's influence as other countries and non-US companies turn to capable open models, most of which originate from China.

- The US ban on Fable may push other nations toward Chinese open-source AI models, boosting China's geopolitical influence.
- Meta's engineering culture continues to deteriorate, with non-engineering teams losing more than 10% of staff and Integrity teams already overstretched.
- SpaceX's IPO made Elon Musk a trillionaire, and the company acquired Cursor, which itself acquired Continue, signaling SpaceX's ambition to compete directly with Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Industry shifts include Gemini's lead leaving for OpenAI, Epic Games releasing an open-source version control system, and Microsoft considering dropping OpenAI for DeepSeek.
- Research confirms that LLMs amplify existing expertise, deepening the gap between skilled and unskilled users.
