# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta’s layoffs and forced reassignments have triggered a wave of voluntary resignations among engineers who were not directly impacted, creating a self-inflicted talent drain. The company has responded with large equity retainers, but these offers appear ineffective at stemming the exodus, as trust and morale have already been damaged (Orosz, 2026).

The article also highlights the release of Grok Bot, an AI harness built by the Cursor team for automating knowledge work. The author describes it as a 'Codex experience, but for knowledge work' and reports using it to automate daily workflows, predicting that more AI vendors will copy the approach (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta’s layoffs and forced reassignments have pushed unaffected engineers to seek new jobs, creating a resignation wave.
- Meta is offering large equity retainers to retain engineers, but these measures are not working.
- Grok Bot is a new AI harness from the Cursor team that automates knowledge work, analogous to Codex for software engineering.
- The author expects other AI vendors to replicate Grok Bot’s capabilities.
