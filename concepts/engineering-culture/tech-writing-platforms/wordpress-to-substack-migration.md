# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her departure from WordPress after a decade of blogging on charity.wtf, citing WordPress's friction and the vibrant longform tech writer ecosystem on Substack as key reasons for the move. She reflects on the timing—ten years after her first post and just after leaving Facebook—and notes that she has been writing the second edition of 'Observability Engineering,' which has generated insights she plans to share on Substack.

- After 10 years and ~1,000 posts, Charity Majors is moving her blog from WordPress to Substack.
- WordPress is described as high-friction and disliked, while Substack has become the hub for longform tech writing.
- She has been working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and will stream thoughts from that process.
- Email subscriber import is stuck in manual review; old comments will not transfer; charity.wtf will remain up to preserve links.
