# Hello World: Charity Majors on Moving to Substack and Deciding to Stay

Charity Majors announced her decision to move her blog from Wordpress to Substack after ten years, but faced criticism from readers who argued that Substack platforms Nazis. After pausing her migration and reading LeaveSubstack, she decided to stay on Substack, explaining that her primary goal is to re-engage with the tech community, and Substack lowers the friction of posting compared to Wordpress. She acknowledges that while Substack is not a perfect platform, it is not in the same category as Gab or Parler, and she plans to minimize any financial benefit to Substack by not accepting paid subscriptions, encouraging RSS/email use, and cross-posting to Wordpress.

- Charity Majors initially announced a move from Wordpress to Substack, then paused to reconsider after backlash.
- She decided to stay on Substack, citing her need to be more active in technical discussions and the lower friction of Substack for quick posts.
- She does not believe Substack is equivalent to far-right extremist platforms; she compares it to 'light dove grey' rather than outright Nazi-supporting.
- To mitigate ethical concerns, she will not accept paid subscriptions, will use RSS/email to minimize Substack's benefit, and will auto-post to Wordpress.
- She respects that others may disagree and choose to boycott Substack entirely.
