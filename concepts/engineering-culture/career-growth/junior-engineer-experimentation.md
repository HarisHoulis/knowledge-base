# Junior Engineers: Don't Wait for Permission

Matt Pocock argues that junior developers shouldn't wait until they reach mid-senior level before experimenting with advanced workflows like a "software factory" approach. The common objection is that juniors make more mistakes, but Pocock reframes the issue: what matters is not your mistake rate but the level of trust and responsibility you've been granted [source].

Because juniors typically carry less responsibility and lower stakes, they have more room to experiment safely. Rather than treating junior status as a reason to defer adopting new workflows, Pocock suggests juniors should start experimenting now, within the bounds of the trust they've been given [source].

- Don't wait until mid-senior level to try advanced workflows like a software factory.
- The deciding factor isn't your mistake rate but the trust and responsibility you're given.
- Lower responsibility at junior level can mean more freedom to experiment safely.
