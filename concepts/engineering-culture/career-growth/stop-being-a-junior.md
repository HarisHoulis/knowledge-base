# Stop Being a Junior

The article 'Stop Being a Junior' (Dodds, 2023) argues that software developers should not wait a fixed amount of time to shed the 'junior' label. Because technology evolves rapidly, it's possible to become expert in a chosen area quickly, and experience upstream doesn't limit one's current capability. The author emphasizes that no time limit defines when a developer graduates from junior status, and staying on top of modern tools allows anyone to contribute at a high level.

- There is no required time to stop being a junior; expertise can be gained quickly in fast-moving technologies.
- Act like the senior you want to become: volunteer for complex work, attend meetings, and learn from others.
- Share your knowledge through brown bag lunches and meetups to demonstrate your value and credibility.
- Communicate your goals and promotion aspirations to your manager early, not just during performance reviews.
- Sometimes switching companies is necessary to escape the 'junior' perception and achieve larger responsibilities.
