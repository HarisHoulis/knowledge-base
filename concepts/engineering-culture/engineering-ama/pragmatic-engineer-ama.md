# The Pragmatic Engineer AMA

On AI, Orosz argues that AI doesn't make work easier; it forces harder thinking. He deliberately uses zero AI in his writing to avoid skill atrophy, while accepting that his hand-coding ability will degrade as he uses AI for coding. He advises being mindful of tradeoffs and continuing to practice skills you value (Orosz, 2026).

- The Pragmatic Engineer's origin story: COVID-19 and Uber layoffs pushed Orosz into full-time writing.
- Editorial policy: focus on what works inside companies, not what seems broken, after a Bunq article was pulled.
- LeetCode interviews remain because they filter for candidates willing to tolerate pointless work.
- MCP became standard because Anthropic was not yet a dominant lab; today's market would likely block adoption.
- AI tools require deliberate practice to avoid skill atrophy; Orosz uses no AI for writing but accepts coding skill degradation.
