# Why is Meta destroying its engineering organization?

Meta's engineering culture was once the envy of Big Tech—engineers had autonomy, impact-driven work, and a 'move fast with stable infra' ethos. But starting in April, leadership began dismantling this, forcefully reassigning 30-50% of engineers on core teams to data labeling and RLHF duties, while also introducing mandatory keystroke and mouse-click tracking for AI training data with no opt-out (Pragmatic Engineer). These changes have left engineers feeling like a cost center, with infrastructure and security teams hit hardest as their best engineers are 'drafted' into menial work.

- Meta force-reassigned 30-50% of engineers on core teams to data labeling and RLHF, often removing top performers from infrastructure and security roles.
- Mandatory keystroke/click tracking for AI training data caused privacy backlash, prompting Meta to later add pause controls and exemptions.
- The strategy follows Meta's perceived AI failure with Llama 4 and its $14.8B investment in Scale AI, bringing in Alexandr Wang to lead AI efforts.
- Engineers describe the experience as 'The Hunger Games'—arbitrary, disruptive, and a sharp break from Meta's historical engineering-centric autonomy.
- The article questions whether this 'AI psychosis' is unique to Meta or a broader industry trend.
