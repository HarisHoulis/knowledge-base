# A Review of My Time at Remix

Kent C. Dodds announces he is leaving the Remix team to focus on his new project, EpicWeb.dev, but emphasizes he remains deeply committed to the Remix community and its future. He reflects on his 10 months at Remix, where he served as Director of Developer Experience and helped shape a rapidly growing community around the framework. Dodds highlights significant community metrics, including 13k Discord members, 429 GitHub contributors, 12.5k public projects using Remix, and sold-out Remix Conf with over 300 attendees. He also notes Remix won 'Breakthrough of the Year' at React Summit 2022. Dodds describes his technical contributions, such as building the Remix CLI, the Stacks feature, and speeding up test runs, alongside his responsibilities as a cofounder in weekly strategy meetings with Ryan and Michael.

- Leaving Remix team to focus on EpicWeb.dev, but staying active in Remix community.
- Community growth milestones: 13k Discord members, 429 contributors, 12.5k public projects, sold-out Remix Conf.
- Director of Developer Experience: built Remix CLI, Stacks, doubled test speed, merged 485 PRs.
- Remix won Breakthrough of the Year at React Summit 2022.
- Mission-driven work focused on quality software and user/developer experience.
