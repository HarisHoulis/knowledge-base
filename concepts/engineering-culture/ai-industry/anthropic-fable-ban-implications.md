# The Pulse: Big implications of US banning Anthropic’s new model, Fable

The article discusses how the US government's decision to restrict Anthropic's new model, Fable, to US citizens only could backfire by making China more influential in AI. As non-US countries and companies look for capable open models—many of which originate from China—this ban may drive them away from US AI products. The piece also covers a follow-up on Meta's restructuring, revealing that non-engineering teams lost over 10% of staff, leaving Integrity teams stretched thin. In tech industry moves, SpaceX's IPO made Elon Musk a trillionaire, SpaceX acquired Cursor, and Cursor acquired Continue, signaling SpaceX's ambition to compete with OpenAI and Anthropic. Additional news includes Gemini's lead quitting for OpenAI, Epic Games releasing an open source version control system, and Microsoft considering dropping OpenAI for DeepSeek.

- The US ban on Anthropic's Fable could increase China's influence as other countries turn to open models, many from Chinese developers.
- Meta's non-engineering teams saw >10% staff cuts, worsening strain on Integrity teams.
- SpaceX acquired Cursor, which acquired Continue, positioning SpaceX to rival Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Microsoft is reportedly considering switching from OpenAI to DeepSeek, while research shows LLMs amplify existing expertise.
