# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments have triggered a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly impacted, as they proactively seek new opportunities. In response, Meta is offering large equity retainers to retain these employees, but the article suggests these efforts are not proving effective. This highlights the unintended cultural and retention consequences of broad workforce restructuring (Orosz, 2026).

Separately, the article covers Grok Bot, a generic AI harness built by the Cursor team, described as the 'Codex experience, but for knowledge work.' The author tried it and found it highly useful for automating daily workflows, predicting that more AI vendors will adopt similar approaches. This points to a growing trend of AI agents moving beyond code generation into general knowledge work automation (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments are pushing unaffected engineers to leave, despite equity retainer offers.
- Equity retainers appear insufficient to counter the loss of trust and morale caused by restructuring.
- Grok Bot is a generic AI harness for knowledge work, developed by the Cursor team.
- The author reports that Grok Bot successfully automated many daily workflows and expects industry-wide imitation.
