# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her move from WordPress to Substack after a decade of blogging on charity.wtf. She explains that WordPress has become too friction-heavy, preventing her from writing, and notes that the vibrant longform tech writing ecosystem has shifted to Substack. She also misses tech Twitter and hopes to find a new community on Substack (Majors, 2025).

- Majors started blogging on December 27, 2015, shortly after leaving Facebook and before incorporating Honeycomb.
- She cites WordPress's friction as a major reason for moving, along with Substack's growing presence in the tech longform community.
- She is working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share insights after drafts are submitted.
- Housekeeping: email subscriber export is in manual review, comments cannot be migrated, and charity.wtf will remain active to preserve links and bookmarks.
