# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her move from WordPress to Substack after nearly a decade of blogging at charity.wtf. She cites frustration with WordPress's friction and the perception that Substack hosts a vibrant longform tech writing ecosystem. She plans to port her post corpus but will keep the original site up to avoid breaking links and bookmarks.

- WordPress has too much friction, discouraging publishing.
- Substack appears to be the current home for longform tech writing.
- She is working on the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' and plans to share insights via Substack.
- Email subscriber export is stuck in manual review; historical comments cannot be migrated.
- The original site will remain up to preserve bookmarks and article links.
