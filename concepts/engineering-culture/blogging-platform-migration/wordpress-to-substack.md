# Moving from WordPress to Substack

Charity Majors announces her decision to move her long-running tech blog from WordPress to Substack, citing frustration with WordPress's friction and the vibrant longform tech writing ecosystem on Substack. She reflects on a decade of blogging since leaving Facebook and before incorporating Honeycomb, and notes that the second edition of 'Observability Engineering' has inspired her to share more thoughts via streaming posts.

- WordPress's friction discourages regular posting, prompting the move to Substack.
- Substack is seen as the current home for longform tech writers.
- Existing email subscribers are being exported but held for manual review; comments cannot be migrated.
- The original site charity.wtf will remain up to preserve bookmarks and article links.
- Upcoming posts will draw from lessons learned while writing the second edition of 'Observability Engineering'.
