# The Pulse: We need to talk about migrations with AI

The article discusses how AI is transforming framework migrations, citing Asana's migration off the testing framework Enzyme as a key example. The project, which involved rewriting massive numbers of test cases, was completed in two weeks with AI assistance; without AI, the work would likely have been postponed indefinitely. Airbnb and Uber report similar successes, suggesting AI is a superb fit for this type of migration work.

- Asana completed its Enzyme migration in two weeks using AI, a task that would otherwise have been deferred.
- Airbnb and Uber have also used AI for migrations, indicating a broader trend.
- AI appears especially well-suited for framework migrations.
- Gartner's Magic Quadrant ranks AWS, Microsoft, and IBM above AI labs like Anthropic and OpenAI, likely due to vendors paying Gartner, while AI startups avoid this 'Gartner tax.'
