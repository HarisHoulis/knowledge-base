# Thoughts on Motivation and My 40-Year Career

Charity Majors reflects on her unconventional path from a fundamentalist homeschooled childhood to a successful tech career, emphasizing how work became a source of liberation and meaning. She left home at 15, forged her own path through university and into Silicon Valley, and eventually co-founded Honeycomb. Her motivation, she explains, is not solely about advancing the industry but about living with integrity and building things of enduring value (Majors, 2025).

- Work can be a vehicle for personal growth and liberation, not just a paycheck.
- Personal values and integrity become central when leading a company.
- Joy in work comes from solving problems and building enduring things.
- Creation is harder than critique, and running a business forces ethical trade-offs.
- Ideals must be balanced with the reality of winning in the marketplace.
