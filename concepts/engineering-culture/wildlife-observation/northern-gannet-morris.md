# Northern Gannet

The article describes a Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus) named Morris, who is the only known individual of this species in the entire Pacific Ocean. Morris first appeared in the Farallon Islands off San Francisco 14 years ago, as referenced by a Bay Nature article, and has since made Pillar Point Harbor in California his home. The bird is easy to spot due to its distinctive appearance: the only white bird with a yellow head, often associating with smaller black Brandt's cormorants near the harbor sign at the end of the commercial pier.
