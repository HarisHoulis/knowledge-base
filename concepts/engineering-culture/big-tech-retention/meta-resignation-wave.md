# The Pulse: Meta’s self-inflicted resignation-wave

Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments have unexpectedly triggered a resignation wave among engineers who were not directly impacted. These unaffected employees are proactively seeking new jobs, prompting Meta to offer large equity retainers to retain them, though the efforts appear ineffective. The situation highlights how poorly managed restructuring can alienate even retained talent, creating a self-inflicted talent drain.

- Meta's layoffs and forced reassignments pushed non-impacted engineers to look for new jobs.
- Meta is offering large equity retainers to keep these engineers, but it seems to be failing.
- The resignation wave is described as predictable, stemming from the restructuring's impact on morale.
- This event illustrates the broader engineering-culture risk of talent loss during organizational change.
