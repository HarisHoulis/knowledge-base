# Hello World

Charity Majors explains her decision to stay on Substack despite community backlash over the platform's handling of Nazi content. She initially paused her migration after receiving criticism and reading LeaveSubstack, but ultimately chose to remain, citing a need to re-engage with the technical mainstream and lower friction for posting. She acknowledges ethical trade-offs but argues Substack is not comparable to explicitly extremist platforms like Gab or Parler, calling it a 'light dove grey' in a continuum of governance challenges.

- Primary goal is to return to mainstream technical discussion, which still largely happens on X; she prefers LinkedIn and Substack over X.
- Substack reduces friction compared to WordPress, making it easier to post regularly.
- She does not believe using Substack equates to supporting Nazis, noting she feels more ethically compromised by her YouTube subscription.
- She offers ways for conscientious objectors to engage: no paid subscriptions, RSS/email feeds, and an auto-poster to WordPress.
