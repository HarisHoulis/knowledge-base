# Quoting Matt Webb on Learning with AI

Matt Webb describes how, after releasing version 1.0 of his app Galactic Compass, he expected to manually implement quaternion rotations. Instead, he used ChatGPT as a patient, interactive tutor to educate him on the topic, enabling him to finally understand quaternions well enough to make the app work—something he had not achieved through books or asking mathematician friends. Webb emphasizes that outsourcing thinking to AI does not stop learning; rather, it pushes him to learn more, which he finds a positive outcome. The quote is shared by Simon Willison, highlighting the educational potential of conversational AI in practical development contexts.

- AI can serve as an interactive tutor, not just a code generator, helping users understand complex concepts.
- Hands-on learning with AI can overcome barriers that traditional study methods (books, expert consultation) could not.
- Outsourcing thinking to AI can actually increase motivation and capacity to learn new skills.
- Learning continues even when AI assists with implementation, as the user gains conceptual understanding.
