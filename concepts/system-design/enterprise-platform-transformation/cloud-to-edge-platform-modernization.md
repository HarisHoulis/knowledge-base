# Modernising Retail at Scale: Architecting a Cloud-to-Edge Platform for a Global Enterprise

Jayesh Bhayani presents Tesco's decade-long enterprise transformation, moving from fragmented legacy systems to a unified cloud-edge platform. The talk frames the effort as three aligned workstreams—system, talent, and process modernization—arguing that scaling mission-critical retail operations to millions of customers requires all three to move together rather than treating technology as an isolated migration.

The presentation emphasizes practical trade-offs encountered along the way, notably build-versus-buy decisions and managing cloud costs at enterprise scale. These are presented as recurring architectural and organizational choices rather than one-time decisions.

Looking forward, Bhayani connects the platform work to preparing for agentic commerce, positioning the unified cloud-edge architecture as the foundation for emerging AI-driven retail interactions.

- Tesco's transformation spanned roughly a decade, replacing fragmented legacy systems with a unified cloud-edge platform.
- Success depended on aligning system, talent, and process modernization in parallel.
- Build-vs-buy and cloud cost management were key practical lessons at enterprise scale.
- The platform is positioned as groundwork for agentic commerce.
