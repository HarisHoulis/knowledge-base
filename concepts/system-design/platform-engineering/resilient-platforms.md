# Building Resilient Platforms: Insights from 20+ Years in Mission-Critical Infrastructure

Matthew Liste presents 12 core principles for building scalable, reliable platforms for enterprise technology teams [1]. The talk covers avoiding technical debt, managing deprecation, and building a strong engineering culture as foundations for resilient infrastructure platforms [1].

The principles are aimed at designing infrastructure platforms that reduce complexity, streamline developer workflows, and deliver long-term value [1]. The content targets senior architects and engineering leaders, drawing on 20+ years of experience in mission-critical infrastructure [1].

- 12 core principles for scalable, reliable enterprise platforms.
- Avoiding technical debt and managing deprecation are central concerns.
- Strong engineering culture supports platform resilience.
- Goals include reducing complexity, streamlining developer workflows, and delivering long-term value.
