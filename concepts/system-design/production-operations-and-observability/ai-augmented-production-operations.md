# Beyond Observability: Evolving Production Operations in the Age of AI

In this panel discussion, Michael Hausenblas, Sujana Sooreddy, Noam Levi and Renato Losio examine how production operations are changing under the influence of AI. Their central claim is that operational data can be turned into actionable insights that directly support incident response, moving beyond passive observability toward operations that actively inform responders (InfoQ).

The panelists also describe how automation and new architectural practices help engineering teams build systems that are easier to understand. Alongside these practices, they explore how AI reshapes software delivery more broadly and alters the historically deterministic nature of production applications — a shift that affects how teams reason about and operate the systems they run (InfoQ).

- AI is positioned as a way to convert operational data into actionable insights for incident response, extending beyond traditional observability.
- Automation and new architectural practices are presented as means of building more understandable systems.
- AI is described as reshaping software delivery and changing the traditionally deterministic nature of production applications.
