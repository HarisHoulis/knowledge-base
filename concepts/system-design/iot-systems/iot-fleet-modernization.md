# When Software Meets Hardware

Martin Fowler introduces Kuldeep Singh's account of modernizing a large IoT fleet of battery-powered sensors. The central observation is that when software meets hardware, engineering principles remain largely the same, but the sensible defaults often change. Familiar principles stay important; what shifts is how they are applied in practice.

The practices described emerged through experiments, failures, and operational experience. Each challenge helped the team replace a reasonable assumption with a more sensible default, reflecting the iterative, experience-driven nature of adapting software engineering to hardware-constrained environments.

- Engineering principles stay largely the same when software meets hardware, but sensible defaults change.
- The context is modernizing a large IoT fleet of battery-powered sensors.
- Practices emerged through experiments, failures, and operational experience.
- Each challenge replaced a reasonable assumption with a more sensible default.
