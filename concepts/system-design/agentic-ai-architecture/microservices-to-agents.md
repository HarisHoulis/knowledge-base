# From Microservices to Agents: the Next Evolution of Distributed Systems

Mallika Rao's InfoQ article frames agentic AI as the next step in distributed systems architecture, following microservices. Where microservices decompose a system by function, agentic systems decompose by decision, moving the unit of design from services to autonomous decision-making agents.

This shift, per the article, brings new complexity in observability, reliability, and system design. The piece argues that existing architectural patterns and practices must be updated to handle these agent-driven systems rather than simply reusing microservices conventions.

- Microservices decompose by function; agentic systems decompose by decision.
- The move to agents introduces new complexity in observability, reliability, and system design.
- Updated architectural patterns and practices are needed for agentic distributed systems.
