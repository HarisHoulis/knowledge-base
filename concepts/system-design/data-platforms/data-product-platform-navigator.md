# The Data Product Platform Navigator

Martin Fowler introduces a "platform navigator" for organizations adopting data mesh and data products. Many teams face a common challenge: either building a self-service platform for data products from scratch, or dealing with an existing platform that suffers from low adoption and prevents their data mesh from scaling.

The navigator is based on the CNCF Platform Engineering Maturity Model. Its purpose is to help teams reason about their current situation and determine the next steps for their platform, rather than prescribing a single path.

- Organizations adopting data mesh often must build a self-service data product platform from scratch or fix an existing low-adoption platform.
- Low platform adoption blocks data mesh from scaling.
- The navigator is built on the CNCF Platform Engineering Maturity Model.
- It helps teams assess their current situation and decide next steps for their platform.
