# Amazon Acquires DuckLabs and Integrates DuckDB in Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL

Amazon acquired DuckLabs, the company behind the analytical database DuckDB, with co-founders Hannes Mühleisen and Mark Raasveldt continuing to lead the team under AWS. The acquisition is intended to enhance DuckDB's development and reach while preserving its open-source status.

Following the acquisition, Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL received its first DuckDB-powered feature, bringing analytical database capabilities into the managed PostgreSQL service.

- Amazon acquired DuckLabs, the company behind DuckDB
- DuckDB co-founders Hannes Mühleisen and Mark Raasveldt will lead the team under AWS
- DuckDB is to remain open source
- Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL gets its first DuckDB-powered feature
