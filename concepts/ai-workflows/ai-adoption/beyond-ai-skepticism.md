# Stop being skeptical about AI for development with Charity Majors

The article chronicles Charity Majors' evolution from AI skeptic to proponent, arguing that while skepticism was rational in 2025, by 2026 AI is clearly a generational shift for software engineering. Majors identifies November 2025 as her turning point, driven by the Claude Code harness and Opus 4.5, and compares the impact to cloud computing in 2010 (Orosz, 2026). She emphasizes that the key challenge is shipping code that humans haven't read, requiring a shift from hand-crafted 'pet' code to disposable 'cattle' code verified through tests, observability, and conformance checks (Orosz, 2026).

- Skepticism about AI in 2025 was justified, but 2026 marks a clear inflection point (Orosz, 2026).
- Majors' turning point came in November 2025, driven by the Claude Code harness and Opus 4.5 (Orosz, 2026).
- AI code may become 'cattle' rather than 'pets': regenerated and verified rather than hand-maintained (Orosz, 2026).
- The bottleneck shifts from code generation to verification, requiring more engineering discipline, not less (Orosz, 2026).
- Career advice: gain AI experience now, or risk being filtered out in future interviews (Orosz, 2026).
