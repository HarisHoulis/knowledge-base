# AI-Assisted Software Delivery: From Code to Production

This InfoQ panel discussion brings together Garima Bajpaj, Martin Reynolds, Patrick Debois, Gunnar Grosch, and Renato Losio to examine how engineering teams must adapt delivery architectures, pipelines, security, and governance for AI-assisted development. Rather than focusing on code generation alone, the panel frames AI assistance as a full delivery-pipeline concern spanning from authoring through production.

The panelists share practical strategies for verifying AI-generated code and for addressing the testing and deployment bottlenecks that AI-assisted workflows can expose or amplify. They emphasize maintaining human oversight throughout the process rather than treating AI output as automatically trustworthy.

A recurring theme is measurement: the panel discusses how teams can assess AI's true impact on software velocity, quality, reliability, and operational cost, moving beyond anecdotal productivity claims toward concrete delivery metrics.

- AI-assisted development requires adapting delivery architectures, pipelines, security, and governance, not just coding tools
- Teams need practical strategies for verifying AI-generated code before it reaches production
- AI adoption surfaces testing and deployment bottlenecks that must be solved alongside code generation
- Human oversight remains essential in AI-assisted delivery workflows
- Impact should be measured across velocity, quality, reliability, and operational cost
