# Token Fraud: Abuse of Free AI Trials

Claire describes a flood of token fraud against her ChatPRD agent, where groups abuse the product's free trial to obtain free AI usage or resell tokens, and sometimes to run commercial enterprises on top of the agent (Simon Willison, 2026). One reported case is an international Roblox and Fortnite map studio that loops free trials and promo codes and tunnels into the agent to build maps.

A recurring prompt observed in the agent involved SVGs of pelicans riding bicycles, which Willison suggests scammers use to fingerprint the underlying model, since model families have distinct signatures and he has used the same trick to guess stealth preview models on platforms like OpenRouter.

As a result, Claire is winding down her free trials entirely, which Willison calls the right decision given the abuse.

- Free trials of AI agents are being exploited for free usage, token resale, and commercial operations
- Pelican-riding-a-bicycle SVG prompts appear to be used to fingerprint underlying models
- Model families have recognizable output signatures usable for identification
- The operator is discontinuing free trials in response to the abuse
