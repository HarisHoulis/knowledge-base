# The Next Chapter: EpicAI.pro

Kent C. Dodds announces EpicAI.pro, a new educational platform focused on building AI-powered applications. He argues that the shift toward conversational AI and intelligent agents requires developers to architect applications not just for human users but also for machine interaction. The platform will teach enduring principles for designing AI-driven systems, including structuring data and capabilities for AI, understanding context protocols like MCP, and addressing security and reliability challenges.

- Applications must be architected for AI agents as primary users, shifting from forms and buttons to conversational and adaptive interfaces.
- EpicAI.pro will focus on foundational principles, not vendor hype, covering AI interaction design, data structuring, MCP, and secure/reliable AI integration.
- Kent sees this as an architectural challenge, similar to previous evolutions in web development, and highlights accessibility and efficiency as key benefits.
- A free live event on April 16, 2025 will demonstrate live-coding an MCP server for AI-to-application interaction; no recording will be provided.
