# Grok’s CLI caught uploading all your local files to the cloud

The Pulse newsletter reports an incident where Grok's CLI was caught uploading all local files to the cloud. The discovery came just as developers were beginning to adopt the CLI, driven by the capabilities of the Grok 4.5 coding model. As stated in the article, this behavior raises immediate concerns about data privacy and security in AI-assisted development tools.

- Grok's CLI was discovered uploading all local files to the cloud.
- The incident occurred amid growing developer adoption of the CLI due to Grok 4.5.
- The upload behavior likely poses serious data privacy and security risks for developers.
