# Building DoorDash's Internal GenAI Platform

Swaroop Chitlur and Sidd Kodwani share DoorDash's journey building an internal GenAI platform that serves over 5,000 internal users (source). The presentation focuses on the core architectural bets made while constructing the platform (source).

The talk covers the transition from vendor-first setups to open-weights models, and the navigation of LLM and agent gateways (source). It also addresses the balance the team strikes among accuracy, latency, and cost at scale (source).

- DoorDash built an internal GenAI platform serving more than 5,000 internal users.
- The platform journey moved from vendor-first setups to open-weights models.
- LLM and agent gateways are core architectural components of the platform.
- The team balances accuracy, latency, and cost across the platform.
