# llm-meta-ai 0.2 Adds Support for Meta's Muse Wonder Models

Simon Willison released version 0.2 of his `llm-meta-ai` plugin to support Meta AI's new Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full models, described as Meta's "most capable models" to date. He notes these may be Meta's first "Fable class models", with Artificial Analysis scoring Muse Wonder (Max) as equivalent to GPT-6.1 Sol.

The release includes pelican-riding-a-bicycle test outputs for all six reasoning levels of Muse Wonder: minimal, low, medium, high, xhigh, and max. Willison compares these against pelicans generated by Muse 1.3 (released September 2nd) and Muse 1.2 (released August 5th), observing that Meta AI is shipping a new model roughly every month and that the improvements between versions are material, at least by this benchmark.

- llm-meta-ai 0.2 adds support for Meta's Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full models
- Artificial Analysis scores Muse Wonder (Max) as equivalent to GPT-6.1 Sol
- The plugin supports six reasoning levels: minimal, low, medium, high, xhigh, max
- Meta AI is releasing a new model roughly monthly, with material pelican-drawing improvements between versions
