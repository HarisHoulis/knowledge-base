# Slow down to speed up: so much has changed in 6 months’ time

In this article, Gergely Orosz summarizes his keynote at Craft Conference, using Meta's recent outage as a cautionary tale. The outage, where Meta AI could change any account's email without authentication, was reportedly caused by AI-generated and AI-reviewed code, combined with layoffs and forced reassignments that gutted security and integrity teams. This incident highlights the dangers of prioritizing AI progress over the health of core revenue-generating products (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's outage illustrates the risks of AI-generated code and understaffed safety teams, showing what happens when AI focus overshadows core product stability.
- Since November 2025, AI agents like Opus 4.5 and GPT-5.4 have become genuinely useful, leading to a six-month explosion in AI-assisted development.
- Data from Cursor and Linear show devs shipping 5x more PRs, producing 2.5x more code, and creating 3x larger PRs, with more changes accepted without human review.
- Company adoption varies: Anthropic is all-in (70-90% code from Claude), OpenAI's Codex team uses AI review and parallel agents, Uber built custom internal infrastructure, and startups and traditional firms are heavily investing.
- Individual productivity is up, but team productivity is flat, and code review overload and slop are emerging as major challenges; engineers should adapt to stay relevant.
