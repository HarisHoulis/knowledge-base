# What Does It Really Mean To Program With AI: A Conversation with Joy Ebertz

In this InfoQ podcast, Michael Stiefel speaks with Joy Ebertz about how artificial intelligence is changing the way architects and developers do their jobs. The conversation centers on the shifting nature of software development work as AI becomes more embedded in the practice.

A key thread is that focusing on the long-term success of the business is one of the most neglected tasks of an architect. Ebertz argues this responsibility becomes particularly important as the nature of artificial intelligence changes the landscape of software development, since architects must weigh AI adoption against durable business outcomes rather than short-term novelty.

- AI is changing how architects and developers do their jobs.
- Focusing on long-term business success is one of the most neglected architect responsibilities.
- That responsibility grows more important as AI reshapes software development.
