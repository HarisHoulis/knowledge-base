# Agents Refactor 300K Lines in Three Weeks, and Practitioners Ask What It Proves

CodeScene published a case study in which coding agents refactored 300,000 lines of C over three weeks for roughly $4,000 in tokens (Wiggers, 2026). The work was verified by a frame-by-frame replay harness, and the agents built a playbook of codebase-specific recipes along the way (Wiggers, 2026).

Practitioners have questioned the scope, the metric, and how much of the result depends on the harness (Wiggers, 2026).

- CodeScene's case study reports coding agents refactoring 300,000 lines of C in three weeks for about $4,000 in tokens.
- The result was verified by a frame-by-frame replay harness.
- The agents built a playbook of codebase-specific recipes during the refactor.
- Practitioners questioned the scope, the metric, and the harness dependency of the result.
