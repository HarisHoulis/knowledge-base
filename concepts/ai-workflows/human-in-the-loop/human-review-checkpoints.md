# Do you even need human review?

Matt Pocock introduces tools like /grill-me and /grill-with-docs that facilitate this process, allowing AI to self-critique or reference documentation before human involvement. This approach reduces unnecessary human intervention while maintaining quality control at the most impactful stages. The overall goal is to balance autonomy with oversight, ensuring that human attention is used where it adds the most value.

- Human review should occur at strategic checkpoints, not at every step.
- Involve humans early for alignment, then let AI execute autonomously.
- Use tools like /grill-me and /grill-with-docs to refine AI output before final human review.
- Final review remains important to catch issues AI might miss.
