# We Tracked a Shipment of Rare Books. It Ended at an Amazon AI Training Facility

404 Media used an Apple AirTag hidden inside a book to track a large order of around 1,000 rare books purchased through the marketplace Biblio. The investigation aimed to uncover which company was behind the bulk purchase, suspected to be an AI training data operation. The AirTag revealed the book was delivered to the VGT3 corner of the LAS8 Amazon facility in northeast Las Vegas, where an on-the-nose dinosaur-with-a-book logo marked the entrance.

- A bookseller received a bulk order of ~1,000 books via Biblio and cooperated with 404 Media by placing an AirTag in one book.
- The tracked book ended up at the VGT3 section of Amazon's LAS8 facility in Las Vegas.
- Online forum discussions among Amazon workers indicated VGT3 destructively scans large volumes of books, suggesting AI training data collection.
- The investigation raises ethical concerns about how tech companies source copyright-protected material for AI training.
