# Anthropic's best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive

Anthropic's annualized revenue reached $65bn in July, up from $47bn in May, and the company expects Q3 to be profitable under the same model used to declare Q2 profitable. It also told investors it has 6,000 customers that spend $100,000 annually or more. These figures come from the FT story, gathered from people with knowledge of the matter, and are cited via Simon Willison's blog.

- Anthropic's annualized revenue grew to $65bn in July, up from $47bn in May.
- Anthropic expects Q3 profitability and reports 6,000 customers spending $100k+ annually.
- OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% to over $40bn following GPT 5.6's launch.
- Ramp AI index (70,000 companies) shows Opus 4.8 at 28% of Anthropic spend, while newer/costlier models like Fable 5 (8.0%) and Opus 5 (3.5%) lag.
- Cost appears to limit adoption of Anthropic's newest models despite their capabilities.
