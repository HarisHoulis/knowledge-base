# Anthropic’s Best AI Model Struggles to Attract Users as Cheaper Tools Thrive

Anthropic’s most advanced AI model, Fable 5, is struggling to gain user traction despite being the company’s flagship offering. Data from the Ramp AI index, which tracks billing data from 70,000 companies, shows that Fable 5 captured only 8.0% of Anthropic’s model spend in July 2026, while the older Opus 4.8 led with 28.0% and Sonnet 4.6 took 8.3%. The article notes that Fable 5’s high cost has made it a less popular choice, especially since it was only released on July 24th (Simon Willison, 2026).

Financially, Anthropic’s annualized revenue reached $65bn in July, up from $47bn in May, and the company expects Q3 to be profitable. It also reported 6,000 customers spending over $100,000 annually. Meanwhile, OpenAI’s annualized revenue has jumped 35% to over $40bn, driven by the launch of GPT 5.6. This comparison highlights a broader trend: while premium models push boundaries, cost-sensitive users often prefer cheaper, established alternatives (Simon Willison, 2026).

- Fable 5, Anthropic’s newest flagship model, saw only 8.0% of Anthropic’s model spend in its launch month, trailing behind Opus 4.8 (28.0%).
- High costs appear to be a major barrier to adoption, pushing users toward cheaper models like Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 4.6.
- Anthropic’s annualized revenue reached $65bn in July 2026, up from $47bn in May, with 6,000 enterprise customers spending over $100k.
- OpenAI’s annualized revenue jumped 35% to $40bn+ after the July launch of GPT 5.6, showing the competitive impact of new model releases.
