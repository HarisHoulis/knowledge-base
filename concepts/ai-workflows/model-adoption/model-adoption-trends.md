# Anthropic’s best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive

Anthropic's annualized revenue reached $65bn in July 2026, up from $47bn in May, according to people familiar with the matter. The company expects Q3 to be profitable under the same model that declared Q2 profitable, and reported 6,000 customers spending $100,000 or more annually. Meanwhile, OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% in the quarter to date, surpassing $40bn, driven by the launch of GPT-5.6 in July.

Data from the Ramp AI index, which analyzes billing data from 70,000 companies using Ramp credit cards, reveals that Anthropic's newest flagship models—Fable 5 and Opus 5—are not the most used. Opus 4.8 leads with 28.0% of Anthropic model spend, followed by Sonnet 4.6 at 8.3% and Fable 5 at 8.0%. Opus 5 accounts for only 3.5% of spend, suggesting that cost-conscious users prefer older, cheaper models over the most capable but expensive ones.

- Anthropic has 6,000 customers spending at least $100,000 annually.
- OpenAI's annualized revenue exceeds $40bn, up 35% quarter-to-date after GPT-5.6 launch.
- Ramp AI index shows Opus 4.8 commands 28% of Anthropic model spend, while flagship Opus 5 only 3.5%.
- Cheaper, older models like Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 4.6 are more widely used than the most capable new models.
