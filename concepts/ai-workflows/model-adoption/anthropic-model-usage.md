# Anthropic’s best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive

A few interesting numbers in this FT story gathered from 'people with knowledge of the matter' indicate that Anthropic's annualized revenue for July reached $65bn, up from $47bn in May. Anthropic expects Q3 to be profitable and told investors it had 6,000 customers spending $100,000 annually or more. OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% in the quarter to date to over $40bn, with GPT 5.6 boosting performance. The Ramp AI index, using billing data from 70,000 companies, breaks down Anthropic model spend for July 2026, showing Opus 4.8 leading at 28%, while newer Fable 5 and Opus 5 were only 8% and 3.5% respectively, supporting the idea that Fable's cost has made it less popular.

- Anthropic's annualized revenue rose to $65bn in July, with Q3 expected to be profitable.
- OpenAI's annualized revenue exceeded $40bn, boosted by GPT 5.6.
- Ramp AI index data shows Opus 4.8 leads Anthropic spend at 28%, while newer Fable 5 and Opus 5 lag at 8% and 3.5%.
- Fable 5's high cost appears to have limited its adoption relative to cheaper older models.
