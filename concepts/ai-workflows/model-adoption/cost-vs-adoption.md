# Anthropic’s best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive

These figures illustrate a broader trend: cheaper, sufficiently capable models tend to dominate usage even when a premium flagship exists. The data underscores that cost-effectiveness and practical performance drive adoption more than headline intelligence, shaping the competitive dynamics of the AI industry.

- Anthropic's annualized revenue grew to $65bn in July 2026, up from $47bn in May.
- Anthropic expects Q3 profitability and counts 6,000 customers spending $100k+ annually.
- OpenAI's revenue surged 35% quarterly to $40bn+ after the GPT 5.6 launch.
- Ramp AI index shows Opus 4.8 leading at 28% of Anthropic spend, while the premium Fable 5 lags at 8%.
- Cost sensitivity drives adoption; cheaper models often outperform flagship offerings in real-world usage.
