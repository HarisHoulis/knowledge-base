# The Investor's Edge Lives in Their Head — Anekha Sokhal, Moshi

Anekha Sokhal, founder and CEO of Moshi, argues that financial investors face a paradox: the world generated roughly 175 zettabytes of data in 2025, yet human attention remains fixed, so more data does not equal more information. Because everyone now has access to the same models, prompts, MCP connectors, and data streams, the real competitive advantage is something that cannot be downloaded — the context and judgment built up in an experienced investor's mind over a decade of working alongside top traders.

- More data does not equal more information; attention is the scarce resource for financial professionals.
- Advantage for investors means knowing what's happening first, understanding second- and third-order consequences, and having confidence to size positions.
- Shared models, prompts, and connectors are commodities — the durable edge is tacit knowledge in the investor's head.
- When experienced investors retire or move firms, that intellectual property leaves with them, motivating AI systems that capture it.
- The human brain runs on ~20 watts, so AI should be placed thoughtfully in the decision-making cycle rather than assumed to replace it.
