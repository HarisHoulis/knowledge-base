# Local Models: Trust, Control, Optimization — Carter Abdallah, NVIDIA

This panel discussion explores the growing importance of local and open models for achieving AI sovereignty and ownership. Vincent (Prime Intellect) emphasizes the goal of making frontier intelligence open and accessible, including the full training stack. Lucas (RCAI) describes how his company pivoted to pre-training large open models, such as a 400-billion-parameter model, in response to the West losing leadership in open models to China, and now works as an open model lab with partners like NVIDIA and Prime Intellect. Chris (NVIDIA) highlights that the Neumotron family of models is focused on being as open as possible, including weights, data, training methodology, and frameworks.

- Open models are essential for local ownership, control, and sovereign AI.
- RCAI reoriented to pre-train a 400B parameter model in 6 months to reclaim Western leadership in open models.
- True openness includes weights, data, training methodology, and frameworks.
- Collaboration between organizations (e.g., NVIDIA, Prime Intellect, RCAI) accelerates open model development.
