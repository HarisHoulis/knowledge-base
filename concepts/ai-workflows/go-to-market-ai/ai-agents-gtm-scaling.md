# How AI Agents Let GTM Teams Scale — Justin Joyce, Cloudflare

Justin Joyce, a principal sales operations and strategy manager at Cloudflare, argues that traditional go-to-market (GTM) operations do not scale due to two core problems. First, back-office teams often rely on manual analysis in spreadsheets, which becomes exponentially time-consuming as projects multiply, or at best produce static dashboards that don't meet all team needs. Second, sales and customer-facing teams face a 'context gap'—the effort required to gather information for each prospect or customer interaction—and an 'expert gap' between top performers and new hires. These gaps create inconsistency in execution and messaging. To address this, Joyce proposes a three-pillar framework, with the first pillar focusing on scaling analysis using AI to reduce tasks from hours to minutes, enabling operations teams to answer data questions directly and build applications with business context.

- Traditional GTM operations struggle with manual, unscalable analysis and static dashboards that fail to meet all team needs.
- Sales teams face a context gap (gathering info between calls) and an expert gap (difference between top performers and new hires), leading to inefficiency and inconsistency.
- A three-pillar framework aims to improve GTM efficiency; the first pillar leverages AI to dramatically speed up data analysis and enable self-service answers.
- AI can reduce analysis tasks from 2 hours to 5 minutes, empowering operations teams to directly query data and build context-rich applications.
