# The Missing Layer: Design Taste in AI Agents

To address this, he built a design skill called Hallmark, which codifies AI slop patterns as 'slop gates' to instruct AI models what to avoid, and provides a set of diverse themes to ensure apps don't all look alike. This approach aims to inject design taste into AI agents directly, making it easier to produce visually distinctive and polished UIs.

- Design and UX are primary reasons AI apps succeed, not just model capabilities.
- AI slop has recognizable patterns: purple gradients, italic headers, scroll-to-explore, all-caps pills, gradient logos, emojis, and padding issues.
- Spending 10–20% extra effort on UI is a huge competitive advantage.
- Hallmark is a design skill that encodes anti-slop patterns and themes for AI models.
- Tools like Hallmark help AI agents produce more varied, polished, and non-generic interfaces.
