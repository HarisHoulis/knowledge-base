# Slow down to speed up: so much has changed in 6 months’ time

In a keynote at Craft Conference, Gergely Orosz highlights how AI agents have fundamentally transformed software engineering over the past six months, using Meta's embarrassing outage as a cautionary tale. The outage occurred because AI-generated and AI-reviewed code was shipped without adequate human oversight, while Meta had gutted security and integrity teams to focus on AI training. This illustrates the danger of prioritizing AI advancement over the health of core revenue-generating products (Orosz, 2026).

- Meta's account-takeover bug was caused by AI-generated, AI-reviewed code and diminished security teams, showing the risks of over-prioritizing AI.
- AI agents released around November 2025 became genuinely useful, leading to 5x more pull requests, 2.5x more code produced, and 3x larger PRs.
- Companies like Anthropic, OpenAI, Uber, and traditional firms are rapidly adopting AI agents, with Anthropic generating 70-90% of code via Claude.
- Trends include rising individual productivity but flat team productivity, more code accepted without human review, and increasing AI slop lowering software quality.
- Engineers and leaders should 'slow down to speed up' by maintaining human oversight and investing in reliable engineering practices alongside AI adoption.
