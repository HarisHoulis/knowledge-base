# The Missing Layer: Design Taste in AI Agents

In this talk, Hassan El Mghari argues that design and UX are the number one reason his AI apps have reached millions of users, despite not being a designer. He emphasizes that many vibe-coded AI apps fall into recognizable 'AI slop' patterns, including purple gradient backgrounds, italicized headers, 'scroll to explore' sections, all-caps pill labels with wide letter spacing, gradient logos, and excessive emoji use. These patterns make AI-generated interfaces look identical and low-effort, and a small 10–20% extra effort on UI can become a major competitive advantage.

- Design and UX are the primary drivers of adoption for AI apps, even for non-designers.
- AI slop patterns like purple gradients, italic headers, and all-caps pills make apps look generic and AI-generated.
- Investing 10–20% extra effort in UI design provides a significant competitive edge.
- Hallmark is a design skill that codifies AI slop patterns to avoid and provides multiple theme options for AI models.
- Building simple, one-page apps with strong design and animations can attract hundreds of thousands of users.
