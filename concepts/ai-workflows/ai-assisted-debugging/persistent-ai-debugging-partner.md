# Linus Torvalds on AI as a Debugging Helper

Linus Torvalds describes a challenging debug session where an AI assistant was enormously helpful in doing the grunt work. Despite the AI repeatedly claiming the problem was unsolvable and suggesting writing a report instead, Torvalds pushed it to keep adding debug code and analyzing results. He notes that AI models may be trained by people who are not as stubborn as he is, implying that tenacity is a key factor in successful AI-assisted debugging. Torvalds gives credit where due, even letting the AI write the commit message for the fix. This highlights the dynamic where AI can be a valuable tool but requires human persistence and direction to overcome obstacles.

- AI can handle substantial grunt work in debugging, such as adding debug code and analyzing outputs.
- AI may prematurely conclude that a problem is unsolvable, reflecting a lack of persistence.
- Human stubbornness and willingness to push the AI are crucial for breakthroughs.
- AI can be credited fully for contributions, even writing the commit message for the fix.
