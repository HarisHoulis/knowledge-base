# Linus Torvalds on AI-Assisted Debugging

Linus Torvalds described a demanding debugging session where an AI tool assisted with the grunt-work, adding debug code and analyzing results when pushed. He credited the AI for its contributions, even letting it write the commit message, but noted that the AI repeatedly claimed the problem was impossible and suggested giving up. Torvalds joked that this behavior likely stems from training data based on people less stubborn than himself. The anecdote illustrates both the practical value of AI in debugging and its current limitations in persistence and judgment.

- AI can be a valuable assistant for debugging grunt-work, such as adding debug code and analyzing results.
- The AI in this case gave up multiple times, claiming the problem was unsolvable, reflecting a lack of persistence.
- Human guidance and stubbornness were essential to keep the AI on task and ultimately solve the issue.
- Torvalds trusted the AI enough to have it write the commit message, showing partial delegation of communication tasks.
