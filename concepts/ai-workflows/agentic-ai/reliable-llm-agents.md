# Building Reliable Agentic AI Systems

The article, from Martin Fowler's work with Bayer, describes the evolution of a pharmaceutical research system from keyword-based search to an intelligent agentic assistant. The system enables researchers to query decades of information buried in PDF reports, answering complex questions and drafting regulatory documents. The key challenge addressed is reliability in LLM-based agents, ensuring that outputs are trustworthy and accurate for high-stakes domains like pharmaceuticals.

- Agentic AI systems can evolve from simpler search tools to handle complex, domain-specific queries.
- Reliability is a critical concern when using LLMs in professional settings like pharmaceutical research.
- Such systems can assist in drafting regulatory documents, demonstrating practical applications beyond question answering.
- The project highlights the importance of grounding LLMs in existing data sources, such as PDF reports.
