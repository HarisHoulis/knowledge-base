# The Rise of CaaS: Context-as-a-Service for Agentic AI

In this talk, Omer Primor of Bright Data argues that the web is evolving from a source of raw data to a source of context for AI agents, giving rise to a new category called Context-as-a-Service (CaaS). He explains that while the web has always been the world's greatest data source, AI agents performing knowledge work require not just data but context—relevant, timely information that can directly inform their actions and conclusions. This shift repositions the web as an ongoing, dynamic input rather than a static dataset, with CaaS emerging as the infrastructure to deliver it [1].

Primor emphasizes the inherent messiness of the web: it is unstructured, constantly changing, and subject to rapid data decay. He cites Bright Data's analysis showing that data freshness varies by domain—social media decays in under a day, while news, finance, and retail lose relevance within 30 days. This means that extracting context from the web cannot be a one-time effort; it requires continuous, real-time retrieval to be useful for agents, making context provisioning a persistent operational challenge [1].

The talk traces the evolution of search from human-centric Google dominance, to LLM integration in chatbots, to the emergence of AI-native search companies like Exa, Parallel, you.com, and Tavili that index the web specifically for agents. This convergence of AI and web data is creating the foundation for CaaS, where companies like Bright Data, which already serve over 20,000 teams and 70% of the world's largest AI labs, are positioning themselves to provide context at scale. Primor concludes that CaaS is the next logical step in the AI-data ecosystem, enabling agents to act on fresh, relevant web-derived information [1].

- The web is transitioning from a data source to a context source, driving the emergence of Context-as-a-Service (CaaS) for agentic AI.
- Web data decays rapidly—across social, news, finance, and retail—so context must be continuously fetched, not snapshotted.
- Search has evolved from human-centric engines to AI-native tools built specifically for agents, blurring the line between search and context delivery.
- Bright Data's scale (50+ billion HTML pages daily) demonstrates the infrastructure needed to power CaaS for thousands of teams and major AI labs.
