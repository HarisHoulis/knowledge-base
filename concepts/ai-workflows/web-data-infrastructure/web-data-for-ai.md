# How Web Data Infrastructure Powers the Next Generation of AI

The talk highlights that while AI models often dominate attention, the infrastructure providing them with fresh, real-time web data is equally critical. Oxylabs builds such infrastructure, enabling companies to extract public web data at scale for AI models, agents, and databases. The industry is shifting from static training data to needing live information, making this infrastructure layer increasingly vital for keeping AI useful.

A case study involves a client request for a video API for AI training, with a two-week deadline and a scale of 5 petabytes per month. The team built a scraper, then iteratively added transcript, subtitle, and metadata support based on client feedback, transforming a single feature into a full product suite. The story illustrates that innovation often emerges from repeated adaptation under pressure rather than a neat roadmap.

The talk also emphasizes that AI infrastructure is becoming more multimodal, requiring pipelines for video, metadata, transcripts, and subtitles. The speaker's engineering and product management background provides a practical view of how such infrastructure evolves to meet real-world AI workloads.

- Web data infrastructure is essential for supplying fresh, real-time data to AI models and agents.
- The shift toward multimodal AI requires complex data pipelines beyond text, including video, metadata, and subtitles.
- A two-week deadline for a video API evolved into a full product suite through iterative client feedback and adaptation.
- Innovation often comes from repeated adaptation under high pressure, not from a planned roadmap.
- The project scaled to 30 petabytes of data, demonstrating the massive data needs of AI training workloads.
