# While my guitar gently speaks — Todd Fisher, Philo Ventures

The talk concluded with a live demonstration where an audience member asked the guitar a question and it answered. Fisher also shared his broader philosophy: go build your passion project, even if it seems impractical. He declined to play the song his title alludes to, citing that the recording would be posted online (Todd Fisher, 2026).

- A speaking guitar was built as a JUCE plugin that integrates with a DAW, converting speech recognition output into synthesized speech emitted through the guitar.
- Word segmentation is difficult because running speech lacks clear silences; combining energy gap segmentation with a sonority peak syllabifier improved results.
- Singing synthesis uses the YIN algorithm for pitch detection, envelope shaping, and a vocoder, with precomputed pitch-shifted vocal samples to reduce live processing load.
- The project evolved from a simple Halloween experiment into a full creative tool, demonstrating the value of pursuing passion projects.
- Peter Frampton's talk box is cited as an inspirational predecessor, but Fisher's approach is fully digital and software-based.
