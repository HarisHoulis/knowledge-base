# Quoting Matt Webb

After releasing version 1.0 of Galactic Compass, Matt Webb needed to implement rotations. Rather than having ChatGPT write the code, he used it as a patient, interactive tutor to teach him quaternions. This approach succeeded where reading books and asking mathematician friends had failed, enabling him to learn just enough to make the app work (Matt Webb, 2026).

Webb reflects that outsourcing thinking to AI does not stop learning; it actually pushes him to learn more. He welcomes this outcome, suggesting that AI can serve as a catalyst for acquiring new skills rather than a replacement for them. The quote highlights a positive, educational use case for large language models in software development workflows.

- Used ChatGPT as an interactive tutor, not as a code generator.
- Learned quaternions sufficiently to complete the app's rotation feature.
- AI-assisted learning can be more effective than traditional methods like reading books.
- Outsourcing thinking to AI can motivate further learning.
