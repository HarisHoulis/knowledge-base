# Learning with AI: Matt Webb on AI as an Interactive Tutor

In a quote shared via Simon Willison's blog, Matt Webb describes how he used ChatGPT not to write code for him, but to educate him about quaternions for his app Galactic Compass 2. Despite having previously tried books and asking mathematician friends, Webb found that a patient, interactive AI tutor enabled him to learn just enough to implement the feature himself. He emphasizes that outsourcing thinking to AI did not stop his learning; rather, it pushed him to learn more. This highlights a positive outcome of using generative AI as a personalized educational tool.

- AI can serve as a patient, interactive tutor, explaining concepts in a way that works for the individual.
- Using AI for education can complement or replace traditional learning methods like books or consulting experts.
- Outsourcing thinking to AI does not necessarily hinder learning—it can motivate and enable further learning.
- Practical application (making the app work) was the driver for Webb's successful learning experience.
