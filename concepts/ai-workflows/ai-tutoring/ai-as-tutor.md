# Matt Webb on Using AI as an Interactive Tutor

In a quote shared by Simon Willison, Matt Webb describes how, after releasing version 1.0 of his app Galactic Compass, he needed to implement rotations. Rather than asking ChatGPT to write the code, he used it as a patient, interactive tutor to learn about quaternions. Webb notes that this approach succeeded where reading books and consulting mathematician friends had failed, allowing him to understand the concept just enough to make the app work (Matt Webb, quoted on Simon Willison's blog, 2026).

The experience leads Webb to reflect on the relationship between AI and learning. He observes that outsourcing thinking to AI does not stop learning; instead, it pushes him to learn more. This highlights a shift in how AI can be integrated into problem-solving: as an educational collaborator that fills gaps in understanding and enables users to tackle unfamiliar domains (Simon Willison, 2026).

Webb's example illustrates a broader trend in AI workflows, where tools like ChatGPT are used not only to generate output but to empower individuals to acquire new skills. This case suggests that AI can lower barriers to complex topics and foster deeper engagement, rather than creating dependency or reducing human expertise.

- Matt Webb used ChatGPT as an interactive tutor to learn quaternions for his app, instead of having it write the code directly.
- He succeeded despite previous attempts with books and mathematician friends, showing AI's effectiveness as a personalized educator.
- Webb states that outsourcing thinking to AI actually pushes him to learn more, countering the idea that AI reduces learning.
- This example demonstrates AI's potential to catalyze learning and help developers tackle unfamiliar technical challenges.
