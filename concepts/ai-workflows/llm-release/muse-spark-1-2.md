# Introducing Muse Code and Muse Spark 1.2

An interesting pricing twist: the model is offered as two model IDs. The standard muse-spark-1.2 is priced at $1.25 per million input tokens and $4.25 per million output tokens, close to Gemini 3.6 Flash. But if users agree to let Meta use their data to improve products, they can use muse-spark-1.2-contributor at $0.10/$0.20 per million tokens, a huge discount closer to GPT-5.6 Luna and Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite. The author also notes that the Muse Spark 1.2 pelican SVG is a small but material improvement over the 1.1 version. (Source: same post.)

- Muse Spark 1.2 focuses on coding: improved code generation, complex debugging, codebase understanding, and end-to-end developer workflows.
- Co-trained with Muse Code, with training optimizations for harness compatibility and long-horizon tasks like whole-repository generation.
- Two pricing tiers: standard muse-spark-1.2 at $1.25/$4.25 per million tokens, and a heavily discounted muse-spark-1.2-contributor at $0.10/$0.20 for users who allow data usage.
- The author added the new prices to llm-prices.com and observed a small but material improvement in the generated pelican SVG.
