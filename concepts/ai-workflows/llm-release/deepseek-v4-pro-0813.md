# DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (on OpenRouter)

DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 has been released on OpenRouter, with open weights subsequently made available on Hugging Face at 1.7 trillion parameters and 893 GB. The article notes that while the open-weights release was initially unconfirmed, it was later confirmed via a Hugging Face link. This continues DeepSeek's pattern of releasing open-weight models, following April's V4 Pro and July's V4 Flash-0731.

A notable observation is that the model produced 'very different looking pelicans' across low, medium, and high reasoning levels, a variation the author has not noticed from any other model. Examples were provided via a Markdown SVG renderer.

Benchmark results, as far as the author could tell, were originally released to the Official DeepSeek WeChat Group, then copied to Reddit, deleted by moderators for being 'low-effort', and finally posted as an ASCII-art table on Hacker News. The article links to these community sources for further reference.

- DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 is available on OpenRouter and its open weights (1.7T params, 893 GB) are on Hugging Face.
- The model shows significant variation in output across reasoning levels, as demonstrated by the pelican image test.
- Benchmarks were circulated via WeChat, Reddit (where the post was removed), and Hacker News.
- The release follows DeepSeek's pattern of publishing open-weight models.
