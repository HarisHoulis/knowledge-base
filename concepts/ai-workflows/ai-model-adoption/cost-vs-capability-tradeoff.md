# Anthropic's Best AI Model Struggles to Attract Users as Cheaper Tools Thrive

An FT report cited by Simon Willison reveals that Anthropic’s annualized revenue reached $65 billion in July, up from $47 billion in May, while the company expects Q3 profitability and counts 6,000 customers spending $100,000 or more annually (Willison, 2026). Despite this growth, Anthropic’s newest and most advanced model, Fable 5, has seen limited adoption compared to cheaper alternatives. Data from the Ramp AI Index, which tracks billing data from 70,000 companies, shows Fable 5 captured only 8.0% of Anthropic model spend in July 2026, while the older Opus 4.8 led with 28.0% (Ramp, 2026). This suggests cost remains a major barrier to adopting frontier models.

- Anthropic's annualized revenue hit $65B in July 2026, up from $47B in May, with Q3 expected to be profitable.
- Ramp AI Index data shows Opus 4.8 (28%) far outpacing the newer Fable 5 (8%) in spending share for July 2026.
- OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% to over $40B after the launch of GPT 5.6, highlighting competitive pressure.
- The adoption gap between older cheaper models and the newest frontier model underscores cost sensitivity in AI purchasing decisions.
