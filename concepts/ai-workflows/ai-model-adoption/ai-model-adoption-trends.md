# Anthropic’s best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive

The article reports on Anthropic's strong overall revenue growth—reaching $65bn in annualized revenue for July 2026, up from $47bn in May—and its expectation of profitability in Q3. It also notes 6,000 customers spending $100k or more annually. Meanwhile, OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% quarter-to-date to over $40bn, driven by GPT 5.6. However, adoption data from the Ramp AI index, based on billing data from 70,000 companies, reveals that Anthropic's newest and most capable models are not leading in spending: Opus 4.8 accounts for 28% of Anthropic model spend, while the newer Fable 5 holds only 8.0% and Opus 5 just 3.5%. This suggests cost sensitivity is steering users toward cheaper, established models.

- Anthropic's July annualized revenue reached $65bn, up from $47bn in May.
- OpenAI's annualized revenue exceeded $40bn, boosted by GPT 5.6.
- Ramp AI index shows Opus 4.8 dominates with 28% of Anthropic spend.
- Fable 5 and Opus 5 have low adoption (8.0% and 3.5%) despite being newer.
- Cost appears to be a major factor in model choice, favoring cheaper models.
