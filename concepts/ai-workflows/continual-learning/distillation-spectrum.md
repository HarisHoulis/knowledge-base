# Bringing Continual Learning into Enterprises — Samuel Denton, Applied Compute

Samuel Denton from Applied Compute presents a framework for bringing continual learning into enterprises, centered on a distillation spectrum. On one end is offline distillation: learning from a single batch of production traces. On the other end is fully online continual learning, where inference and training are combined into a unified engine. Most enterprises today are at the offline end, holding traces and wanting to improve their agent but lacking a continuous update loop (AI Engineer, 2026).

- Distillation spans a spectrum from offline batch learning to fully online unified inference-training.
- Most enterprises currently operate at the offline end of the spectrum.
- Hinting is a separate axis; teacher models require privileged information.
- Hints can be rubrics or behavioral priors, e.g., a customer support agent too willing to give refunds.
- Applied Compute aims to serve enterprises across the entire spectrum.
