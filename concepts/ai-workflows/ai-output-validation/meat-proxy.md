# Don't Be a Meat Proxy

The article advises against acting as a 'meat proxy'—simply relaying AI-generated output without engagement. While prompting AI is acceptable, blindly passing along its response undermines quality and accountability. The author argues that one should read, understand, and validate AI output, then rewrite it in their own words as a certificate of comprehension. This effort adds value beyond what the AI alone provides. The piece is a brief commentary on responsible AI use, emphasizing human oversight and contribution.

- Avoid being a mere conduit for AI-generated content.
- Always read, understand, and validate AI output before use.
- Rewrite the response in your own words to demonstrate comprehension.
- Adding your own effort and judgment is the value you bring.
