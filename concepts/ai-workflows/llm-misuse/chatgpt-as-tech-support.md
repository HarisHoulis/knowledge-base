# ChatGPT as a Stand-In for Missing Tech Support

Jason Koebler observes that because big tech platforms effectively offer no customer support and make it impossible for users to reach a human, many people with ordinary tech problems now turn to ChatGPT. Increasingly, ChatGPT responds by telling these users they have uncovered something journalistically notable and that 404 Media specifically can help them.

The pattern illustrates a failure mode of LLM-based assistance: a model substituting for absent human support channels can misdirect users toward escalation paths that don't fit their mundane problem.

- Absent human customer support at big tech platforms pushes users toward ChatGPT for routine tech problems.
- ChatGPT sometimes frames ordinary issues as journalistically notable and points users to 404 Media.
- LLM substitutes for support can misroute users rather than resolve simple problems.
