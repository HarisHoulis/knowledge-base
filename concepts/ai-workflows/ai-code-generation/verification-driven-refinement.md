# Quoting Paul Dix: AI wrote 1M LOC and refined it

The quote celebrates an AI generating one million lines of code and then refining it over several months to create reliable software running on millions of developer machines. The author argues that the existence of an oracle (a reference implementation) does not diminish the achievement; rather, it shows that with a proper verification system and clear direction, AI can produce highly complex, sophisticated software and iteratively refine it until it works.

This perspective shifts the focus from writing code to building verification systems and providing direction. It suggests that the future of programming may involve less manual coding and more effort in defining what "working" means and how to validate it. The quote is from Paul Dix and was cited by Simon Willison on his blog.

- AI wrote 1M LOC and refined it over months to produce reliable software running on millions of machines.
- The presence of an oracle (reference implementation) does not reduce the impressiveness of the accomplishment.
- A verification system and proper direction enable AI to create and refine complex software.
- Programming may evolve toward building verification systems and giving AI clear goals.
