# Context Language Models: Self-Managing Context to Improve Performance and Reduce Compute Costs

Researchers from Meta, MIT, and the University of Washington introduce Context Language Models (CLMs), an approach in which language models manage and edit their own context instead of relying on predefined mechanisms for summarization, compression, and information retrieval. According to the report, this self-managing context yields substantial gains in both performance and computational efficiency.

- CLMs let language models manage and edit their own context.
- The approach replaces predefined summarization, compression, and retrieval mechanisms.
- Reported benefits include substantial gains in performance and computational efficiency.
- The work comes from researchers at Meta, MIT, and the University of Washington.
