# Making Your Data Ready for Agentic AI

The article highlights the growing excitement among organizations about AI's potential to streamline processes and increase profits, but stresses that AI's effectiveness is entirely dependent on the data it accesses. Many organizations, however, have data foundations that are 'little more than sand'—unreliable and untrusted. To address this, the article references the work of Pramod Sadalage and Prem Chandrasekaran, who focus on building a robust foundation for data that ensures accuracy and trustworthiness. This foundation is critical for the success of agentic AI workflows, as without reliable data, AI systems cannot deliver the expected value. The authors emphasize the need for deliberate data engineering and governance to prepare enterprise data for AI consumption.

- AI capabilities are only as strong as the data they rely on.
- Many organizations have fragile, untrusted data foundations that undermine AI initiatives.
- Building a reliable data foundation is essential for enabling accurate and trusted AI outcomes.
- Sadalage and Chandrasekaran's work provides guidance on preparing data for agentic AI.
