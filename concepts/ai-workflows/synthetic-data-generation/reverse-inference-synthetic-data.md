# Don't be data poor — Anuj Iravane, Anterior

Anterior deals with medical communication that still largely arrives by fax, including scanned bundles that can exceed 300 pages of handwriting, checkboxes, and images. Anuj Iravane calls this an observation through a fuzzy lens over a patient's lifespan. While this data is ideal for evals, contracts forbid retaining it or deriving datasets from it, so no real data survives into datasets. Because 95% accuracy is insufficient in this domain, Anterior needed alternative data sources.

- Anterior generates synthetic datasets by reversing the forward inference task: sample a label and reasoning trace, then construct the unstructured record that would produce it.
- Policies are modeled as decision trees, enabling sampling of a uniform distribution of reasoning traces rather than the narrow variety generated by LLMs.
- A coarse-to-fine pipeline builds patient invariants into provider encounters, then expands into documents, with consistency evals catching contradictions.
- Clinicians own the generation pipeline as skills rather than code; roughly 90% of datasets are synthetic, and blind reviews separated synthetic from real only about 60% of the time.
