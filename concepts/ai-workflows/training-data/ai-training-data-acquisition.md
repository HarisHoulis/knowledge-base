# We Tracked a Shipment of Rare Books. It Ended at an Amazon AI Training Facility

404 Media used an Apple AirTag to track a large order of rare books placed through Biblio. A bookseller who received a roughly 1,000-book order agreed to hide the AirTag inside one of the books, allowing investigators to trace the shipment's final destination. The book was delivered to the VGT3 corner of Amazon's LAS8 facility in northeast Las Vegas, where the building's entrance displayed a logo of a dinosaur holding a book.

- A bookseller received a suspiciously large order of about 1,000 books via Biblio and cooperated with 404 Media by placing an AirTag in one book.
- The tracked book was delivered to Amazon's LAS8 facility in Las Vegas, specifically the VGT3 section.
- Online forum discussions among Amazon workers indicated that VGT3 destructively scans large volumes of books.
- The investigation suggests that Amazon is using rare books for AI training data, consistent with known practices of scanning books for model training.
