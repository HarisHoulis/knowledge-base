# Cloudflare Open Sources Decision Models for AI Agents

Cloudflare announced Clef during its "Birthday Week", a set of open-weight AI models designed to choose between predefined options rather than generate text. The company released 9B- and 27B-parameter models along with a platform for adapting them to specific decision-making tasks.

The models target decision-making scenarios where an agent must select among a fixed set of choices, contrasting with general-purpose text generation. Cloudflare positions Clef as tooling for AI agents that need to make discrete decisions.

- Cloudflare released Clef, open-weight AI models that select between predefined options instead of generating text.
- Two model sizes were published: 9B and 27B parameters.
- A platform accompanies the models for adapting them to specific decision-making tasks.
- The announcement came during Cloudflare's "Birthday Week".
