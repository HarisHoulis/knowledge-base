# AI Evals in Practice: Live Course on Evaluating Production AI Agents

ByteByteGo has partnered with Manjeet Singh, Senior Director at Salesforce, to deliver a live, hands-on course titled "AI Evals in Practice," focused on building reliable evaluation systems for production AI agents. Enrollment closes three days from the announcement date (ByteByteGo, 2026).

The course curriculum centers on designing evals that cover quality, safety, reliability, cost, and latency. It extends into building and validating LLM-as-a-Judge systems, red-teaming agents against prompt injection and jailbreaks, and constructing meaningful eval datasets from both real and synthetic data (ByteByteGo, 2026).

Coverage also includes evaluating tool use, RAG, multi-step execution, and multi-agent handoffs, plus running evals in CI/CD and production to catch regressions and drift. A stated goal is turning observed failures into permanent regression tests (ByteByteGo, 2026).

- Course teaches designing evals across quality, safety, reliability, cost, and latency dimensions.
- Covers LLM-as-a-Judge systems, red-teaming for prompt injection and jailbreaks, and building eval datasets from real and synthetic data.
- Includes evaluating tool use, RAG, multi-step execution, and multi-agent handoffs.
- Emphasizes running evals in CI/CD and production to catch regressions and drift, and converting failures into permanent regression tests.
