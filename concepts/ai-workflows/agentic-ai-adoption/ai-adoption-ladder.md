# What 25 Trillion Tokens Reveal About How Developers Actually Adopt AI Agents

Brendan O'Leary argues that real-world AI adoption depends on building trust rather than chasing model benchmarks. Drawing on 40 trillion tokens of usage data, he frames adoption as a ladder with four rungs and focuses on where teams get stuck as they move up it.

He analyzes key failure points in context construction, multi-model routing, and feedback loops, treating these as the practical obstacles engineering leaders must address to scale agentic workflows. The emphasis throughout is on trust as the driver of adoption, not raw model performance.

- Real-world AI adoption depends on building trust rather than chasing model benchmarks.
- Adoption follows four rungs of an AI adoption ladder.
- Key failure points include context construction, multi-model routing, and feedback loops.
- The findings are drawn from 40 trillion tokens of usage data.
