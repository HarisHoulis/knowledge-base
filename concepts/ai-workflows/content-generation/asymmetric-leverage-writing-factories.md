# I Don't Fear AI Slop. I Fear Untold Stories

Zack Proser, an engineer on the Applied AI team at WorkOS and a 15-year open source developer who has written obsessively since his teens, argues that the real problem with AI-generated content isn't 'AI slop' but the stories that never get told. He notes that many talented colleagues tell themselves 'I'm not a writer' and are held back by fear and shame from sharing impactful work, so he proposes building 'factories of love and grace' for the people you care about and work with regularly.

- AI slop is fixable; the bigger concern is the untold stories of capable people who believe they can't write
- Proser's own writing speed comes from writing constantly—drafting headlines, ideas, and sentences obsessively
- At WorkOS he built 'Blogbot' on Cloudflare, encoding his personal heuristics for good writing into an automated system handed to colleagues
- The result is 'asymmetric leverage': colleagues can publish great work without being writers themselves
