# Making Your Data Ready for Agentic AI

The article emphasizes that while many organizations are excited about AI's potential to streamline processes and increase margins, the effectiveness of AI is fundamentally dependent on the quality of the data it accesses. Often, this data foundation is unstable, and many organizations are unprepared for AI initiatives. Pramod Sadalage and Prem Chandrasekaran argue that building a reliable data foundation is essential, focusing on making data accurate, trusted, and ready for AI use. They advocate for treating data as a product and integrating data management practices directly into AI workflows, ensuring that the data foundation can support the demands of agentic AI systems. Without such intentional preparation, AI-driven processes are likely to underperform or fail to deliver expected benefits.

- AI capabilities are limited by the quality and reliability of underlying data.
- Many organizations have poor data foundations that are not ready for AI.
- Organizations must intentionally prepare data to be accurate, trusted, and suitable for AI applications.
- Treating data as a product and embedding data management into AI workflows are key strategies.
- A solid data foundation is critical for successful agentic AI implementations.
