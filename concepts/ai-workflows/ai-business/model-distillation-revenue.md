# Anthropic reports $16.8bn quarterly revenue, distillation 5-7% of it

Anthropic told investors its quarterly revenue rose nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of a planned IPO, according to an FT report cited by Simon Willison. The company also disclosed that "distillation"—rival AI labs training their models on Anthropic's outputs—accounted for 5% to 7% of revenue, and that it had cut this activity back "materially" in Q3.

Willison notes that if distillation was 5% of Q2 revenue before the cutback, unnamed "other labs" spent over half a billion dollars distilling Anthropic's models in that quarter. He also observes that Anthropic appears to have stopped leaning on "annualized run-rate revenue" in investor communications, a framing it had used previously.

- Anthropic's quarterly revenue rose nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of an IPO.
- Distillation accounted for 5-7% of revenue and was cut back materially in Q3.
- Willison estimates rival labs spent over $500m distilling Anthropic's models in Q2.
- Anthropic no longer appears to use "annualized run-rate revenue" in investor communications.
