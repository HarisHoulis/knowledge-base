# You Can't Trust Your AI Bill: AI Spend and the Software Engineering Cost Crisis

Erik Peterson argues that AI spend is eclipsing cloud bills and triggering a major software engineering cost crisis. The presentation frames this as a trust and accountability problem: organizations cannot reliably verify what they are paying for when it comes to AI usage.

Peterson introduces the concept of "tokenmaxxing" — the tendency to maximize token consumption — alongside unit economics as the lens for understanding AI costs. He also discusses "forced developer promotions," suggesting that AI economics are reshaping developer roles and incentives.

Looking forward, Peterson predicts that by 2028 fully autonomous AI will write, deploy, and govern code, which would further intensify the cost and governance challenges he describes.

- AI spend is eclipsing cloud bills and creating a software engineering cost crisis
- Tokenmaxxing and unit economics are central to understanding AI cost dynamics
- AI economics are driving forced developer promotions
- Peterson predicts fully autonomous AI will write, deploy, and govern code by 2028
