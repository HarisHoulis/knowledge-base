# We Tracked a Shipment of Rare Books. It Ended at an Amazon AI Training Facility

404 Media used an Apple AirTag hidden in a rare book to trace the destination of a bulk order of about 1,000 books placed through Biblio. The tracked book was delivered to the VGT3 section of Amazon's LAS8 facility in Las Vegas, where the entrance features a dinosaur-with-book logo. Online discussions among Amazon workers indicated that VGT3 destructively scans large volumes of books. (404 Media, 2026)

This investigation shows that Amazon is sourcing rare books in large quantities for AI training purposes, likely scanning them to build training datasets. The report highlights a growing practice where AI companies and cloud providers acquire physical books, including rare editions, to expand their language model training data. The use of an AirTag demonstrates a novel investigative technique for revealing the hidden supply chains behind AI data collection. (404 Media, 2026)

- A bookseller received a bulk order of ~1,000 rare books via Biblio, which included an AirTag supplied by 404 Media.
- The tracked book was delivered to the VGT3 section of Amazon's LAS8 facility in Las Vegas.
- Amazon worker forum discussions confirm VGT3 destructively scans large volumes of books.
- The investigation reveals Amazon's involvement in acquiring rare books for AI training data.
- Shows a practical example of investigative journalism using physical tracking devices to uncover AI supply chains.
