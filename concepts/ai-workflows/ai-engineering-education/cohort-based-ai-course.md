# LAST CALL FOR ENROLLMENT: Become an AI Engineer - Cohort 7

The announcement from ByteByteGo promotes the upcoming start of Cohort 7 of 'Becoming an AI Engineer,' a live, cohort-based course developed with best-selling author Ali Aminian. The course begins on Saturday, July 11, and emphasizes a learn-by-doing approach where participants build real-world AI applications rather than merely watching videos. The curriculum is described as structured and systematic, taking learners from fundamentals to advanced topics, with live feedback and mentorship from instructors and peers. The program is community-driven, highlighting that learning in a group is easier than learning alone, and focuses on skill building over passive theory. The ultimate goal is for every participant to gain a strong foundation for building AI systems, making it an ideal starting point for those looking to learn AI from scratch.

- Starts tomorrow, Saturday, July 11, as a live cohort-based course
- Created in collaboration with best-selling author Ali Aminian and published by ByteByteGo
- Emphasizes learning by building real-world AI applications, not just watching videos
- Offers a structured, step-by-step curriculum from fundamentals to advanced topics
- Provides live feedback, mentorship, and a community-driven learning environment
- Focuses on skill building and a strong foundation for building AI systems
