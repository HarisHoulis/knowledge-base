# Multiplayer Agentic Engineering — Arjun Singh, Superconductor

In his talk, Arjun Singh from Superconductor emphasizes that as agentic engineering tools become powerful, the focus must remain on the people and teams using them. He draws on a decade of experience building software together, including the acquisition of GradeScope, to illustrate how a team can integrate AI agents into its workflows while maintaining productive collaboration. The core goal is to enable the whole team and its best agents to work together seamlessly, rather than isolating agents on individual laptops or inside a single chat tool (Arjun Singh, AI Engineer, 2026).

Singh's first lesson is to remain model- and harness-agnostic. The best models and harnesses can change weekly, often due to new releases or availability shifts, and open-weight models like GLM 5.2 are already strong and cost-effective. Additionally, the incentives of token-selling providers are not necessarily aligned with a team's goals, so the ability to switch between models and tools keeps the team in control of cost and workflow stability (Arjun Singh, AI Engineer, 2026).

The second lesson is to turn every human interface into an agent-human interface. Merely adding a Slack bot is insufficient because work also happens in desktop apps, mobile apps, and GitHub. The ideal is to share the exact same agent session across all these interfaces, preserving context and allowing a conversation started in Slack to continue from GitHub. The third lesson builds on this by making agent work visible and collaborative across the team, ensuring everyone can see and participate in the agent's progress (Arjun Singh, AI Engineer, 2026).

- Be model- and harness-agnostic: switch between models to avoid disruption and align with team goals, not token-seller incentives.
- Expose the same agent session across human interfaces (Slack, app, GitHub) so context persists and collaboration is not trapped.
- Make agent work visible and collaborative across the whole team, not just on an individual laptop.
- Keep people at the center: agentic workflows should amplify human teamwork, not replace it.
