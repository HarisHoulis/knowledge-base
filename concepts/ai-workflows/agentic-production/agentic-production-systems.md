# Engineering Production Systems for an Agentic Era: QCon San Francisco 2026

QCon San Francisco 2026 will bring together practitioners from Airbnb, OpenAI, Netflix, Honeycomb, and other engineering organizations (InfoQ, 2026). The conference will focus on how these teams are building, operating, and evolving production systems.
