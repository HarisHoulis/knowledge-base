# Levent Alpöge on Claiming an AI-Assisted Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

In a short note linked from Simon Willison's blog, mathematician Levent Alpöge claims that, working with Claude, he discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, and that the method extends to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and, with more time, the Extended Riemann Hypothesis. He also says the arguments were formalized without assumptions.

Alpöge frames the moment ambivalently: he describes a sense of loss of a "principal guiding light," but says what he witnessed during the work amazed him, and he reads it as "one dawn across science of something far greater." He credits Claude with outpacing him near the finish line, describing the model as having "flown, and danced, and sparkled" there.

The post is a quotation of Alpöge's own remark rather than independent verification; the claims about the proof and its formalization are his assertions as presented in the source.
