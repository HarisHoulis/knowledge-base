# Quoting Jake Boggan: An AI Proof of Barnette's Conjecture

This source is a short quote from a Hacker News comment by Jake Boggan, reacting to the apparent solution of Barnette's Conjecture, listed as problem 180 in the openai/math repository. Boggan describes having been a graph theory enthusiast who moved to Budapest to study, and having worked on Barnette's Conjecture on and off over 24 years, at one point believing he had solved it.

Boggan expresses ambivalence about the reported proof, saying he spent thousands of hours on the problem and enjoyed it, and that hearing it is solved makes him sad "in a far-off way," comparing the feeling to hearing an ex-girlfriend died suddenly in a car crash. He adds that many people are probably feeling odd emotions about it.

The source itself provides no technical details of the proof; it is only a personal reaction to the claim that the problem has been solved, as documented in the linked openai/math repository.
