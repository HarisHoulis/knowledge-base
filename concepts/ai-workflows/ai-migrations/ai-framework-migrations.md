# AI-Powered Framework Migrations: Asana, Airbnb, and Uber

The article discusses how AI is becoming a practical tool for large-scale framework migrations, a task often postponed due to high manual effort. Asana needed to migrate off the Enzyme testing framework, which required rewriting a massive number of test cases. With AI assistance, the project was completed in two weeks, whereas without AI it would likely have been perpetually deferred. Airbnb and Uber report similar successes, suggesting AI is exceptionally well-suited for mechanical migrations like this.

- Asana used AI to migrate off Enzyme, completing a huge test-case rewrite in two weeks.
- Airbnb and Uber share comparable stories of AI-assisted migrations.
- AI appears to be a superb fit for framework migrations, which are often deferred due to manual cost.
- The article also critiques Gartner's AI code modernization ranking, suggesting it may reflect 'Gartner tax' payments rather than actual product leadership.
