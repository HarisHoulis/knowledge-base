# TDD inside the agent loop - theater or actual value?

The article raises the question of whether instructing LLM agents to follow Test-Driven Development (TDD) genuinely improves outcomes or is merely performative. It notes that TDD is strongly favored by Thoughtworks colleagues and is widely recommended in the industry for AI-assisted coding.

To explore this, the author references experiments conducted by Birgitta Böckeler, who was curious about the real impact of TDD in the agent loop. These experiments are presented as a way to move beyond advocacy and examine evidence.

The piece thus serves as an inquiry into the practical value of TDD within AI agent workflows, emphasizing the need for empirical validation of common AI development practices.

- TDD is a common prescription for LLM agents, but its true value is questionable.
- Birgitta Böckeler conducted experiments to test whether TDD in the agent loop makes a real difference.
- The article distinguishes between performative adherence and actual benefit.
- The piece encourages evidence-based evaluation of AI-assisted development practices.
