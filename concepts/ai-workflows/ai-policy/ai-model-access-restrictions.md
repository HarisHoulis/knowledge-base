# The Pulse: Big implications of US banning Anthropic’s new model, Fable

According to Orosz in 'The Pulse,' the US government's decision to restrict Anthropic's new model Fable to US citizens only could have significant global consequences. This move may drive other countries and non-US companies toward capable open models, many of which originate from China, thereby increasing China's influence in the AI landscape. The article highlights this as a major strategic shift with potential to reshape international AI adoption.

- US-only restriction on Anthropic's Fable could boost China's open-source AI models globally.
- Meta's follow-up reveals non-engineering teams lost over 10% of staff, further straining Integrity teams.
- SpaceX acquired Cursor, which had just acquired Continue, signaling entry into the AI race against Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Microsoft is reportedly considering replacing OpenAI with DeepSeek, indicating shifting industry alliances.
- Recent research confirms LLMs amplify existing expertise, widening the gap between skilled and unskilled users.
