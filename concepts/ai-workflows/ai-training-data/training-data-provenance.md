# We Tracked a Shipment of Rare Books. It Ended at an Amazon AI Training Facility

404 Media investigated a large bulk order of rare books placed through the Biblio marketplace. A bookseller agreed to include an Apple AirTag in one of the 1,000 books, allowing the outlet to trace the shipment's destination. The book arrived at the VGT3 corner of Amazon's LAS8 facility in northeast Las Vegas, where the entrance features a dinosaur logo holding a book (404 Media via Simon Willison, 2026). Online forum discussions among Amazon workers confirmed that VGT3 destructively scans large volumes of books, indicating the facility is used for AI training data collection. This raises ethical and legal questions about Amazon's sourcing of copyrighted and rare books for AI training without explicit authorization.

- A bookseller received an order of ~1,000 books from Biblio, and 404 Media hid an AirTag in one volume to track its destination.
- The book ended up at Amazon's VGT3 corner of the LAS8 facility in Las Vegas.
- Amazon worker discussions confirm VGT3 destructively scans large volumes of books.
- This suggests Amazon is using rare and used books for AI training data, potentially without author consent.
