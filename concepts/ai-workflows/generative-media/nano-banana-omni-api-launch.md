# SOTA Generative Media Panel — Dumitru Erhan, Shane Gu & Nicole Brichtova, Google DeepMind

In this panel from The AI Engineer, Google DeepMind researchers discuss their recent launches in generative media. Nicole Brichtova highlights NanoBanana 2 light, a fast and inexpensive image model that outperforms the original NanoBanana, delivering approximately three-second latency for ideation and iteration while approaching frontier quality for production outputs. The team also announced the availability of the Gemini Omni Flash APIs, pre-announced at I/O, marking the first time developers can access video generation and editing capabilities, priced comparably to Veo 3 Fast. The discussion emphasizes the practical benefits of low latency and accessible pricing, and includes creative use cases such as using the API to automatically insert sloths and ramen into videos, a popular demo by team member Furer. The panelists also touch on the model's quality improvements and the broader implications for AI-driven content creation workflows.

- NanoBanana 2 light is faster, cheaper, and better than the original NanoBanana, enabling ~3-second latency for iterative image generation and editing.
- The model quality is close to frontier-level, making outputs suitable for both rapid ideation and production-ready use.
- Gemini Omni Flash APIs have been launched for developers, providing video generation and editing at the same price as Veo 3 Fast.
- The APIs unlock automated creative workflows, as demonstrated by viral use cases like adding sloths or ramen to existing video content.
- Team member Furer is highlighted as a key source for innovative use-case ideas, despite remaining anonymous.
