# SOTA Generative Media Panel — Dumitru Erhan, Shane Gu & Nicole Brichtova, Google DeepMind

The panel highlights two recent launches from Google DeepMind's generative media team. First, NanoBanana 2 Light, described as the fastest and cheapest image model in the NanoBanana family, outperforms the original NanoBanana and approaches frontier quality with roughly 3-second latency, making it ideal for rapid ideation and iteration (AI Engineer, 2026). Second, the Gemini Omni Flash APIs, pre-announced at I/O, are now publicly available for developers, enabling video generation and editing at the same price point as Veo 3 Fast (AI Engineer, 2026). The panel also notes creative use cases, such as adding a sloth or cat to videos, which showcase the API's potential for automated editing.

- NanoBanana 2 Light is faster and cheaper than the original NanoBanana, with quality close to frontier models.
- ~3-second latency unlocks new workflows for iterative image generation and editing.
- Gemini Omni Flash APIs are now available for developers, supporting video generation and editing.
- Omni Flash APIs are priced the same as Veo 3 Fast, offering high quality at an accessible price.
- Fun examples like adding animals to videos demonstrate the creative potential of the API.
