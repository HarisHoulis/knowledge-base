# SOTA Generative Media Panel — Dumitru Erhan, Shane Gu & Nicole Brichtova, Google DeepMind

The panel also covers the long-awaited launch of the Gemini Omni Flash APIs, pre-announced at I/O. This marks the first time developers can access Omni's video generation and editing capabilities through APIs, priced at the same level as Veo 3 Fast, offering high-quality results at an accessible cost. The discussion touches on creative use cases demonstrated by Furer, an anonymous team member known for imaginative demos, such as adding a sloth and ramen to existing video content, showcasing the API's potential for automated and playful workflows (AI Engineer, 2026).

- NanoBanana 2 Light delivers fast, low-cost image generation with ~3s latency, surpassing the original NanoBanana.
- Gemini Omni Flash APIs are now available to developers, enabling video generation and editing at competitive pricing.
- The APIs support creative and automated video editing, as shown by demos like the sloth and ramen video.
- These launches aim to lower barriers for ideation and production in generative media.
