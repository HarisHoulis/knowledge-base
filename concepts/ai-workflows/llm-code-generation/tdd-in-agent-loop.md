# TDD inside the agent loop - theater or actual value?

Martin Fowler's article explores the practice of Test-Driven Development (TDD) when instructing AI agents to build software. He notes that his Thoughtworks colleagues are strong TDD advocates, and that the broader industry also recommends telling LLM agents to use TDD. Birgitta Böckeler, a colleague at Thoughtworks, was curious whether this recommendation actually makes a meaningful difference, so she conducted a few experiments to investigate.

The article is part of Fowler's ongoing exploration of generative AI in software development, focusing on the potential value of TDD within the agent-based coding workflow. The experiments aim to provide empirical evidence on whether TDD instructions to agents yield tangible benefits or are merely a form of performative practice.
