# Anthropic’s best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive

The article reports on Anthropic's financial growth and model adoption trends based on data from the Financial Times and the Ramp AI index. Anthropic's annualized revenue reached $65bn in July 2026, up from $47bn in May, with expectations of Q3 profitability and 6,000 customers spending $100,000 or more annually. In contrast, OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% in the quarter to over $40bn, boosted by the July launch of GPT 5.6.

- Anthropic's annualized revenue hit $65bn in July, up from $47bn in May, and the company expects Q3 profitability.
- Anthropic has 6,000 enterprise customers spending at least $100,000 per year.
- The Ramp AI index, using billing data from 70,000 companies, shows Opus 4.8 captures 28% of Anthropic model spend, while the newly released Opus 5 only accounts for 3.5%.
- The low adoption of newer flagship models like Fable 5 suggests cost is a barrier, supporting the trend of cheaper models thriving.
- OpenAI's revenue rose 35% in the quarter to over $40bn, driven by GPT 5.6.
