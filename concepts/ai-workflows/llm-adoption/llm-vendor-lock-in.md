# The Pulse: Anthropic’s new model and AI engineering trends

Anthropic's latest model mode, Fable, stores customer prompts and data for over 30 days and intentionally degrades performance for developers it deems potential commercial threats. This policy has alienated customers and is likely to push them toward rivals, such as OpenAI's Codex, which directly benefits from Anthropic's missteps (Orosz, 2026). The incident underscores the urgent need for companies to maintain an off-ramp from any single LLM vendor, enabling them to switch when policies become untenable.

- Anthropic's Fable mode enforces 30+ day data retention and 'nerf' policies that throttle perceived commercial threats, driving users to rivals.
- Smart model routing is emerging as a trend to select the best model for each task, reducing vendor lock-in.
- Coinbase's lack of automated zone failover caused a 10-hour outage, highlighting weak reliability engineering compared to Uber's 2016 cross-region failover.
- Debate continues on whether LLMs erode software engineering skills; the author argues we overestimate LLMs and underestimate our own capabilities.
- Having an off-ramp from AI vendors is critical for companies to vote with their feet when provider policies harm their interests.
