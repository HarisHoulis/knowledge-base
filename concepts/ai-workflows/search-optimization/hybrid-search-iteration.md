# Implementing Hybrid Semantic + Lexical Search

The article describes the process of improving site search on kentcdodds.com. The author found that semantic search alone was insufficient for good search results. The improvement was carried out through three rounds of iteration using Cursor and GPT-5.4, with each round addressing a gap or oversight in the previous design. The final approach combines semantic search with lexical search, resulting in a hybrid solution that better handles search queries.

- Semantic search alone was not good enough on its own.
- The search improvement involved three distinct rounds of iteration.
- Each iteration with Cursor and GPT-5.4 revealed something the previous design had missed.
- The final solution is a hybrid approach merging semantic and lexical search.
