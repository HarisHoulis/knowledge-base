# Knowledge Systems: The New GTM Stack

Jeffrey Wang, co-founder of Exa, argues that go-to-market (GTM) should be treated as an AI engineering problem, specifically a data problem. He introduces Exa as a search engine for agents that enables AI systems to access web data, powering tools like Cursor and Cognition. The core hypothesis is that GTM requires a live model of the world, combining internal data (customer info, product usage) with external data (companies, people, news) that agents can act upon. Wang emphasizes that both product and distribution are essential for a company's success, and engineers should embrace GTM as a technical challenge rather than an afterthought (Wang, 2026).

- GTM is fundamentally a data problem requiring a live model of the world that agents can act on.
- Exa provides a web access layer for AI agents, exemplifying the infrastructure needed for AI-driven GTM.
- Engineers should treat GTM as an AI engineering problem, leveraging automation to scale efforts.
- Both product quality and distribution (GTM) are necessary; neither alone is sufficient.
- Internal and external data must be integrated for effective target research and customer engagement.
