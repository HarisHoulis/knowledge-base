# The Pulse: We need to talk about migrations with AI

The article highlights how AI is transforming code migrations, using Asana's migration off the Enzyme testing framework as a case study. The project, which involved rewriting massive test cases, was completed in two weeks with AI, whereas without AI it would likely have been postponed indefinitely. Airbnb and Uber share similar success stories, indicating AI's strong fit for framework migrations (Orosz, 2026).

The author notes that AI seems particularly suited for migrations because they are large-scale but mechanical tasks. However, the article also touches on broader industry trends, including a discussion about Gartner's Magic Quadrant ranking AI code modernization tools. Gartner placed traditional vendors like AWS, Microsoft, and IBM above AI-first companies like Anthropic, Cursor, and OpenAI, likely due to the 'Gartner tax' that traditional vendors pay, while AI startups refuse (Orosz, 2026).

Additionally, the article mentions a 'great engineering leader career break' and an industry pulse with news like GitHub outage, Slack Code, and more. The core message is that AI is becoming an essential tool for tackling long-delayed technical debt through migrations (Orosz, 2026).

- Asana completed migration off Enzyme in two weeks using AI; without AI it would have been postponed indefinitely.
- Airbnb and Uber share similar successful AI-assisted migration stories.
- AI appears to be a superb fit for framework migrations, which are large-scale but mechanical.
- Gartner's Magic Quadrant ranking may be influenced by vendor payments, as AI startups refuse to pay the 'Gartner tax.'
