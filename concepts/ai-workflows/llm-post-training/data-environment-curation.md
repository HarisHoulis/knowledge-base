# Data and Environment Curation for Post-Training LLMs

Mahesh Sathiamoorthy, co-founder and CEO of Bespoke Labs, presents a talk on data and environment curation for post-training LLMs. He describes Bespoke Labs as an applied data research lab helping enterprises and frontier labs access high-quality data and RL environments for post-training needs. He highlights their open-source projects, including the Curator tool for synthetic data curation, Bespoke Stratos (leading to Open Thoughts), and contributions to Terminal Bench. The talk emphasizes that while compute and training infrastructure are increasingly well-defined, data and RL environments remain the primary bottleneck in post-training efforts (Sathiamoorthy, 2026).

Building on this, Sathiamoorthy explains how AI evaluation has shifted from knowledge-based benchmarks to agentic benchmarks focused on doing tasks. He argues that the ultimate goal is autonomous agents capable of operating for hours, days, or weeks, and that reliability is the key blocker to achieving this. Post-training, particularly reinforcement learning, is a powerful lever to improve agent reliability. As such, investing in data and RL environment quality is essential for both enterprises and frontier labs. Sathiamoorthy also stresses the importance of researchers and data creators working closely together to understand what actually moves model metrics (Sathiamoorthy, 2026).

- Bespoke Labs builds data curation tools and RL environments for post-training, including open-source projects like Curator and Open Thoughts.
- The field has shifted from evaluating what models know to evaluating what agents can do, with autonomy and reliability as central goals.
- Data and RL environment quality, not compute, is the main bottleneck in post-training LLMs.
- Post-training, especially reinforcement learning, is a powerful method for improving agent reliability over long durations.
