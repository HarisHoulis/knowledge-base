# Can AI Understand a Distressed Credit?

Harneet Kaur, founder and CEO of Axim Intelligence, describes building an "intelligent layer for investing in distressed loans" and asks how far AI can go in understanding problem credit. She defines a problem loan as corporate debt under pressure from cyclical downturns, operational shocks like product recalls, or structural shifts such as AI's impact on software companies, and notes that outcomes range from recovery to bankruptcy, liability management, or out-of-court restructuring.

- Distressed credit analysis is traditionally manual and hard to scale, requiring financial statement review, lender analysis, and loan documentation reading
- Static tools like Altman's Z-score struggle in opaque, fast-changing markets such as private lending
- The speaker frames true credit understanding as predicting future outcomes and producing a probability distribution over paths
- She uses iHeartMedia (2018 bankruptcy, 2024 stress) as a real-time case study with no known ground truth
