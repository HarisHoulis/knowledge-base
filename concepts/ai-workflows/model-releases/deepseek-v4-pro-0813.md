# DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (on OpenRouter)

DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 was released on OpenRouter. The author notes that while open weights were not initially confirmed, they subsequently became available on Hugging Face, with 1.7T parameters and a size of 893 GB. This follows a pattern of previous open-weight releases for DeepSeek V4 Pro and V4 Flash.

- DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 is available on OpenRouter.
- Open weights were eventually released on Hugging Face (1.7T parameters, 893 GB).
- The author observed notably different pelican images across low, medium, and high reasoning levels, a difference not seen with other models.
- Benchmark details were circulated via unofficial channels: posted in a DeepSeek WeChat group, copied to Reddit (where removed), then reposted as an ASCII table on Hacker News.
