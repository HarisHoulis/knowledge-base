# Your Company Should Train Its Own AI Models

Will Brown, who leads applied research at Prime Intellect, argues that the next stage of AI adoption moves beyond agents and chatbots to the organizational level: what it means for a large enterprise to be truly AI-native, with agents deeply integrated across all lifecycles of the company. He frames this as the next step along the path from chatbots to reasoners to agents, asking what companies of the future will look like and what needs to be built along the way.

- Organizations hold vast tacit knowledge—informal decision paths and unwritten workflows—that agents are well suited to surface from unstructured text
- Agents can spot inefficiencies and duplicated solutions across teams that no individual employee can see or openly raise
- The goal is continuous organizational learning, not one-off analysis: agents should improve both themselves and the organization
- Current agent learning is primitive, relying on context learning such as skills files rather than learning on the job like human colleagues
- Prime Intellect builds open-source infrastructure for training, deploying, and continuously improving models on an organization's own data and tools
