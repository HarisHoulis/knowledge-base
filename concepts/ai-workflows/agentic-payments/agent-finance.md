# Why AI Agents Will Need Their Own Bank Accounts

Lincoln Murr, who leads AI product direction at Coinbase, argues that "agent finance"—agents that can manage money or access a financial account—is already real, splitting into two use cases: agents that spend money and agents that multiply it. Coinbase's first experiment, Agent Kit, bolted a crypto wallet onto a LangChain agent before MCP or standardized AI tooling existed; it worked, but revealed a core problem: agents had money but nothing to spend it on.

- Agent finance splits into agents that spend money and agents that multiply it; spending is the more mature category.
- Coinbase's early Agent Kit gave LangChain agents a wallet, but lacked a payments ecosystem for them to transact against.
- HTTP 402 "Payment Required" was reserved for future use for decades; X402 revives it as a native machine-to-machine payment standard built on stablecoins.
- X402 adoption exceeded expectations: a 25,000-transaction year-end goal became that volume every 17 seconds, with PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, and Amex interoperating.
- Bot traffic has surpassed human traffic, breaking the ad-and-attention model publishers relied on to monetize content.
