# The Tokenpocalypse: Companies Scramble to Reduce AI Token Spending

The article highlights the growing financial strain of AI token consumption, referred to as the 'tokenpocalypse,' and how companies are urgently seeking to cut costs. Accenture's agentic AI strategy lead, Justice Kwak, reveals that internal data shows non-engineers, rather than engineers, are the primary drivers of token usage. Stuart Henderson identifies the conversion of PDFs to markdown as a major token-consuming activity, which Kwak confirms based on Accenture's own metrics. The authors suggest that recognizing PDFs as an inefficient medium for information could lead to broader cost reductions across the business world.

- Token consumption is becoming a critical cost issue for AI-driven companies.
- Accenture's data indicates that non-engineers are responsible for most token usage.
- Converting PDFs to markdown is specifically called out as a significant token 'chewer'.
- The article implies that moving away from PDFs as a communication format could reduce AI expenses.
