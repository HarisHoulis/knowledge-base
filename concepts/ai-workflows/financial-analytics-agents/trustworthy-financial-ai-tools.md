# Stop Chatting With Spreadsheets: Building Trustworthy Financial Analytics Tools

Francesco Galletta and Felippe Felisola Caso, both data practitioners at Monzo (previously Nubank and other fintechs), argue that it is time to stop communicating with spreadsheets and instead build financial analytics tools that users can trust. Their talk frames the goal around trust rather than raw code output, noting that the era of spending hours at the keyboard to produce something of uncertain reliability is passing.

They describe finance as a specific domain: it involves numbers, strategy (which is sometimes absent), and the personal knowledge or misconceptions a user holds about their own financial situation. Financial analytics work spans research for investment firms and banks as well as personal finance cases, and they see a gap in tooling that helps people master their finances and conduct analysis quickly.

At Monzo, their team implements AI with a focus on real results rather than maximizing token counts or spending money that does not bring specific business value. They contrast this balanced approach with the pattern of creating a client instance and plugging it into everything, favoring instead the question of what in one's life can be simplified with AI. They also mention a separate session on 'Agent-ship' covering Monzo's remote agents.

- The talk's stated goal is trust: moving past the stage where hours of keyboard work produce output of uncertain reliability.
- Finance is treated as a distinct domain combining numbers, strategy, and users' personal knowledge or misconceptions.
- Monzo's AI implementation is balanced, prioritizing business value over maximizing tokens or spend.
- They reject the plug-AI-into-everything approach in favor of asking what can genuinely be simplified.
