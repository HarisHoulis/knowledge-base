# The Pulse: tech companies move to open AI models

This article from The Pulse series highlights a growing trend among tech companies to shift from proprietary AI models to open models. The newsletter reports that this movement is driven by significant cost savings on AI-related expenditures, as companies seek more economical alternatives without sacrificing performance. The source article explicitly names Uber, Pinterest, Stripe, Coinbase, Ramp, and AT&T as adopters of this strategy. These companies are dropping proprietary models in favor of open models, leveraging a technique called smart model routing to optimize their AI workloads and reduce expenses.
