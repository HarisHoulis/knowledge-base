# 2025 in Review

In his annual review, Kent C. Dodds reflects on a transformative year marked by a professional pivot from EpicReact/EpicWeb toward EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). He describes how a conversation with Ryan Florence about the future of interaction—envisioning a 'Jarvis' assistant that can coordinate across services—shifted his focus from building websites to building AI-driven workflows. The core idea is that instead of adding a chatbot to an app, developers should add their app to a chatbot, making MCP the standard protocol for this integration. This vision led to the launch of EpicAI.pro and numerous workshops, confirming his belief that 2026 will see an explosion of MCP-powered apps.

- Pivoted from EpicReact/EpicWeb to EpicAI and MCP, shifting the paradigm from 'add a chatbot to your app' to 'add your app to the chatbot'.
- Launched EpicAI.pro and Ran MCP workshops, while improving the Epic Workshop App with a new interactive CLI experience.
- Epic Stack updated with React Router v7, passkeys, Tigris image storage, Tailwind v4, and other modernizations.
- Had to cancel Epic Web Conf 2025 and Epic Web Camp due to financial losses and the need to focus, which was heartbreaking.
- Traveled 58,698 miles, delivered talks on MCP and React Router, and welcomed a sixth child; plans for 2026 include a TypeScript course for a local college.
