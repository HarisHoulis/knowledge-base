# Keyword Search Is Dying. Is Your Catalog Ready for AI Agents? — PayPal

PayPal's Nixon Dean, Director of Agent Commerce Products, argues commerce is moving from an era of keyword search to an "Age of Intention" and then an era of delegation (source). In the search era, buyers bore the burden of finding products through keywords, directory navigation, and SEO; in the intention era, users express needs in natural language—e.g., "my daughter is starting fifth grade"—and AI adapts, making brands come to the shopper rather than the shopper searching for them (source). As trust in agent recommendations grows, PayPal expects consumers to delegate more shopping and payment tasks to agents (source).

PayPal cites market urgency: Bain & Co predicts 15% of all trade will be carried out by agents by 2030, and within a decade internet agents may outnumber people (source). PayPal projects $1.1 trillion in U.S. retail sales and $3–5 trillion globally through agents, with over 2 billion users expected to use AI for purchases in the next few years (source). Partners reportedly see over 693% of referral traffic from AI engines, 39% of U.S. shoppers already use AI for shopping, and first agent interactions can drive 4x higher checkout conversions because agents access more detailed product information (source).

For sellers, the shift means catalogs and product data must be ready for agent-mediated discovery and checkout, not just SEO (source). Dean says sellers should invest in change alongside customers to succeed in the age of agents (source).
