# DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 on OpenRouter

A notable observation from the article is that the model produced very different pelican images when asked at low, medium, and high reasoning levels—a difference the author had not seen from any other model. The article also discusses how the benchmark results were initially shared in the Official DeepSeek WeChat Group, then copied to a Reddit post that was deleted by moderators for being low-effort, and finally appeared on Hacker News as an ASCII-art table [1].

- DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 is available on OpenRouter with open weights on Hugging Face (1.7T parameters, 893 GB).
- The model shows unexpectedly different outputs across low, medium, and high reasoning levels, as illustrated by pelican images.
- Benchmark results were circulated via unofficial channels: WeChat group → Reddit (deleted) → Hacker News ASCII table.
- Open-weight release follows the precedent set by previous DeepSeek V4 variants.
