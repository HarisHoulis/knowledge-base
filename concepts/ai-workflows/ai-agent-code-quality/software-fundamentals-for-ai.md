# Do Software Fundamentals Still Matter?

In the age of AI, software fundamentals such as code quality, design patterns, and managing software entropy are more critical than ever. AI agents are increasingly used to generate and modify code, but their effectiveness depends heavily on the state of the codebase they operate in. Well-structured, low-entropy codebases make it easier for AI to understand context, make safe changes, and avoid introducing bugs. Conversely, messy or poorly designed code can confuse AI agents, leading to suboptimal output or unintended side effects. The source argues that developers should double down on good engineering practices to create codebases that AI agents can work with efficiently, turning AI into a powerful ally rather than a source of technical debt.

- Software fundamentals like code quality and design patterns remain essential for AI-agent effectiveness.
- AI agents work best in codebases with low entropy and clear structure.
- Good design patterns help AI understand intent and make safe modifications.
- Invest in building 'AI-friendly' codebases to maximize the benefits of AI tools.
