# Why Are You Typing: Dictating to AI Agents

Matt Pocock argues that dictating to AI agents instead of typing is a major productivity shift. Voice input lets you brain-dump your thoughts faster than typing, and the agent is capable of handling messy transcripts and extracting meaning from them.

The core insight is that you don't need a clean, well-structured prompt. Because the agent can parse imperfect speech, you can simply speak your intent and let it do the cleanup work. This removes the friction of composing text and speeds up the overall workflow.

Pocock points viewers to specific tools for this approach and frames the change as a simple shift with outsized impact on how fast you work with agents.

- Dictating to AI agents is faster than typing because you can brain-dump thoughts at speaking speed.
- Agents can handle messy transcripts and extract meaning, so clean input isn't required.
- The shift is simple but can massively speed up your workflow.
- Specific tools are recommended for voice input to agents.
