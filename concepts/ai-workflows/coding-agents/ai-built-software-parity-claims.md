# Developer Behind AI-Built Adobe Clones Says They Are "Not Anywhere Close to Ready"

A developer used Claude Opus 5.5 to build open-source replacements for seven Adobe applications and promoted them on Reddit with a projection of roughly 100% parity within a month, but later told Hacker News the project was not anywhere close to ready. The project's own roadmap places it at 45% ready for real work and estimates 2,300 to 4,100 agent-hours from full parity.

The episode illustrates the gap between optimistic marketing of agent-built software and the actual readiness of the resulting product, with the developer's own assessment contradicting the earlier public projection.
