# Research Acceleration Through Coding Agents at OpenAI

Simon Willison highlights an OpenAI article about how the company's research team uses coding agents, noting that 2026 has seen agentic engineering take off at OpenAI, as illustrated by a chart of AI spend per researcher (Willison, 2026). He observes a significant acceleration in spending starting in late July, speculating that this coincides with internal access to the model later released as GPT-6 Astra (Willison, 2026). The post reflects the broader trend of AI-assisted software development and its impact on research productivity.

- OpenAI research staff use coding agents to accelerate their work.
- Agentic engineering became mainstream at OpenAI during 2026.
- AI spend per researcher jumped sharply in late July 2026.
