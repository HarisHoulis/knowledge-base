# Don't Underestimate the Communication Barrier With AI Agents

Matt Pocock argues that the biggest obstacle to getting better outputs from AI agents isn't the model itself, but the communication barrier between the user and the agent. The core issue is that the agent lacks context about you and about what you actually want, so improving results depends on closing that gap rather than swapping models.

Pocock frames the problem as thinking carefully about what your agent doesn't know about you and your intent. The video is a short teaser pointing viewers to his skills resource at aihero.dev, with links to his Twitter and Discord for ongoing updates.

- The main bottleneck for AI agent output quality is the communication barrier, not the model.
- Consider what the agent doesn't know about you and what you actually want.
- Closing that gap is the path to better agent results.
