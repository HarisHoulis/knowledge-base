# There are no lossless transformations of natural-language text

The article argues that when using LLMs to massage or rewrite natural-language text, there is no such thing as a lossless transformation. Every rephrase or rewrite subtly alters the meaning, and because the AI lacks the author's detailed mental representation of intent, information is inevitably lost. This principle is crucial for anyone integrating AI into their writing workflow. The author stresses that you must stand behind every idea and every sentence in your documents. If a reviewer questions a line, it is unacceptable to dismiss it as AI-generated; doing so confuses readers and wastes their time. Ultimately, sharing AI-assisted writing means taking full ownership of its content, ensuring it genuinely represents your thoughts. The post cites a related piece by Sophie (sophiebits.com) that expands on the same idea of lossless transformation, reinforcing the responsibility of the author when leveraging AI tools.

- Every rewrite or rephrase of natural language is lossy; meaning always shifts.
- LLMs lack the author's full mental model, so AI-driven edits introduce information loss.
- Writers must stand behind every idea and sentence in their final document.
- Attributing lines to AI and disowning them is not acceptable and harms reader trust.
- AI-assisted writing requires the author to take ultimate responsibility for the output.
