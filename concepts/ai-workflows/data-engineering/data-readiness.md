# Making Your Data Ready for Agentic AI

Organizations are optimistic about AI's ability to streamline processes, save money, and improve margins, but the effectiveness of AI is fundamentally tied to the quality of the data it accesses. Many organizations have data foundations described as 'little more than sand,' which undermines AI capabilities. The article, authored by Pramod Sadalage and Prem Chandrasekaran, addresses how to build a reliable foundation of data that is accurate and trusted, focusing on the specific needs of agentic AI. It emphasizes that preparing data is a critical prerequisite for successful AI deployment, rather than an afterthought.

- AI capabilities are only as strong as the underlying data foundation.
- Many organizations lack reliable, trusted data for AI initiatives.
- The article provides guidance on building accurate and trustworthy data foundations for agentic AI.
