# Mastering AI Observability with Braintrust

Doug Guthrie, a solutions engineer at Braintrust, presents a workshop on mastering AI observability. He frames Braintrust as both an evaluation (Evals) platform and an observability platform for AI, arguing the two are like "two sides of one medal" that together enable teams to build high-quality agents and to understand what quality means and how to improve it in a measured way.

The foundation of observability, in his view, is tracing: understanding the steps an agent takes to reach a final result, including the input data for tools and the results they return. As agents grow in complexity they generate a huge amount of data, and the core challenge is isolating useful signal from that noise flow. He notes that without tracing, teams cannot really understand what is happening or where quality is degrading.

Guthrie draws a contrast between the industry's current "passive" observation of data and what Braintrust calls "active observability" — providing tools and basics that yield analytical conclusions rather than forcing users to extract data from platforms and sort it out alone. He says active observability is the way to deliver actionable conclusions that help teams create the highest-quality agents.

He mentions his work talking with engineers at companies like Cloudflare and Dropbox to understand how they build agents and what automation they build on top of such platforms, knowledge that feeds back into Braintrust's product. He notes the workshop uses a repository reachable via a QR code and via googling "DPGuthrie" on GitHub, and invites questions throughout and offers to take further questions at the Braintrust exhibition stand.

- Braintrust is positioned as both an evaluation (Evals) platform and an AI observability platform, which Guthrie describes as two sides of one medal for building high-quality agents.
- Tracing — capturing each step an agent takes, the input data for tools, and tool results — is the foundation of observability; without it, quality degradation cannot be understood.
- Agents generate a huge amount of data, and the central difficulty is isolating useful signal from the noise flow and turning it into conclusions.
- Guthrie distinguishes today's mostly passive data observation from Braintrust's "active observability," which aims to deliver analytical conclusions rather than requiring users to extract and sort data themselves.
