# There are no lossless transformations of natural-language text

Consequently, using LLMs for editing demands a rigorous editorial pass where the author verifies that the text still expresses their own intent. Since the model's transformations are not lossless, every edit must be judged against the author's mental model. The responsibility cannot be delegated; the author must be able to explain and defend each sentence, because ultimately the document is shared under their name.

- Every rewrite or rephrase of natural language changes meaning; no transformation is truly lossless.
- LLMs lack the author's detailed mental representation, so AI-assisted rephrasing risks losing authorial intent.
- Writers must stand behind every idea and sentence in their docs, even if an AI generated or edited it.
- Dismissing AI-generated text with 'AI wrote that' confuses readers and is an unacceptable evasion of responsibility.
- The final document must be a genuine reflection of the author's thoughts, not an unexamined AI output.
