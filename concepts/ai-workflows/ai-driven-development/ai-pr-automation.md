# Forget "read the code," I don't even merge PRs myself

When a change is flagged as medium risk with broad impact—such as altering how Cody runtime exports are bound—Dodds steps in for manual review before merging. His overarching goal is to get humans out of the loop as much as possible while maintaining quality through automated validation and monitoring (Dodds, 2026).

- Reading code is a spectrum, not an absolute; risk tolerance and agentic loop quality determine the need.
- The industry is generating more code than humans can review, making systems review more important than line-by-line reading.
- In Dodds' workflow, AI agents open PRs, iterate with review bots, and auto-merge low-risk changes, then monitor production.
- Medium-risk changes are flagged for human review before merging.
- The goal is to minimize the human bottleneck while maintaining quality via automated checks and production monitoring.
