# Muru: Graham Dumpleton's AI-Assisted Rust Server for Python WSGI/ASGI Apps

Graham Dumpleton is building Muru, a new AI-assisted Rust server intended to offer the best possible tools for running ASGI and WSGI Python applications in both development and production. As with his earlier project wrapture, he is publishing an extraordinarily detailed account of the work as it progresses, treating the write-up as part of his development process; all of the material cited was published within the last three weeks.

In his post "AI as a lifestyle choice," Dumpleton argues that although the AI may be doing a lot of the work on the surface, deciding what isn't good enough yet remains his responsibility. He credits twenty years of getting things wrong with mod_wsgi as useful judgment for that evaluation, and states he is prepared to put in the hard work to keep polishing Muru until it is something he would trust, and something users would trust too.

- Muru is an AI-assisted Rust server aimed at running ASGI and WSGI Python apps in development and production.
- Dumpleton publishes detailed build-in-public accounts as part of his development process, as he did with wrapture.
- He frames AI as doing much of the surface work while human judgment decides what still isn't good enough.
- His mod_wsgi experience informs the quality bar he applies to Muru.
