# Quoting Paul Dix

Dix argues that AI's success in this context underscores its potential when paired with a robust verification system and clear direction. With these components, AI can produce highly complex and sophisticated code, iteratively refining it until it works. The quote emphasizes that the iterative refinement process, guided by verification, is what makes AI-generated software reliable and production-ready.

- AI generated 1 million lines of code, later refined into reliable software used by millions.
- The achievement is impressive even when compared against an oracle.
- A verification system and proper direction are critical for AI to build complex software.
- AI can continuously refine code until it meets reliability standards.
