# The Rise of CaaS: Context-as-a-Service for Agentic AI — Omer Primor, Bright Data

The talk traces the convergence of AI and the web, noting that just a few years ago search was dominated by Google. More recently, LLM chatbots integrated web search, blurring the line between human and agent access. Now, a new breed of AI search companies — like Exa, Parallel, you.com, and Tavily — are indexing the web specifically for agents, bypassing human-facing search entirely. This shift signals a growing market for context-as-a-service (CaaS), where companies provide not just data extraction but curated, fresh, and structured context that agents can reliably use (Primor, 2026).

- The web is the world's greatest source of data, but for AI agents it is increasingly a source of context.
- Web data decays quickly: social relevance fades in under a day, and news/finance/retail data largely loses relevance within 30 days.
- AI search companies are building indexes optimized for agents, not humans, marking a shift away from Google's search dominance.
- Context extraction must be an ongoing process, not a snapshot, due to the web's constant change.
- A new breed of CaaS companies is emerging to provide reliable, up-to-date context for agentic AI.
