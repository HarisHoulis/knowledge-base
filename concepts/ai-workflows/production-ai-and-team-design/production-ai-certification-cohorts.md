# Production AI and Engineering Team Design: InfoQ's November Certification Cohorts

InfoQ previews three online certification cohorts running in November, aimed at practitioners building production AI systems and designing engineering teams around them (Chatziou, 2026). The cohorts cover production AI systems, retrieval and agent design, engineering team flow, AI roles, and measurement. The sessions are facilitated by Adi Polak, Lan Chu, and Olimpiu Pop.

- Three November InfoQ certification cohorts are announced.
- Topics span production AI systems, retrieval and agent design, team flow, AI roles, and measurement.
- Facilitators are Adi Polak, Lan Chu, and Olimpiu Pop.
