# GPT-6 Astra for Developers

Simon Willison's post links to an OpenAI announcement video introducing GPT-6 Astra for developers. The post highlights that Astra offers more attention to detail, better understanding of user prompts, and more sophisticated outputs across a range of tasks. Notably, it excels at building 3D models, with examples including gardens, shipyards, animals, cityscapes, and even Dyson spheres. (Source: [Introducing GPT-6 Astra for developers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOC3DisEOfg))
