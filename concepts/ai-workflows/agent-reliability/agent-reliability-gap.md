# We Trust AI Agents That Break Every Day

Shy Ruparel of Temporal presents findings from a second annual survey of 554 engineers and engineering leaders actively using AI agents, arguing that agent adoption is widespread but production deployment lags badly. Daily agent usage doubled year-over-year from 47.3% to 80.8%, yet only 49.1% of respondents launch agents into production or consider them essential to product launches—a roughly 30% gap between daily use and real deployment. The survey skews experienced: 48.4% have 11+ years in the industry and another third have six to ten years, with respondents spanning engineers, AI/data engineers, platform teams, and IT leaders, mostly from the US and EMEA.

The core tension is trust versus reality. 85.5% of respondents trust agent work results at least partially, and a quarter trust them almost completely, with almost no one expressing complete distrust. Yet 41.1% encounter agent problems daily or more often, and 16.4% hit problems every hour. Ruparel frames this as a mismatch: confidence is high while failures are routine, which is why agents that break constantly are still trusted.

He grounds the stakes in finance, where KPMG-cited figures point to $3 trillion in corporate productivity gains from agent-based AI, 94% of fintechs calling AI key to their business, and 62% of teams losing significant time or revenue to reliability issues. Financial workflows—loan processing, KYC/AML, fraud screening, payments—span many days, systems, and people, so inserting an agent mid-process demands reliability that current tooling does not deliver. The talk promises four gaps in total, with usage-versus-production and trust-versus-reality as the first two.

- Daily agent usage doubled from 47.3% to 80.8% year-over-year, but only 49.1% ship agents to production or treat them as essential—a ~30% gap.
- 85.5% trust agent results at least partially, yet 41.1% see agent problems daily and 16.4% see them hourly.
- Survey respondents are experienced professionals (48.4% with 11+ years), not amateurs.
- Finance workflows (KYC/AML, loan processing, fraud screening) are long-running and multi-system, making reliability failures costly—62% of teams lose time or revenue to them.
