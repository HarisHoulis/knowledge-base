# The Pulse: We need to talk about migrations with AI

The article highlights how AI is becoming a game-changer for large-scale code migrations. Asana successfully migrated off the testing framework Enzyme by using AI to rewrite massive test cases, completing the project in two weeks—work that would otherwise have been postponed indefinitely. Airbnb and Uber report similar successes, suggesting AI is exceptionally well-suited for framework migrations (Orosz, 2026).

The article also discusses the diminishing relevance of Gartner's Magic Quadrant for AI code modernization tools. Traditional vendors like AWS, Microsoft, and IBM are ranked above AI-native companies such as Anthropic, Cursor, and OpenAI, likely because the former pay significant sums to Gartner, while AI labs refuse to pay this 'Gartner tax.' This shift reflects broader industry changes, including a GitHub outage, Slack launching Slack Code, Claude text being watermarked, and Uber open-sourcing its SubmitQueue system (Orosz, 2026).

- AI can dramatically accelerate migration projects, as demonstrated by Asana's completion of an Enzyme migration in two weeks.
- Airbnb and Uber share similar positive experiences, indicating AI is a strong fit for framework migrations.
- Gartner's Magic Quadrant may be losing credibility in the AI space due to paid placements and AI vendors opting out.
- The industry is seeing rapid developments, including GitHub outages, Slack Code, Claude watermarking, and Uber's SubmitQueue open source.
- AI-assisted migrations enable tackling technical debt that would otherwise be deferred indefinitely.
