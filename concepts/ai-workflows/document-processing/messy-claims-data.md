# Insurance Claims Are AI's Messiest Data Problem

Denys Linkov, who leads the AI program at Wisedocs, argues that insurance claims processing is one of the hardest data problems for enterprise AI because the industry never finished its digital transformation. He notes that many companies have not completed cloud migration, fully exploited big data, or built a clean data lake, yet are now under pressure to deploy AI on top of that incomplete foundation. Paper remains a primary processing medium, with claims arriving as stacks of documents sorted by divider cards, which makes cross-analysis of statements extremely difficult.

- Insurance claims data is fragmented and largely paper-based, limiting cross-analysis across documents
- Many enterprises are deploying AI before completing cloud migration and data lake foundations
- Forms vary widely across jurisdictions, e.g. 50 US states typically have 50 different workers' compensation form types
- Fax and paper persist in healthcare and insurance, slowing information exchange
