# 2025 in Review: The Pivot to MCP and the 'Jarvis' Interaction Model

Kent C. Dodds reviews 2025 as a year of major professional pivot, shifting focus from EpicReact/EpicWeb toward EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). He recounts how a conversation with Ryan Florence and an Evan Bacon demo at React Conf 2024 crystallized the idea of "Jarvis"—an interface that coordinates across services rather than a simple chatbot. This led him to conclude that the industry is moving from "add a chatbot to your app" to "add your app to the chatbot" ([source](https://kentcdodds.com/blog/2025-in-review)).

He launched EpicAI.pro in March, ran MCP workshops, and released a self-paced workshop, "Epic MCP: From Scratch to Production." The Epic Workshop App CLI was renamed to `epicshop` and significantly improved, while the Epic Stack received updates including React Router v7, passkeys, Tigris storage, and Tailwind v4. Dodds anticipates an explosion of MCP-powered apps in 2026, largely due to ChatGPT's adoption of MCP Apps ([source](https://kentcdodds.com/blog/2025-in-review)).

However, the year also brought disappointments: Epic Web Conf 2025 lost money and will not be run again for the foreseeable future, and Epic Web Camp 2025 was canceled for the same reasons. He cites the need to avoid splitting focus and losing money. On a personal note, he welcomed his 6th child. Looking ahead to 2026, he is developing an "Intro to TypeScript Programming" course for a local college while retaining full IP ([source](https://kentcdodds.com/blog/2025-in-review)).

- Pivoted from EpicReact/EpicWeb to EpicAI and MCP, driven by the 'Jarvis' vision of an AI assistant that coordinates across services.
- Launched EpicAI.pro and the self-paced workshop 'Epic MCP: From Scratch to Production'; improved the epicshop CLI and Epic Stack.
- Industry shift is from 'add a chatbot to your app' to 'add your app to the chatbot'; expects MCP-powered app explosion in 2026.
- Canceled Epic Web Conf and Epic Web Camp due to financial losses and need for focus, despite their value.
- Welcomed 6th child and plans to build an 'Intro to TypeScript Programming' course for a local college in 2026.
