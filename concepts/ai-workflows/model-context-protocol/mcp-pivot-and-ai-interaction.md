# 2025 in Review

Kent C. Dodds reflects on a year of major professional pivot, moving his primary focus from EpicReact and EpicWeb toward EpicAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The turning point was a conversation with Ryan Florence that crystallized the vision of 'Jarvis' — an AI interface that goes beyond chatbots by coordinating across services and rendering interactive native UI, not just text. This realization led to the launch of EpicAI.pro in March and a series of MCP workshops, culminating in the self-paced course 'Epic MCP: From Scratch to Production' (source: Kent C. Dodds, "2025 in Review").

The article also highlights significant improvements to the Epic Workshop App (epicshop CLI becoming more interactive and cross-platform) and continued advancement of the Epic Stack, including upgrades to React Router v7, passkeys, Tigris image storage, Tailwind v4, and react-router-devtools. However, personal and financial realities brought difficult decisions: Epic Web Conf 2025 lost money and will not be repeated for the foreseeable future, and the planned Epic Web Camp 2025 was cancelled. Despite these setbacks, Dodds expresses optimism about 2026, citing ChatGPT's adoption of MCP Apps as a catalyst for an explosion of MCP-powered applications.

On a personal level, the biggest highlight was welcoming a 6th child, which has significantly shaped his availability and will influence upcoming commitments. Looking forward, Dodds is working with a local college to build an 'Intro to TypeScript Programming' curriculum, retaining full IP rights, which he plans to make publicly available as well.

- 2025 marked a strategic pivot from React-centric content to AI workflows, centered on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and the vision of a 'Jarvis'-like assistant that plugs into multiple services.
- EpicAI.pro launched as a new hub for MCP education, including workshops and a self-paced production-oriented MCP course.
- The Epic Workshop App CLI ('epicshop') received major UX improvements, including interactive workflows and better Windows support.
- The Epic Stack saw ongoing evolution with React Router v7, passkeys, Tigris image storage, Tailwind v4, and react-router-devtools.
- Despite success in content, Epic Web Conf 2025 lost money and Epic Web Camp 2025 was cancelled, leading to a more focused and financially responsible approach going forward.
