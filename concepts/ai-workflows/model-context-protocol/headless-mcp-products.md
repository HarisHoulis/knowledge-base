# Quoting Ben Hylak: Every Product Should Be a Headless MCP

Ben Hylak, quoted by Simon Willison, jokes that every product he uses should be a headless MCP server feeding into an agent, so he never has to think about anything underneath. The punchline is the exception he carves out for his own product, where customers supposedly love the UI and love the company very much — "we're special that way."

The quote captures a common double standard in the AI-agent era: users want every other tool reduced to a machine-readable interface an agent can drive, while product teams still assume their own UI is the differentiator. Willison surfaces it under the model-context-protocol and ai tags, framing MCP as the emerging abstraction layer between products and agents.

- The quote imagines a world where products are reduced to headless MCP servers consumed by agents.
- The humor comes from the author exempting his own product, claiming customers love its UI.
- It highlights a tension between agent-friendly interfaces and UI-centric product identity.
