# Meta's Recipe for Building Agents as "Organizational Second Brains"

Meta describes an approach for designing AI agents that capture the logic and expertise of domain experts, rather than simply storing documents or retrieving relevant information. This system, called an 'organizational second brain', was developed for a specialized compliance domain. Meta argues that the architecture is generalizable to other fields such as security, finance, engineering, and procurement, suggesting a reusable pattern for embedding expert knowledge into agent workflows.

- Agents should capture expert logic and reasoning, not just act as document repositories or retrieval tools.
- The 'organizational second brain' concept was built for a compliance use case, demonstrating domain-specific knowledge capture.
- Meta claims the architecture generalizes across multiple enterprise areas, including security, finance, engineering, and procurement.
