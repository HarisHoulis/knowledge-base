# Rebuilding Office for Multiplayer AI Agents in Finance

Yijia Liang, formerly a VP at Coatue, describes why personal agents have taken off (reliable background execution, proactive behavior, and integration with email/Drive/Calendar) and why those same factors don't transfer directly to institutional finance. In finance, the analog of booking a restaurant table is compressing a four-week data-room-to-IC-materials process into a few days, and the context that matters is not an inbox but data rooms with 15,000 files and petabytes of SharePoint history.

- Personal agents work because of reliable background execution, proactive assistance, and integration with key context systems
- Finance workflows are inherently multi-user, so single-user agent plugins force manual file/email/Teams handoffs
- Compound gives every agent a built-in mailbox so it can join existing email threads as a team member
- Agent email requires authentication and read/write access control because of strict NDA and privacy constraints
