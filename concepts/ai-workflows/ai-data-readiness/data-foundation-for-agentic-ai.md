# Making Your Data Ready for Agentic AI

The article argues that the effectiveness of agentic AI is directly tied to the quality and trustworthiness of the data it accesses. Many organizations are excited about AI's potential to streamline processes and improve margins, but their underlying data foundations are often fragile or unreliable, akin to building on sand. The authors, Pramod Sadalage and Prem Chandrasekaran, emphasize the need for a systematic approach to building a reliable data foundation that ensures accuracy and trust, which is critical for AI systems to deliver value. Without such a foundation, AI initiatives are at risk of producing poor outcomes or failing altogether.

- AI capabilities are fundamentally dependent on the data they can access.
- Many organizations have weak or untrustworthy data foundations that undermine AI efforts.
- Building a reliable, accurate, and trusted data foundation is a prerequisite for successful agentic AI.
- The article emphasizes the importance of data quality and governance in AI readiness.
