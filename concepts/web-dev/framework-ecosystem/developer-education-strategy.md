# How I help you build better websites

Kent C. Dodds announces that he has joined Remix as a Co-Founder and Director of Developer Experience, aligning with his personal mission of 'Helping people make the world a better place through quality software.' He explains that Remix, a React-based JavaScript framework, represents the best way he knows to build websites, and that teaching Remix is now the most valuable use of his time. By joining the Remix team, he gains the ability to shape the official learning materials, which gives him built-in credibility and control over how developers are introduced to Remix. Existing content on EpicReact.dev and TestingJavaScript.com remains available, though he does not plan to update them in the near term.

- Kent C. Dodds is now Co-Founder and Director of Developer Experience at Remix.
- He believes Remix is the best way to build websites and will focus on teaching it to fulfill his mission.
- Joining Remix allows him to influence the framework's official learning resources directly.
- Existing paid and free content remains available, but future updates will focus on Remix.
