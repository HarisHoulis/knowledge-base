# I'm building EpicWeb.dev

In this announcement, Kent C. Dodds shares his plans for EpicWeb.dev, an ambitious educational project to teach full-stack web application development. The platform will be an extensive series of exercise-focused workshops, at least double the size of EpicReact.dev, with the ultimate goal of taking someone from knowing nothing about programming to being able to build and maintain a web application. Dodds emphasizes that it will cover far more concepts while delivering improved content quality and leveraging newer tools so learners can accomplish more in less time.

The core format will be workshops, each structured like a day of teaching (about six hours) and broken into 6-9 exercises. Some workshops will be sequential, while others can be taken independently given the right prerequisites. Throughout the workshops, learners will collaboratively build a single ambitious application. Dodds plans to live stream the development of this app and also bring in paid experts to review and provide feedback, which will be recorded as part of the course. Because developing and recording everything would take too long, he will release the material in chunks, starting with more advanced workshops and working backwards to beginner lessons — the latter of which he plans to offer for free.

Targeted release dates are the first chunk being available in mid-to-late 2023 and the full program completed by the end of 2024. Dodds clarifies that these timetables are plans, not promises, and that he sees charging for beginners' hopes/dreams as undesirable. In the FAQ, he states that content will only cover topics necessary for building a production-scale full-stack app, and he will not present multiple frameworks or optional approaches for the same thing.

- EpicWeb.dev is a planned full-stack web development curriculum presented as a large collection of exercise-focused workshops.
- Workshops will be released in chunks, starting with advanced material and working backward to beginner content; no programming prerequisites will be required once finished.
- Dodds will live stream the app development process and include paid expert reviews as part of the final product.
- First chunk release is estimated for mid-to-late 2023, with the full program planned by the end of 2024.
- The course will cover topics required to build a single production-scale app, without offering alternative frameworks or optional ways of doing things.
