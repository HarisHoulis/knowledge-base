# Launching Epic Web

Kent C. Dodds reflects on the launch of Epic Web, a learning platform for building full-stack web applications. He explains that the original vision of teaching everything he knew was refined to offering a proven path with strong opinions, which led to the creation of the Epic Stack—a simplified note-taking app used as the basis for the curriculum. The process involved extensive live workshops, which were delivered under intense time pressure, followed by recording sessions completed in a short window while also managing expert interviews and speaking engagements. The launch itself was highly successful, driven by a strong 24-hour promotion that boosted early adoption. Looking forward, he plans to expand the platform with additional content and collaborate with other instructors while maintaining a consistent, curated learning experience.

- Epic Web is a full-stack web development learning platform centered on the Epic Stack, a proven application architecture.
- The author shifted from teaching everything he knew to providing a curated path with clear opinions, making those opinions a key value.
- The content creation required an intense schedule: 16-18 hour days for five weeks to prepare workshops, then recording 16 days of material in under a month.
- The launch included a 40% discount plus free access to other courses, creating a massive first-day response and ongoing demand.
- Future plans include new workshop topics and enlisting other instructors, while preserving a consistent teaching methodology and stack.
