# Android Bench 2 Adds Support for Long-Horizon Tasks, Agentic Evaluation, and Continuous Scoring

Google has released Android Bench 2.0, a major update to its benchmark framework for evaluating AI models and agents on Android development tasks (InfoQ, Sergio De Simone). The release introduces three main capabilities: long-horizon tasks (LHTs), agent-based evaluation, and continuous scoring.

These additions are intended to better assess performance on complex, multi-step development tasks, moving beyond simpler evaluation setups. The framework targets AI models and agents working within the Android development domain.

- Android Bench 2.0 is Google's updated benchmark framework for evaluating AI models and agents on Android development tasks.
- It introduces long-horizon tasks (LHTs) to evaluate complex, multi-step development work.
- It adds agent-based evaluation and continuous scoring to better measure performance.
