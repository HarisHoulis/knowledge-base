# Kotlin TDD - Degrading

Part 12 of the Kotlin TDD series explores the Gilded Rose stock control system. Having already implemented the ability to update item quality, this episode focuses on implementing the 'silly' quality update rules that define how items degrade over time. The work is driven by Test Driven Development, building features incrementally through failing tests and minimal code changes. The video is part of a broader educational series on refactoring and Kotlin, and the author references the book 'Java to Kotlin, A Refactoring Guidebook' as a supplementary resource for developers looking to deepen their refactoring skills. The episode highlights the practical application of TDD to handle complex business rules in a maintainable way.

- The series uses Test Driven Development to implement the Gilded Rose system in Kotlin.
- This episode specifically addresses the quality degradation rules for items.
- The approach emphasizes incremental development and refactoring.
- The author recommends 'Java to Kotlin' for further guidance on refactoring strategies.
