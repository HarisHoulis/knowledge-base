# Now in Android #90

According to the Now in Android #90 article, Android is updating its visual identity with a modernized logo and a new 3D Bugdroid, reflecting the brand's open, iterative, and inclusive ethos. The updated branding will begin appearing on devices and across materials starting this year. This change is part of a broader effort to keep Android's look as dynamic as the platform itself.

The article also highlights that ART (Android Runtime) can now be updated independently via Google Play system updates, bringing performance improvements, security fixes, and OpenJDK APIs to hundreds of millions of devices. ART 14, expected soon, will include OpenJDK 17 support and compiler/runtime optimizations. In parallel, Dagger and Hilt v2.48 introduces the alpha release of their KSP processors, a step toward replacing KAPT and speeding up Gradle builds.

Additionally, the article covers updates to Jetpack Compose Accompanist libraries: Pager Indicators and WebView are deprecated, Drawable Painter and Permissions are feature complete, and Adaptive, Test Harness, and Navigation Material remain experimental. It also mentions improved developer guidance for modularization (including test modules and dependency inversion), a new guide on Java versions in Android builds, and a quick guide to Compose animations. Finally, the Now in Android open-source app now uses screenshot tests to verify visual appearance across states, screen sizes, locales, and themes.

- Android announced a brand refresh with a new 3D Bugdroid and updated logo.
- ART is updatable via Google Play system updates; ART 14 brings OpenJDK 17 and optimizations.
- Dagger and Hilt 2.48 released alpha KSP processors to replace KAPT.
- Accompanist library statuses: some deprecated, some feature complete, some experimental.
- Android developer guidance was updated for modularization, Java versions, and Compose animations; Now in Android app uses screenshot tests.
