# Now in Android #90

The article is the 90th edition of the 'Now in Android' series, summarizing recent updates in the Android development ecosystem [1]. It covers Android's new visual identity, including a revamped logo and 3D bugdroid, aimed at reflecting the community's ethos [1]. It also highlights ART 14 updates delivered via Google Play system updates, which bring OpenJDK 17 support and performance optimizations to hundreds of millions of devices [1]. Additionally, Dagger and Hilt v2.48 introduces alpha KSP processors, providing an alternative to KAPT for faster builds [1]. The article also discusses the deprecation and feature-complete status of certain Accompanist libraries, and announces improved official guidance on modularization, Java versions in Android builds, and Compose animations [1]. Finally, it mentions that the Now in Android app now uses screenshot tests to verify visual UI components [1].

- Android is rolling out a new brand identity featuring an updated logo and a 3D bugdroid.
- ART 14, delivered via Google Play system updates, brings OpenJDK 17 support and performance gains to many Android devices.
- Dagger/Hilt 2.48 includes alpha KSP processors to help developers migrate from KAPT.
- Accompanist library statuses are clarified: Pager Indicators and WebView are deprecated, while others are feature-complete or experimental.
- New developer guides for modularization, JDK selection, and Compose animations were published, and the Now in Android app added screenshot testing.
