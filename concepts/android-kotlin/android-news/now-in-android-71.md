# Now in Android #71

Now in Android #71 recaps major announcements from Android Dev Summit 2022. The release includes Jetpack Compose October 22 stable (formerly 1.3), Compose for Wear OS 1.0 stable, and renewed focus on tablets, foldables, and ChromeOS with Android Studio Electric Eel beta featuring resizable/desktop emulators and visual linting. Android 13 platform features such as per-app language preferences and the permission-free Photo picker are also highlighted.

- Jetpack Compose October 22 (formerly 1.3) is stable, and Compose for Wear OS 1.0 is now stable.
- Jetpack updates span Architecture (WorkManager, Room), Performance (Baseline profiles, recomposition tracing), and UI (staggered grids, variable fonts, swipe-to-refresh, lookahead layout).
- Material Design Components 1.7.0 and Compose Material 3 are now stable.
- Kotlin Multiplatform Mobile is in beta, with experimental Jetpack multiplatform previews for Collections and DataStore.
- Pixel 7 is the first 64-bit-only Android phone, and passkeys are coming to Android and Chrome.
