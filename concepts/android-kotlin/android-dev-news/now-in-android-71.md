# Now in Android #71

The article is a roundup of announcements from Android Dev Summit 2022, highlighting the stable release of Jetpack Compose October 22 (formerly 1.3), Compose for Wear OS 1.0, and updated support for large screens, tablets, and foldables with Android Studio Electric Eel. It also mentions Android 13 features like per-app language preferences and the permission-free Photo picker.

Modern Android Development (MAD) updates are covered, including new Jetpack releases focused on architecture, performance, and UI, plus Compose features such as staggered grids, variable fonts, and lookahead layout. Kotlin Multiplatform Mobile is now in beta, and experimental previews of Jetpack multiplatform libraries (Collections and DataStore) are available for feedback.

Other notable topics include stable Material Design Components 1.7.0 and Compose Material 3, a case study of the Now in Android app, effective TextField state management in Compose, the 64-bit-only Pixel 7, CameraX device compatibility improvements, Android Go optimization tools, custom ML stack updates, Play Console delivery performance insights, and passkey support in Android and Chrome.

- Jetpack Compose October 22 release is stable, along with Compose for Wear OS 1.0 and Material Design 3 for Compose.
- Kotlin Multiplatform Mobile is in beta, and an experimental preview of Jetpack multiplatform libraries (Collections, DataStore) was announced.
- Android Dev Summit 2022 showcased Android 13 features, large-screen support, and modern Android development guidance.
- Pixel 7 is the first 64-bit-only Android phone, improving performance and security.
- Passkey support is coming to Android and Chrome as a safer authentication alternative to passwords.
