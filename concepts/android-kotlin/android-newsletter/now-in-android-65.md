# Now in Android #65

Now in Android #65 is a development roundup covering recent Android platform and tooling updates as the official release of Android 13 approaches. The article notes that Android 13 reached Beta 4, its final beta, after achieving Platform Stability in Beta 3, meaning all app-facing APIs and behaviors are final and developers should complete their testing (Now in Android #65, Manuel Vivo). It also celebrates 10 years of Google Play and highlights the release of Android Basics with Compose Unit 3, a course unit focused on lists and Material Design.

- Android 13 Beta 4 is the final beta before official release; Platform Stability was reached at Beta 3, so developers can finalize testing.
- Jetpack Compose 1.2 is now stable, adding downloadable fonts, lazy grids, and tablet/Chrome OS improvements.
- Compose for Wear OS 1.0 is stable and is now the recommended approach for building Wear OS UIs.
- Other AndroidX releases include stable Core Splashscreen and Profile Installer, plus RCs for AppCompat, Compose Compiler, Emoji2, and ShareTarget.
- Notable developer articles cover large-screen readiness, testing Wear OS 3 without a device, Compose in RecyclerView interop, and predictive back gestures.
