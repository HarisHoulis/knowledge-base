# Goodbye Medium: Moving to a Self-Hosted Blog

Kent C. Dodds reflects on 15 years of blogging across Blogger, WordPress, Ghost, Jekyll, and Medium, and explains why he finally left Medium for his own platform at kentcdodds.com/blog (Goodbye Medium). He values convenience but also wants full control over how his content is consumed, the ability to dogfood React, and openness to collaboration through an open-source blog.

A key enabler of the move was owning the custom domain blog.kentcdodds.com, which let him redirect existing links without breaking them. He spent about an hour building kcd-blog-redirector to handle redirects and many more hours migrating old Medium posts, noting that the Medium-to-Markdown import process is imperfect (Goodbye Medium).

He lists remaining work for the new blog, including search, category pages, keyword pages, RSS feed fixes, and automated emails with syntax highlighting; he does not plan to add comments. He also asks readers to help fix import issues by comparing old Medium posts to imported versions and filling out a shared Google Doc (Goodbye Medium).

- Kent left Medium for full control, React dogfooding, and open-source collaboration.
- Owning blog.kentcdodds.com and building kcd-blog-redirector let him move content without breaking shared links.
- The Medium-to-Markdown migration was imperfect, and he solicited community help to fix imported posts.
- Remaining blog work includes search, category/keyword pages, RSS fixes, and email automation; comments are not planned.
- He recommends owning URLs or using a URL shortener to avoid broken links when changing platforms.
