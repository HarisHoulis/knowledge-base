# The Dow's Start on the Covenant Path

Kent C. Dodds shares a deeply personal story about meeting the Dow family at a family camp in August 2025, where a simple act of welcoming led to a lasting friendship. The Dows, feeling out of place, were embraced by Kent and his wife, Brooke, and the connection grew naturally. Kent later reconnected with Jeremiah and Beiyi at Remix Jam, where their conversation turned to faith, leading Kent to share the Book of Mormon and help them find a local congregation. The Dows began attending church, were drawn to the 'good fruit' of the community, and ultimately decided to be baptized. Kent describes the profound honor of baptizing them and the spiritual significance of the day, reflecting on how small acts of kindness and intentional sharing can lead to sacred, life-changing connections.

- Kindness and authenticity in everyday interactions can create deep, meaningful relationships.
- Sharing personal faith respectfully and without pressure invites genuine questions and exploration.
- Community plays a crucial role in welcoming newcomers and demonstrating the 'fruit' of a faith.
- The baptism marked the Dows' entrance into a covenant with Christ and the start of their spiritual journey.
- Kent reflects that even brief acquaintances can be part of sacred moments when they act with openness and love.
