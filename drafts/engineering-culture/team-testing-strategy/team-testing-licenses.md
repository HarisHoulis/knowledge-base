# Why your team needs TestingJavaScript.com

The team's testing strategy is described as essentially "fix what the users complain about," with too many bugs slipping into production (source). They argue that manually checking for regressions before every release would halt feature development and cost the company money, so they need to automate code quality and increase deploy confidence (source). They also want everyone on the team on the same page to be as productive as possible (source).

The letter asks the boss for team licenses to TestingJavaScript.com, claiming it is the best place to learn what the team needs to ship free of bugs and regressions (source). It cites industry recommendations and says the author, Kent C. Dodds, is a highly respected community member and the author/maintainer of open source testing tools the team plans to use (source). The requested action is to go to the pricing section, click the team discounts button, and buy a license for each team member (source).

- The team's current testing approach is reactive: fixing bugs users complain about, resulting in production bugs and a need to automate code quality (source).
- Manual regression checks are seen as incompatible with continuous feature development and costly, motivating a need for deploy confidence (source).
- They request team licenses to TestingJavaScript.com so the whole team can learn together and align on quality vision (source).
- Kent C. Dodds is presented as a respected testing educator and maintainer of testing tools the team plans to use (source).
- The concrete ask: purchase a license for each team member via the TestingJavaScript.com team discounts link (source).
