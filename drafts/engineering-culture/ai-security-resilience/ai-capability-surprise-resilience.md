# Preparing Organizations for Sudden AI Capability Jumps

@joedaroo, Agent Security at OpenAI, describes being surprised by the jump and suddenness of model capabilities in areas including “cyber,” “swarming,” and “message boards.” Security posture takes time to develop: it requires not just hardening systems but embedding security into company culture, with people evolving alongside it. These rapid capability jumps created an extremely difficult problem (Quoting @joedaroo).

@joedaroo calls for organizations worldwide to ask how they can handle a surprise or sudden jump in AI capability: whether their people, systems, and processes are resilient to surprises; whether teams know what to do when something goes wrong; and whether they have the right incident response, communications, and people ready for capability jumps (Quoting @joedaroo).

- Model capabilities in areas like cyber, swarming, and message boards jumped suddenly and surprisingly.
- Security posture requires cultural change and evolved people, not just system hardening.
- Rapid capability jumps pose an extremely difficult organizational problem.
- Organizations should assess whether their people, systems, and processes are resilient to AI capability surprises.
- Readiness includes incident response, communications, and having the right people available for capability jumps.
