# The Dow's Start on the Covenant Path

The article narrates Kent C. Dodds' personal experience of befriending Jeremiah and Beiyi Dow at a family camp, which eventually led to their interest in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. After meeting again at a tech event, a conversation about faith resulted in the Dows accepting a copy of the Book of Mormon and connecting with a local congregation. The author highlights how the welcoming community and the Dows' openness to seeking answers contributed to their decision to be baptized together (source: Kent C. Dodds, https://kentcdodds.com/blog/the-dows-start-on-the-covenant-path).

The narrative details the baptism ceremony, where the author was honored to baptize the Dows and give a talk about the covenant path. The author reflects on how small acts of kindness and intentional faith-sharing can create meaningful connections, leading to unexpected and sacred experiences. The article concludes with an invitation for readers to learn more about the church without pressure, emphasizing the author's desire to help others draw closer to Christ (source: Kent C. Dodds).

- Small acts of kindness and genuine friendship can lead to profound spiritual connections.
- The Dows were attracted to the 'good fruit' of the church community and its invitation to seek personal answers.
- Baptism is described as an entrance into a covenant with Christ, not just a symbol.
- The author emphasizes the importance of intentionally sharing faith while respecting individual freedom.
- The experience reinforced the author's belief in what really matters in life.
