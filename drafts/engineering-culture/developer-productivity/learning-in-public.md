# How I am so productive

Kent C. Dodds argues that much of his apparent productivity is an illusion because a large portion of his public work—DevTips livestreams, newsletters, and open-source maintenance—is also part of his job at PayPal, where management supports knowledge sharing and uses the software he maintains (source). He also attributes his output to making as much of his existing value public as possible: turning conversations into blog posts, recording meetup talks, and answering questions in public instead of privately (source).

His advice includes automating repetitive tasks, such as using semantic-release with CI-tested npm releases, and enabling others to contribute so project maintenance is shared (source). He recommends not answering the same question twice by maintaining an active blog and AMA, which lets him answer once, help others, and share a link later (source).

On burnout, Dodds says he avoids obligations he has not committed to, does not stress over unanswered GitHub issues, and relies on short bursts of hyper-focus while acknowledging his current family/religion/coding focus may not be sustainable (source).

- Much of his visible output is part of his PayPal job and open-source work that benefits PayPal.
- Increase impact by turning existing value—conversations, talks, answers—into public artifacts.
- Automate repetitive tasks (e.g., semantic-release, CI) to release open-source software frequently.
- Enable others and answer questions once publicly to avoid repeating work.
- Avoid burnout by not taking on uncommitted obligations and using short bursts of hyper-focus.
