# The Dow's Start on the Covenant Path

Kent C. Dodds recounts how a chance meeting at family camp led to a deep friendship with Jeremiah and Beiyi Dow, and eventually to their baptism into The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The story highlights how small acts of kindness—welcoming a family, sharing faith, and helping them find community—created a meaningful connection that transcended a typical tech-conference acquaintance.

- Small acts of kindness can create meaningful connections that lead to profound outcomes.
- Intentionally sharing faith and welcoming questions can open doors for others.
- Community support and genuine friendships were key to the Dows' decision to be baptized.
- The author participated in the baptism and confirmation, calling it one of the most meaningful experiences of his life.
