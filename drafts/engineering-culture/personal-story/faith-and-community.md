# The Dow's Start on the Covenant Path

Kent C. Dodds recounts how he met his friends Jeremiah and Beiyi Dow at a family camp in August 2025, where his family welcomed them and formed a natural bond. Later, at Remix Jam, a conversation about faith led to Dodds sharing the Book of Mormon and helping them find a local LDS congregation.

- The friendship began with small acts of kindness at family camp, which the Dows later said showed them a love and generosity they weren't used to.
- A conversation at Remix Jam about faith led to the Dows accepting a Book of Mormon and finding a local congregation.
- The Dows were drawn to 'the fruit of God's work'—the welcoming community—and were invited to seek answers for themselves.
- Dodds was honored to baptize Jeremiah and Bei and confer the Holy Ghost, describing the experience as deeply moving.
- The author reflects that small acts of kindness and intentional sharing of faith can create sacred connections.
