# Nail the Narrative: Building Presentations with a Coherent Storyline

In "Nail the Narrative," Sumeet Gayathri Moghe points out that many people building presentations get tangled in constructing slides without first establishing a coherent narrative (Fowler, n.d.). This leads to slide decks that lack a clear story.

Moghe advises presenters to distill the big idea, visualize the audience, and build a structured storyline (Fowler, n.d.). These steps shift focus from slide mechanics to the underlying message and its intended audience.

- Many presentation creators get tangled in building slides without a coherent narrative.
- Sumeet Gayathri Moghe advises distilling the big idea.
- He recommends visualizing the audience.
- He recommends building a structured storyline.
