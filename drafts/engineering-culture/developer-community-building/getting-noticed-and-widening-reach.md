# Getting Noticed and Widening Your Reach

Kent C. Dodds responds to complaints that people create good things but lack a following. He argues that reach is not simply inherited: many people with large audiences worked to get noticed by creating and publicizing things, alongside hard work, luck, timing, and help from others (Kent C. Dodds, 2018).

Using geniejs and angular-formly as examples, he describes low initial popularity but using projects to improve skills, get a job, speak at meetups and conferences, and build community. For angular-formly, taking over maintenance, writing great docs, encouraging contribution, recognizing contributors, speaking, reaching relevant newsletters, and tweeting helped downloads quadruple; he advises against spamming influencers or throwing shade at alternatives (Kent C. Dodds, 2018).

Speaking and teaching also widened his reach: a recorded AngularJS Utah meetup talk led John Lindquist to invite him to egghead.io, and later he reached out to Marc Grabanski to propose a Frontend Masters workshop. His takeaway is to make value-creating experiences public and to have courage to ask, expecting rejections (Kent C. Dodds, 2018).

Podcasting had the biggest impact: Angular Air and JavaScript Air attracted guests because he could promise their time would reach thousands of developers; associating with respected people brought their followers. He also completed a full year of weekly newsletters, then republished them on his blog (Kent C. Dodds, 2018).

- Reach is often earned through a mix of hard work, luck, timing, and help, not just existing popularity.
- Open source projects can create opportunities even if they never become widely used.
- Build community with excellent documentation, contributor encouragement, recognition, and relevant outreach; don't spam influencers or disparage alternatives.
- Make value-creating experiences public: recorded talks can lead to teaching platforms and new opportunities.
- Courage to ask is important; rejections are expected, and podcasts or speaking can widen reach by promising impact to many developers.
