# Training Developers When AI Does Routine Work: The Preceptorship Model

In this podcast, Michael Stiefel spoke with Scott Hanselman about how to develop new software engineers when artificial intelligence agents are doing most of the work on which junior developers were traditionally trained (InfoQ, 2026). The conversation centers on the problem that routine work, once a core training ground for junior developers, is increasingly handled by AI.

Hanselman suggests that the software industry should adopt a preceptorship model similar to the nursing profession (InfoQ, 2026). This points to a more structured, profession-style approach to mentoring and training new engineers as AI takes over routine tasks.

- AI agents are doing most of the work on which junior developers were historically trained.
- The podcast addresses how to develop new software engineers in that environment.
- Scott Hanselman proposes a preceptorship model similar to the nursing profession.
- The discussion frames developer training as an industry-wide challenge requiring a new model.
