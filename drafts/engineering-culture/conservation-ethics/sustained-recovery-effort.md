# Kākāpō Recovery Shows Sustained Effort Works

The article quotes Andrew Digby reporting that the kākāpō population has reached 325 individuals, including juveniles from a record breeding season. This milestone is notable because in 1995 only 51 kākāpō remained, demonstrating that critically endangered species can recover with sustained effort. The quote emphasizes the importance of long-term commitment and consistent action in conservation work, offering a rare piece of positive environmental news.

- The kākāpō population has grown from 51 in 1995 to 325 today.
- This year's record breeding season added many juveniles to the population.
- The recovery shows that sustained, dedicated effort can reverse species decline.
- The news serves as a hopeful example for conservation programs globally.
