# The Pulse: New Trend of Building Internal Vibe-Coding Apps at Tech Companies

Gergely Orosz reports on an emerging trend at mid-sized tech companies: building internal platforms that let non-engineers create internal websites and tools through vibe-coding. Ramp and Stripe are cited as examples of companies that built such platforms, and both are reportedly taking off in those workplaces. Orosz expects more companies to follow suit.

- Mid-sized tech companies are building internal vibe-coding platforms for non-engineers.
- Ramp and Stripe built such platforms and they are taking off in those workplaces.
- Orosz expects more companies to adopt this approach.
