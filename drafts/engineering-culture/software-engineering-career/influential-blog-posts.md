# What blog posts influenced your thinking the most?

Simon Willison shares three blog posts that shaped his software engineering thinking (Willison, 2026). Joel Spolsky's "The Law of Leaky Abstractions" encouraged him to always seek a better understanding of the layers beneath his work, in case an abstraction leaks (Willison, 2026).

Will Larson's "Migrations: the sole scalable fix to tech debt" frames migrations—such as replacing a service or switching database engines—as a core, recurring part of software engineering. Willison says the post convinced him to invest in migration skill rather than treat migrations as special one-offs (Willison, 2026).

Charity Majors' "The Engineer/Manager Pendulum" gave Willison permission to move from engineering management back to an individual-contributor role. Majors argues that many successful developers pendulum between the two tracks multiple times, and doing so makes them better at both (Willison, 2026).

- Joel Spolsky's Law of Leaky Abstractions prompted Willison to always look for better understanding of underlying layers.
- Will Larson's writing on migrations treats migrations as a normal, recurring software engineering skill to invest in, not an exceptional one-off.
- Charity Majors' Engineer/Manager Pendulum helped Willison see switching between management and IC roles as career-enhancing rather than career-damaging.
- The post is a short collection of personally influential software engineering blog posts.
