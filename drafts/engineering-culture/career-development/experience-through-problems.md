# How to get experience as a software engineer

The article argues that technical experience comes from solving varied problems, not simply from time spent. Experience is gained by intentionally exposing yourself to different software-related problems and working to overcome them, which improves your future responses to similar situations (source).

Problems can be found at work and in open source, especially lower-level issues like bugs, flaky tests, or deployment spikes. Becoming the go-to person for a domain (e.g., accessibility, testing) attracts problems through questions, creating a cycle of learning (source).

To gain experience, dig deeply into problems, document your findings, and share them publicly (e.g., blog posts, videos). The author's involvement with angular-formly—starting from a feature request, opening an issue and PR, and eventually becoming a maintainer—illustrates how this approach builds deep expertise (source).

- Experience is not just time; it's about solving varied problems and learning from them.
- Find problems in work and open source, focusing on deep technical issues like bugs or flaky tests.
- Become the go-to person for a domain to attract questions and problems, accelerating learning.
- Dig into problems, document your findings, and share learnings publicly to reinforce experience.
