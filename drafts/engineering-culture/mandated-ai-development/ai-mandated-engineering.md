# Everything Is Written by Claude Code, and Nobody Reads Any of It

An engineer writing as "voxium" describes starting a new role at a big company half a month earlier and finding that every engineering artifact — specs, code, tests, PRDs, tickets, the resolutions of those tickets, reports — is produced by Claude Code. Nobody on the team likes this; they are being forced to ship as much as they can.

Management repeatedly asks why the team is slow if pushing code is not a bottleneck. In response, people work 12 to 13 hours a day "just to press enter," and nobody is reading anything.

The practice is described as universal rather than local to one team: literally everyone, from L1 to L7 engineers, is doing the same thing — "Talk to Claude." Simon Willison quotes the post on his blog and tags it under ai-misuse alongside llms, ai, and generative-ai.

- Every artifact — specs, code, tests, PRDs, tickets, ticket resolutions, reports — is generated by Claude Code.
- Engineers describe being forced to ship maximum output while management treats code volume as the metric and questions the team's slowness.
- People work 12–13 hour days prompting, with nobody reading the resulting output.
- The behavior spans all seniority levels, L1 through L7.
