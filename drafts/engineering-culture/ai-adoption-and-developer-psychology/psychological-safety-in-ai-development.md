# The AI Revolution Fails Without Psychological Safety for Developers

In this InfoQ podcast, Michael Stiefel speaks with Erin Doyle about how artificial intelligence tools are transforming the software development process and how they affect software engineers (InfoQ podcast with Erin Doyle). The conversation highlights that architecture skills are becoming critical as AI changes development work. Engineers need to understand how to build systems and handle the constraints and ambiguity in requirements. The podcast title frames psychological safety for developers as a prerequisite for the AI revolution to succeed.

- AI tools are transforming the software development process and affecting software engineers.
- Architecture skills are now critical for engineers working with AI-driven development.
- Engineers must be able to build systems and manage constraints and ambiguity in requirements.
- Psychological safety for developers is presented as necessary for the AI revolution to succeed.
