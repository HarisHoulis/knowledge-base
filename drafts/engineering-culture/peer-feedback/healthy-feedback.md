# Healthy Feedback

Human collaboration, like most things, improves with feedback (Fowler). The source notes that it is not obvious how to make feedback effective (Fowler).

Anuja Karnik and Sumeet Gayathri Moghe pass on a bevy of tips for healthy feedback, based on the principle that both praise and criticism should be seen as positive (Fowler).

- Feedback can improve human collaboration.
- Making feedback effective is not obvious.
- Anuja Karnik and Sumeet Gayathri Moghe provide tips for healthy feedback.
- Both praise and criticism should be seen as positive.
