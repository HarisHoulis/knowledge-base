# Beyond Relevance: A Governance-First Architecture for Enterprise Personalization

Conventional personalization systems have limitations that a governance-first architecture can address (Jerald Selvaraj, InfoQ). The article argues for separating relevance from governance so that recommendations are context-aware, auditable, and compliant. The proposed architecture integrates stateful memory, policy-driven AI orchestration, and explainable scoring to enhance personalization systems (Selvaraj, InfoQ).

- Conventional personalization systems have limitations that necessitate a governance-first approach.
- Separating relevance from governance enables context-aware, auditable, and compliant recommendations.
- The architecture combines stateful memory, policy-driven AI orchestration, and explainable scoring.
