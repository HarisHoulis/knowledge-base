Modal staff engineers Colin Weld and Connor Adams describe rebuilding the company's sandbox infrastructure from the ground up to support millions of concurrent sandboxes and tens of thousands of sandbox creations per second [1]. The InfoQ report frames this as an effort to go "beyond Kubernetes" at Modal's scale [1].
