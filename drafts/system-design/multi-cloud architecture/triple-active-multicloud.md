# How To Run on Three Clouds at Once, and When Not To

Ross McFarlane and Kevin Holditch present Form3's evolution from a single-cloud setup to a triple active multi-cloud architecture (InfoQ). The talk outlines engineering strategies for cross-cloud networking, distributed databases built with CockroachDB and NATS, and custom Kubernetes operators (InfoQ).

A further focus is navigating distinct regional disaster recovery expectations across the UK, Europe, and US financial markets (InfoQ). The presentation title also frames the discussion around when not to run on three clouds, though the abstract does not detail those cases (InfoQ).

- Form3 moved from a single-cloud setup to a triple active multi-cloud architecture.
- Cross-cloud networking was a key engineering strategy.
- Distributed databases used CockroachDB and NATS.
- Custom Kubernetes operators were part of the approach.
- Disaster recovery expectations differ across UK, Europe, and US financial markets.
