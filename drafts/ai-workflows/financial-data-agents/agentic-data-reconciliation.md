# AI Agents Cut 3-Day Portfolio Builds to Minutes — Jack McDonald, Apollo & Michael Manapat, Rowspace

Michael Manapat, co-founder of Rowspace, describes an agent-based platform that uses AI to help financial partners like Apollo work with chaotic and fragmented data. The agents perform data engineering, extract information, and reconcile data across different systems so portfolio managers and traders can make faster, more accurate decisions. Jack McDonald, COO of Redding Ridge Capital (Apollo's structured credit and CLO manager, managing roughly $50 billion), explains why this matters: his unit receives thousands of data points daily across about a thousand loan pools, and CLO management is an active process in the syndicated loan market where having accurate, consistent data at hand is critical.

The core problem is that this information is private, fragmented, and spread across many documents rather than being widely represented in public markets. Apollo partnered with Rowspace to embed the vendor's team, teach them the calculations and primary sources of truth, and refine the AI engines to reproduce that logic. McDonald gives two concrete examples of impact. First, portfolio formation: CLOs have specific terms, rules, and cash-flow waterfalls buried in deep legal documents, and building a test portfolio of 200–400 credit instruments in Excel used to take two to three days of manual work; now the most up-to-date information can be pulled in real time and a portfolio built in minutes, letting managers generate 80–90% of the foundation and apply judgment to the last 10%.

Second, warehouse purchasing activities: the business runs on bank financing and can buy loans in the fixed income market at any time, but weekly reconciliation of transactions and trading activity was entirely manual, with staff going into different bank accounts to find payments and a separate team summarizing data and emailing reports to management. That same report can now be uploaded to Rowspace for visualization. The through-line is that AI moves from a nice addition to business-critical when it is grounded in the firm's own sources of truth and reproduces its calculations.

- Rowspace uses AI agents to do data engineering, extraction, and reconciliation across fragmented financial data systems for partners like Apollo.
- CLO portfolio formation that took 2–3 days of manual Excel work for 200–400 instruments can now be done in minutes, with managers refining the last 10% using judgment.
- Weekly manual reconciliation of warehouse purchasing and trading activity across bank accounts is replaced by uploading reports to the platform for visualization.
- Success required embedding the vendor team to learn the firm's calculations and primary sources of truth, then refining AI engines to reproduce them.
