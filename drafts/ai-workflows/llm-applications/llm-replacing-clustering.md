# Pinterest Replaces Journey Clustering with a Fine-Tuned 4B Qwen3 LLM

Pinterest replaced its multi-stage user-journey clustering pipeline with a fine-tuned 4-billion-parameter Qwen3 language model. The change reportedly improved email click-through by 1.1% and push open rates by 1.3%.

- Pinterest migrated user-journey inference from a multi-stage clustering pipeline to a fine-tuned 4B Qwen3 LLM.
- The LLM-based approach yielded a 1.1% lift in email click-through.
- Push notification open rates rose 1.3%.
