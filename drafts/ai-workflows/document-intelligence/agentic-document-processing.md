# From Ingestion to Agents: Building AI Teams on Document Intelligence

Reducto co-founder and CEO Adit Abraham frames document intelligence as the unglamorous but decisive layer for agents that work in the real world. Reducto is positioned as an intelligent document processing platform serving leading AI teams — Harvey, Legora, and similar companies — as well as large technology companies, global financial institutions, and insurers holding years of accumulated data that has been "very, very difficult to use outside of a demonstration context" (AI Engineer, Reducto). Across those customers the company reports processing many billions of documents.

The talk traces a shift from RAG-style information synthesis to agents that do work. A few years ago every conversation was about "rag," and programs were essentially search or question-answering products over context extracted from a file or user upload. Today the buzzword is agents: autonomous decision-making and complex work products, including generating and modifying files, not just answering questions about them — a change the speaker says makes the underlying pipeline problems more important, not less (AI Engineer, Reducto).

That importance comes from compounding error. In a single question-answering session the risk is contained, but when agents make multiple decisions across a multi-stage pipeline fed by multiple sources, incorrect input becomes "truly prevalent" in the pipeline. Abraham argues language-model value in the real world is only realized relative to the context it operates on, and for many businesses that context is unstructured, dispersed, and multimodal — with PDFs singled out as a particular bottleneck despite the volume of PDF-processing launches on social media.

On tooling, the talk claims there is a right place and time for traditional computer vision versus VLMs, and touches on evaluation as teams move from RAG-based to agent-based product creation. - Reducto is an intelligent document processing platform serving AI companies (e.g. Harvey, Legora) and large enterprises such as financial institutions and insurers with historical, hard-to-use data; the company reports processing many billions of documents.
- The thematic shift is from RAG-era information synthesis and search/Q&A products to agents that make autonomous decisions and produce or modify work products, which changes the tools and challenges involved.
- Input errors compound in multi-stage agent pipelines: with multiple decisions across multiple data sources, incorrect input becomes prevalent, making data quality more critical than in single-turn Q&A.
- There is claimed to be a right place and time for traditional computer vision versus VLMs, and evaluation practices must change when moving from RAG to agent-based products.
- Business data is characterized as unstructured, dispersed, and multimodal, with PDFs a particular bottleneck despite the number of PDF-processing tools launched.
