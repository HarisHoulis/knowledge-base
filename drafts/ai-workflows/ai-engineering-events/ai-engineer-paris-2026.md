# AI Engineer Paris 2026 Opening Keynotes: Mistral, Langfuse & Sizzy (Day 1)

AI Engineer Paris 2026 opened for its second edition at STATION F in Paris on September 23, 2026, gathering 1,000+ engineers, founders, CTOs, and VPs of AI for two days of technical talks, practical workshops, and AI engineering research. The event is organized around the themes of AI infrastructure, coding agents, software factories, and building frontier AI (AI Engineer, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CGq9KRSb9Kc, 2026-09-23).

The Day 1 main stage lineup pairs conference framing talks with vendor and practitioner perspectives: Jen Person (Mistral) with "Welcome to AI Engineer Paris 2026," Lia McBride (AI Engineer) with "Intro to AI Engineer," and Lara Khanafer (STATION F) with a welcome from the venue. Technical sessions include Clemens Rawert (Langfuse) on "What Can Economic History Teach Us About the AI Moment?" and Kitze (Sizzy) on "The Next Level of AI Engineering."

The day closes with Lélio Renard Lavaud (Mistral) presenting "Building Frontier AI." The livestream covers the Main Stage only; additional sessions and activities run throughout the event, and the schedule is subject to change.
