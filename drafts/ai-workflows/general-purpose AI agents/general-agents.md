# Claude Cowork and chat merge into a single general agent

Anthropic announced that Claude Cowork and Claude chat are merging into one Claude, starting immediately (Simon Willison, 2026-09-16). The pitch is that a user can "bring a quick question, or hand over a report due at noon, and Claude takes it from there, even after you've closed your laptop" — meaning tasks continue running after the client is closed (claude.com, 2026-09-16).

The rollout targets Pro and Max plans first, reaching existing and new users of those plans in the Claude app on web, desktop, and mobile over the coming weeks (claude.com, 2026-09-16).

Willison reads this as Claude becoming a general agent in its own right, and notes it echoes OpenAI renaming their Codex desktop app to ChatGPT a few weeks earlier (Simon Willison, 2026-09-16).

The merger also removed planned work for the author, who had intended to write a follow-up mapping the boundaries between Cowork and regular Claude; he still expects that determining what the change means for features and surfaces will take considerable effort (Simon Willison, 2026-09-16).
