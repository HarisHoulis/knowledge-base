# The Highest Loop: Nested Cycles as a Core AI Engineering Skill

In this talk, swyx frames AI engineering as the skill of identifying and manipulating nested cycles, or loops. He traces a progression from tokens to chat to tool use to goal setting to automation, noting that today's fascination with cron jobs, cycles, and software factories reflects overlapping loops. He references his essay "The Mastery of Cycles" and argues that a core skill of an AI engineer is to identify what cycle you're working in, determine whether it's the right level of abstraction, and level up to a new level of performance and output scaling when ready. When problems arise, engineers should go down to the cycle below and dig deeper to ensure reliability and fix bugs. He simplifies the analogy to a stack of nested loops that all programmers understand.

Swyx draws a human analogy for these cycles: a heartbeat in the womb as proof of life, learning to speak, learning to use tools, starting to work ("eat, sleep, code, repeat or prompt"), working in teams and small groups, and finally the broader human project of collective intelligence and civilization. He suggests that what we do with code translates into human life, and that agents working in one area are becoming universal, with this year adding verticals like AI in healthcare, finance, and go-to-market strategies, alongside advanced engineering.

He also discusses the AI Engineer conference series, noting growth from the first World's Fair in 2023 through about 2,000 attendees in 2024, around 6,000 in 2025, and 7,000 today, exceeding the 6,000 target. He mentions summits in New York, Paris, Singapore, Melbourne, and Miami, organized with partners and 16 partnerships, and encourages people to organize events in their countries. He calls the highest cycle the one where people come together to understand what the next cycle will be—a loop that creates loops—and the World's Fair as the summit of summits. He closes by encouraging attendees to find their generative looping process and extend it to their teams, workflows, and life.

- AI engineering's core skill is identifying which nested cycle you're working in and whether it's the right level of abstraction.
- Level up to a new cycle to scale performance and output; go down a cycle to debug and ensure reliability.
- Human life mirrors these cycles: heartbeat, speech, tool use, work, teams, and collective intelligence.
- The highest loop is people coming together to understand what the next cycle will be—a loop that creates loops.
