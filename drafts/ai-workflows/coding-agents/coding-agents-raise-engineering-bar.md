# Coding Agents Make Software Engineering Harder

In a short note dated 24 September 2026, Simon Willison argues that the more time he spends working with coding agents, the more convinced he becomes that they make software engineering even harder (https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/24/harder/).

The note does not dispute the capability of these tools: Willison states that amazing things can be done with coding agents. The difficulty lies in unlocking their full potential, which he says requires extraordinary discipline and knowledge from the engineer (https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/24/harder/).

The post is tagged with coding-agents, ai, and llms, placing it in the context of ongoing discussion about LLM-based development tooling.

- Coding agents increase the difficulty of software engineering rather than simplifying it.
- They enable impressive results, but realizing their full potential demands extraordinary discipline and knowledge.
- The claim is framed as a personal conclusion drawn from extended hands-on work with agentic coding tools.
