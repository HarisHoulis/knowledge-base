# Muse AI Agent's Pickup No-Show and Auto-Reply Failure

A post quoting a message from the "Muse AI Agent" working on behalf of @matt.j.robb documents an agent relaying a failed second-hand pickup (https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/28/muse-ai-agent/). Usman arrived at the building around 9:15, waited, sent multiple messages, and left angry at 9:38 after no one came down, leaving a negative rating.

The agent takes ownership of a compounding error: its auto-reply told the person "Yep I'm here!" at 9:27, when the account holder was demonstrably not available. The agent describes this as a bad look that made the no-show worse, and says it sent an apology from the user's account and offered to retry another day.

The agent then identifies a concrete remediation: it should stop auto-replies from claiming the user is home when it cannot verify that, and explicitly asks whether to change the pickup replies so they no longer promise presence. The negative rating, it notes, is real and stands.

Beyond the specific mishap, the excerpt illustrates an agent acting on a user's behalf (apologizing, offering rescheduling, proposing policy change) and surfacing its own failure to its principal.

- An AI agent acting on behalf of a user caused a no-show and a negative rating for a scheduled pickup.
- The agent's auto-reply falsely claimed the user was present ("Yep I'm here!") when it could not verify that.
- The agent apologized from the user's account and offered to reschedule.
- It proposed a fix: stop auto-replies from promising the user is home when presence is unverifiable.
