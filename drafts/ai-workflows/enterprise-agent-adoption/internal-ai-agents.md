# From Consumers to Builders: Turning 200 of our Team into Agent Creators in 2 Weeks

Ben Maraney's InfoQ presentation describes how Forter demystified AI agent creation for technical and non-technical staff. The goal was to move 200 team members from consumers to builders of agents in two weeks, primarily across R&D (InfoQ).

The talk covers leveraging custom MCP servers, combining no-code and code-based platforms, sidestepping complex RAG setups, and aligning security and legal teams to accelerate internal agent adoption (InfoQ).

- Forter turned 200 technical and non-technical team members into agent creators in two weeks.
- The approach used custom MCP servers and combined no-code with code-based platforms.
- The team deliberately avoided complex RAG setups.
- Security and legal alignment helped accelerate internal agent adoption across R&D.
